# E051 Reservoir creation (National Inventory of Dams) acting on wetland birds (pre-registered 2026-10-11 18:50,
# before downloading or processing)
hypotheses: [T5, M26]     parents: [E049c, E050]     eval_version: v1     cost: small download + ~15 min cpu
why: the user asked for more covariates (2026-10-11: "There should be other useful covariates to test"). Reservoirs
built between the epochs create open water and shoreline habitat. NID records each dam's completion year and
surface area, so it is a long-spanning, locally precise record. DESK's inputs carry none of it: LUH has no open
water class, and climate does not see it.
data: USACE NID, nation CSV (public API). Checksum beside the file.
covariate: per 27 km cell, cumulative normal surface area of dams completed by year y, as a fraction of cell area,
plus the dam count. Change between the standard epochs (1966-86 -> 2005-25) = reservoirs completed in between.
test: guild_dose.py --pooled --rival-all --aux <nid npz>, both T0 caches. Gain over ALL covariate change, 8 moved-field
nulls, within type and kernel-local.
rule: "RESERVOIRS ACT WHERE THEY SHOULD": WETLAND-guild gain > max null with net CI > 0 at both T0s (either mode).
Forest and grassland gains ~0. Otherwise report sizes.
caveats:
- Open-water birds (gulls, cormorants, waterfowl) may be split between AVONET "Wetland" and other classes.
- Many dams are small farm ponds.
- Area attributes are incomplete for some dams.
DATA-SOURCE AMENDMENT (2026-10-11 18:55, before any processing): the nationwide CSV export
(/api/nation/csv) answered "No export currently available" on three tries over ~8 min. So the dams are taken from
the NID API's /query endpoint, state by state (addProps=all), for the 48 CONUS states. JSON files and checksums are
under /mnt/e/Datasets/houfin/external/nid/states/.
Fields used:
- yearCompleted: missing for ~30% in Kansas. Those dams are left out because their timing is unknown.
- surfaceArea (acres): ~47% reported. Imputed from normalStorage by a log-log fit where missing.
- normalStorage (acre-feet).
Covariates (research/exp/nid_covariates.py): cumulative reservoir surface fraction, log1p cumulative storage per
km^2, and dam count, by completion year. The test and rule are unchanged.

WHY (likely, untested):
