why: the decisive version of the user's covariate question. Every land-use input so far is modelled or allocated (LUH,
HYDE, CropAT below the county), and local change in those carries little (E044d: land use 0.01-0.03 within type and
region). Annual NLCD (USGS, Landsat-based, 30 m, annual 1985-2025, public domain) OBSERVES land-cover change.
- If observed change predicts local bird change for the guilds it should, beyond everything DESK sees: input quality
  is a lever, and the response-transfer strategy (learn responses where change is observed, apply them
  historically) is supported.
- If even observed change does not: local land-cover change at 27 km explains little of local bird change, and the
  bottleneck is elsewhere (lags, dynamics, observers, non-land drivers).
design:
- EPOCHS inside the NLCD era: early 1986-1995, modern 2014-2023. Both are trained years for both caches; this is a
  cross-sectional diagnostic, not a backcast.
- Per cell: rows in each window, ABBA halves by alternating years.
- Land cover: Annual NLCD 1990 and 2019 (mid-epoch), aggregated to the 27 km grid as class fractions of cell area:
  - open water;
  - developed (open, low, medium, high);
  - barren;
  - deciduous, evergreen and mixed forest;
  - shrub;
  - grassland;
  - pasture/hay;
  - cultivated crops;
  - woody and emergent wetland.
  Change = 2019 - 1990.
- Rival: ALL DESK covariate change over the same epochs (F_cov_raw).
- guild_dose.py --pooled --rival-all with custom epochs. Gain = (all + NLCD) - all, 8 moved-NLCD nulls, within type
  and kernel-local, guild-pooled.
predictions (guild <- the classes that should drive it):
- forest <- forest classes;
- grassland <- grassland, pasture/hay, crops;
- wetland <- water and wetlands;
- human modified <- developed;
- shrubland <- shrub.
rule: OBSERVED LAND COVER ACTS WHERE IT SHOULD if, kernel-local or within type:
- forest and grassland each gain > max null with net CI > 0;
- and at least 3 of the 5 guilds show gains beating >= 7 of 8 nulls.
Otherwise report sizes. The size relative to E049c's census crop-type gain (+0.015-0.02) is reported either way.
caveats:
- 1990 and 2019 land cover stand in for epoch means.
- NLCD class definitions are partly modelled (e.g. pasture vs grassland).
- The guilds are small.
- Runs on desk_tempho_1995_s1; the epochs make T0 irrelevant apart from the type basis.
