# E064 — Habitat-specific sub-cell compensation

Preregistered: 2026-10-19, before fitting any E064 arm.

## Question

E017/E058 show that below-grid heterogeneity can remove the plains capacity trough and much of
the Allee-dead area, but a single continent-wide urban tilt is too restrictive. E059 shows that
bounded persistent route heterogeneity also absorbs substantial residual structure. Which part
of the apparent gain belongs to a biological within-cell mechanism, which part belongs to the
observation process, and does urbanization help specifically where a limiting resource is scarce?

Two provisional compensation hypotheses are tested:

1. Urban structure adds nesting/breeding capacity most strongly in cells with little built
   infrastructure (a deliberately crude proxy for a breeding-site deficit).
2. Urban structure/feeders increase overwinter survival most strongly in harsh-winter cells.
   Cold and snow are tested separately before their combined index so a combined result cannot
   hide which input is doing the work.

These are mechanism probes, not proposed final-model covariates. The within-cell BUI strata and
their area weights remain mean-preserving. A moderator changes the strength of the within-cell
tilt by cell and year; it does not directly add birds or change the cell-mean DESK field.

## Design

All arms use the E055a pinned-Allee/Veit-dispersal baseline and the same `quick90` 400-step
continuation MAP used by E050--E063, followed by float64 L-BFGS refinement. They smooth the
sub-cell allocation positive part at 0.001 (the E058b/c hard-threshold gradient failure). Route
arms pin route-effect SD at 0.7; it cannot become the observation-model off switch seen when that
SD was free.

| Arm | Process heterogeneity | Route effect |
|---|---|---|
| 64a | generic capacity + fecundity | no |
| 64b | generic capacity + fecundity | pinned 0.7 |
| 64c | generic capacity only | no |
| 64d | generic survival only | no |
| 64e | capacity + low-built-environment interaction | no |
| 64f | survival + cold interaction | no |
| 64g | survival + snow interaction | no |
| 64h | survival + cold-and-snow interaction | no |
| 64i | capacity-deficit + harsh-winter survival interactions | no |
| 64j | both interactions | pinned 0.7 |
| 64n | full 64j structure on synthetic null data | pinned 0.7 |
| 64r | full 64j structure on synthetic nonzero interaction data | pinned 0.7 |

The capacity moderator is `positive_part(-builtup_mean)`, where `builtup_mean` is the
area-weighted mean standardized log BUI. Winter moderators use standardized `-tmin_q10`,
`snow_q50`, or their equal-weight sum. Each positive tail is RMS-normalized over all land-cell
years. Thus the interaction coefficient is the extra within-cell urban tilt in a typical
targeted cell and is exactly zero outside the target tail.

## Predictions and falsification

- If sub-cell biology is genuine rather than a proxy for route heterogeneity, 64b/64j should
  retain nonzero, stable process effects after route effects enter, with plausible demographic
  intercepts. A process coefficient collapsing to zero only in route arms counts against it.
- Capacity compensation predicts `subcell_beta_k_mod > 0`; winter compensation predicts
  `subcell_sigma_s_mod > 0`. Negative values or boundary-scale values without a spatially
  coherent improvement falsify the named directional mechanism.
- A harsh-winter result is credible only if 64f/64g identify its source or 64h clearly improves
  on both without sign reversal. Large opposing cold/snow behavior is a warning that the index
  is exploiting covariance rather than winter severity.
- 64n must return both moderator coefficients near zero. 64r must recover their signs and rough
  magnitudes without manufacturing a large omitted coefficient. Failure is an identifiability
  warning and blocks interpreting real-data moderator estimates.
- U is compared only within the same likelihood family (route versus route, no-route versus
  no-route). Better U does not validate a mechanism. Primary readouts are coefficient stability,
  plains capacity, Allee-dead area, K trend, demographic plausibility, and spatial residuals.

## Western-sink audit

“Western sink” is not a scalar failure criterion. For every arm, report separately:

- fundamentally unsuitable western cells (Allee-free lambda <= 1);
- fundamentally suitable but Allee-dead western cells;
- viable cells;
- total area and largest connected components with centroids/bounds;
- overlap with the historical native seed, early positive BBS cells (1966–1979), and modern
  positive BBS cells (2010–2025), both record- and count-weighted.

A compact sink confined to ecologically credible terrain is not a contortion. A broad connected
sink covering the native core or much of the positive BBS support is. The audit is generated by
`research/age_model/exp/west_sink_audit.py` from each diagnostic suite.

## Stop/kill criteria

Do not promote any E064 structure if it requires adult and juvenile survival at the same bound,
fecundity outside the biological envelope, a renewed continent-scale capacity trend, or a broad
western sink overlapping the native/observed core. Do not promote habitat-specific modifiers if
the synthetic guards fail. This round is complete when all arms have cold MAP, float64 refine,
standard diagnostics, and the spatial sink audit; its outcome is a narrower next hypothesis, not
a production model.

## Execution amendments

- 2026-10-19: the first 64b execution is invalid. `obs_route_sd` is sampled in the
  likelihood, outside the conditioned `sample_priors` block, so the nominal
  `pinned_sites: {obs_route_sd: 0.7}` did not pin it. Its MAP and refinement fitted the
  scale; the refinement then stalled at U=176,272.69 with gradient norm 123,441. The
  code now makes a pinned likelihood-level route scale a non-optimizable deterministic,
  with a full-model regression test. A cold `fullfresh` 64b repair was placed ahead of
  the remaining queue; its invalid outputs and checkpoints are archived. This plumbing
  bug also makes earlier claims that E057/E059 route scales were *pinned* conditional on
  rerunning them under the fix, although their evidence that persistent route structure
  matters remains a hypothesis worth retesting.
- 2026-10-19 post-discovery controls: checkpoint audit found that the nominally pinned
  route scale actually fitted to 1.288 in E059a, 8.731 in E059b, and 1.02--2.52 in the
  E057 route-panel arms. The E059 conclusions are therefore withdrawn, not merely
  caveated. Added 64k (E059a route-only control, SD truly fixed 0.7) and 64l (the same
  with the loose K-trend prior) as corrected cold reruns. These were added after seeing
  the plumbing failure, before fitting either corrected control; they test whether the
  reported removal of capacity collapse and temporal pressure survives a genuinely
  bounded route effect.

## Interim results (2026-10-20)

Only 64c, 64e, and 64r currently pass numerical validation. The apparent completions
of 64b/d/f/g/i/n are invalid optimizer stops; 64h/j failed during MAP, and the 64a
repair failed before refinement because its reusable MAP was still sent through the
resume fingerprint check. The runner now treats high-gradient plateaus and non-finite
refinements as failures, and the explicit 64a reuse path verifies and skips its finished
MAP.

Capacity-only 64c reverses the proposed sign: urban strata hold *less* capacity. In
64e the generic effect and deficit-targeted moderator both collapse to essentially
zero, improving U by only 28 nats while leaving the same demographic and K-trend
contortions. Thus these valid fits reject the proposed breeding-site compensation in
its current form.

The western sinks in 64c/e are not compact ecological patches. Their largest connected
fundamental-sink components cover 2.22/2.23 million km2, span roughly 129--101 W and
23--55 N, contain 407--417 positive BBS cells, and classify 53.6--54.0% of the western
native seed as fundamentally unsuitable. A further 16.0--19.9% of the native seed is
Allee-dead. On location and extent, these are model contortions.

Synthetic recovery 64r is excellent, but null 64n became non-finite. The preregistered
identifiability gate therefore fails: recovery shows that a strong supplied signal can
be found, but does not establish null calibration or license interpreting real-data
survival moderators.

The survival-bearing E064 arms unintentionally combine feeding/shelter physiology with
perfect survival-informed ideal-free sorting inside each cell. That implicit root is
the source of the numerical failures. E065 preregisters the physiological mechanism
without survival-informed sorting; failed E064 outputs remain archived evidence and
will not be relabeled as biological results.
