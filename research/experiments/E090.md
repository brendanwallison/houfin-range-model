# E090 Do county agricultural PRACTICE and land-use TYPE (census 1978/82 vs 2002-12) predict open-country birds' change beyond everything DESK sees?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any scoring; the census cell table was built and its national sums read, no bird data)
hypotheses: [covariate content]     parents: [E049c, E050, E052, E080]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: CPU, ~15 min
why: the literature for 1980-2003 finds farmland / grassland bird trends track agricultural PRACTICE and pasture-type
land use better than cropland amount: insecticide acute toxicity then cropland-pasture loss (Mineau & Whiteside 2013),
or CRP and pasture area (Hill et al. 2014); Murphy 2003: ~25-30% of among-state trend variation. Our own net crop amount
carries nothing; census crop TYPES (CropAT) gave grassland birds +0.015-0.018 (E049c). The user asked to try county
census by type (2026-10-17). DESK's inputs have LUH management (fertilizer, irrigation) but no pesticides, pasture vs
hay, CRP, or cattle.
data: research/exp/census_cells.py -> /mnt/e/Datasets/ICPSR_agriculture/census_27km.npz (ICPSR 35206; columns from the
codebook inventory of 2026-10-17). Early = mean of the 1978 and 1982 censuses; modern = mean of 2002, 2007, 2012.
13 channels as shares of county land area (cattle as density): cropland excl. cropland pasture, harvested cropland,
all pasture, farm woodland, hay, row crops (corn, soy, sorghum, cotton), small grains (wheat, oats, barley), acres
treated for insects, for weeds, fertilized, irrigated, cattle, CRP (0 early: the program began in 1986). National sums
sane (cropland 446 -> 390 M acres, insecticide-treated 76 -> 101, herbicide 165 -> 285, CRP ~30 M). 10,910 cells.
design: research/exp/guild_dose.py --pooled --rival-all (as E049c / E091: gain = (all covariate change DESK sees +
census change) - all, guild-pooled ridge, held-out cells, 8 moved-field nulls), --aux-mask-key census_mask. Three arms:
census (13), census_land (10), census_chem (insect, herbicide, fert). Caches desk_tempho_1995_s1, desk_tempho_1985_s1.
Strata: SEDENTARY (AVONET Migration 1) reported first; all species. Guilds: Grassland (primary), Shrubland, Wetland,
Human Modified (open / farmland); Forest = NEGATIVE CONTROL.
reading (pre-registered; "type" mode primary, "type+kloc" reported):
- CENSUS ACTS ON GRASSLAND BIRDS if the census (13) gain for Grassland is > 0 with CI > 0 AND above >= 7 of 8 moved
  nulls at BOTH start years, in the stratum where Grassland has >= 8 resolvable species (sedentary if it does, else all
  species, said so), with the Forest control's gain not above its nulls.

  the full set does (then: not attributable).
- Reported regardless: every guild x stratum x arm x mode, against E049c's CropAT gains.

values are spread evenly over each county; CRP early = 0 is true, not missing.
