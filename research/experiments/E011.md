REVISED READING (UNVERIFIED): for real dev species the "change-fitted" captured share (0.25-0.26) is matched or
beaten by position alone (0.30) and by the static community (0.32-0.33); the change features add +0.02 (DESK) /

is WORSE than no change into withheld decades (-0.04/-0.05, both feature sets, both splits); for planted z species
it is near-perfect, so this is a property of real species, not of the instrument; (2) species change is spatially
structured (~30% of available change interpolates from neighbours). D0 as stated is uninformative (neither Delta z
carries much beyond location); B7's "2-3x more with a change-fitted beta" is withdrawn; B7's core (space-for-time
fails at the readout) stands, and E012's split-kernel gain is best read as the model learning to SHRINK the
temporal deviation (learned dz/zbar amplitude 0.09) instead of being forced to apply spatial betas to it.
