# E072 Honest uncertainty for predicted habitat change: representation-shaped and scaled by novelty
hypotheses: [B2, S1, T9, G11]     parents: [E073, E018, E074, E069a2]     eval_version: pre-v1
cost: CPU (cpu2), cached arrays + yearly states; ~1-2 h per cache. Script: research/exp/e072_honest.py.
pre-registered: 2026-10-15, before any E072 number. A smoke run on desk_tempho_1995_s1 was launched minutes before

## Why
The user (2026-10-14):
- "is there a way to inject uncertainty of the appropriate magnitude? ... excessive dissimilarity is less harmful than
  excessive similarity";
- on B, "doesn't let DESK learn anything temporal";
- "2 is appealing" (option 2: errors-in-variables on the representation's own change, shaped by it).
E073 supplied the mechanism and the map:
- over-movement is estimation variance (B4);
- land-cover change beyond the BBS-era range defeats every representation (C3/C3b);
- 1920-39 cover is novel in direction and size, above all on the Great Plains (part A).
E018: the readout's posterior intervals are ~100x too small in variance. E074: flexible readouts over-move at long reach
on CBC.
Considerations: G1, G2 (over-similarity is worse), G4, G11, G12, T9.

## Design
Representations (single-slope within readouts, the production-like form):
- delta: the clock-free community-learned covariate delta (E073; W = climate, physical cover and human within-PCs at
  h50; B = ridge to ESK64 within deviations). Read out by ML (delta) and with prior x0.1 (delta_shr).
- desk: DESK's raw z within deviation.
Designs:
- full: training cells x trained years;
- cover: minus training cells beyond the p70 cover-trend range (E073 C3). Held-out cells beyond the range are then
  novel in cover.
Uncertainty terms per held-out cell x species:
- V: the readout posterior (zero for delta_shr);
- U, representation-shaped errors-in-variables (option 2):
  - delta: the variance of the predicted change over 30 block-bootstrap refits of the community map B. This grows with
    novelty of the covariate change automatically (dW' Var(B) dW);
  - desk: the variance over DESK seeds (T0 1996: s1-s4; T0 1986: s1-s4), each with its own readout;
- nov_g: the novelty of the cell's covariate change per group (climate, cover, human) against the covariance of IN-ERA
  changes at training cells.
Variance models (coefficients >= 0, NNLS on cell means, in units of the species' noise-free spatial variance S):
- M0 posterior only;
- M1 const;
- M2 const + cover novelty;
- M3 const + all novelty;
- M4 const + gU*U;
- M5 const + U + cover;
- M6 U only.
Evaluation: R = sum e2 / sum predicted variance (noise-free e2 = (Ta-P)(Tb-P); R > 1 = overconfident):
- (i) cross-fitted over species halves, by cover-novelty tercile and beyond / inside;
- (ii) fit on INSIDE cells, evaluate on BEYOND cells: the extrapolation test, closest to 1920;
- the fitted laws are applied to 1920-39 vs 2005-25 at every cache cell, by zone: predicted change-error variance (in
  S units) and the implied corr(H1920, H2000) = 1 - var / 2.
Caches: desk_tempho_1995_s1 (seed caches s2-s4) and desk_tempho_1985_s1 (s2-s4).

## Predictions (written before any number)
- M0 R >> 10 everywhere (E018).
- M1 calibrated overall (cross-fit R 0.8-1.25) but OVERCONFIDENT beyond the cover range in the cover design
  (inside->beyond R_beyond >= 1.5).
- U grows beyond the range (delta), so M4 / M5 cut R_beyond toward 1. The bootstrap underestimates the error, so gU
  is fitted well above 1 (3-30).
- Cover novelty alone (M2) helps less than U when delta is the representation; for desk (seed spread), U helps less.
- The 1920 projection: predicted error variance >= 2x the BBS-era held-out value, largest on the Great Plains.

## Decision rules
- HONEST LAW = the simplest model with inside->beyond R_beyond in [0.8, 1.5] AND cross-fit R_all in [0.8, 1.25] for
  sedentary species in the COVER design at both T0s. Ties go to the more conservative (higher predicted variance)
  model, per G2.
- OPTION 2 SUFFICIENT if M4 or M6 qualifies (the representation's own uncertainty carries the novelty); otherwise
  explicit novelty terms are needed (M2 / M3 / M5).
- If no model qualifies: report the best and the residual overconfidence; the 1920 projection is then a lower bound.
- Production implications (reported, not decided):
  - the age model's habitat kernel would add a term: errors-in-variables on delta, i.e. the bootstrap covariance of
    the community map, scaled by gU (option 2), with a novelty term if required;
  - the 1920 numbers say how much dissimilarity that injects, by zone.

---- AMENDMENT (2026-10-15, after the development smoke run, before any registered job) ----
The smoke run (desk_tempho_1995_s1, 10 map bootstraps; development only, not a result) exposed two design flaws:
- (1) Fitting the variance law on per-species ratios e2 / S lets rare, small-S species dominate. Calibration is pooled
  (sum e2 / sum var), so every fitted law over-predicted 4-7x (R 0.13-0.28). Fixed: the law is fitted on RATIOS OF
  SUMS over species per cell (sum_s (e2 - V) / sum_s S), and summaries are pooled the same way.
- (2) Novelty is heavy-tailed (beyond the cover range, cover and human novelty reach ~300-400x the in-era reference), so
  a linear term is dominated by a few cells. Fixed: novelty enters as log(1 + nov).
Everything else, including the decision rules, is unchanged.

WHY (likely):
- Most change error is unpredictable at any distance: a constant ~0.3-0.4 S per ~30-40 y of change. Predictable
  change is the small part, so losing it beyond the range (E073 C's corr drop) adds little to total error.
- The honest uncertainty is a large constant plus a representation-shaped term that grows with novelty. The bootstrap
  of the covariate map supplies the growth (it is extrapolation-aware by construction); DESK's seed spread does not.
PRODUCTION IMPLICATIONS (reported, not decided):
- (i) The age model's habitat-change uncertainty should be of order corr(H1920, H2000) ~ 0.7 relative to the spatial

- (ii) In kernel terms: a constant within-place change term, ~0.3 S per ~35 y and growing with span, plus
  gU x the bootstrap covariance of the covariate delta map, which supplies the novelty growth.
- (iii) G2 asymmetry: take the upper of the M3 / M4 projections (0.6 S, corr ~0.7), with GP and the east at the top.
