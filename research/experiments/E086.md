WHY (likely, close to established by construction): the private dims are point-wise functions of the SAME covariates
as z (the mixer's hidden layer), so at prediction time they hold no information the covariates lack; CBC / eBird can
only change WHICH covariate functions DESK learns. The sum readout's gain is therefore reweighting (E085's ceiling),
whether the head reads z alone or z plus private dims. The orthogonality penalty forces the private block onto
covariate directions z does not use, and pulling the shared encoder there costs breeding change (likely; p8o's k drop).
WHY a 24-dim compression loses the gain (likely): the stack's leading uncentred components are the large spatial
directions (98.6% of energy in 24), while the readout's small change gain lives in low-energy directions -- the same
reason ESK's leading dims predict change poorly (interim predictability result).
Production implication (not a decision): no case for private per-source blocks or an orthogonal joint basis; the
sum-of-Ruzickas readout is a consistent ~+0.005-0.01 on change at no training cost, and if a single basis is wanted,
z at rank 32 captures most of it; a variance-ordered joint basis at 24 does not.

residents actually live in. Small samples (9-10 species per tercile); a pre-registered test would be needed.
---- DIAGNOSTIC (2026-10-17, not pre-registered; the user asked whether the readout is a genuine sum of Ruzickas) ----
Formally: K = a_B z.z' + sum_h a_h (H_h z).(H_h z') = z' M z with M = a_B P24 + sum_h a_h H_h'H_h -- an exact SUM of PSD
kernels, each a learned rank-24 linear stand-in for one source's Ruzicka (BBS via ESK r24 predicted by DESK; CBC 59
residents; eBird 91 species), collapsing to ONE linear kernel on z with a learned metric. A literal Ruzicka cannot be
computed where no survey exists, so fidelity is the question. Held-out cells, 40k random pairs of a source's surveyed
cell-years, (H z_i).(H z_j) vs Ruzicka(log1p X_i, log1p X_j):

READING: the heads are good stand-ins for their Ruzickas at new places (corr ~0.9, R2 0.79-0.88 on the z they were
