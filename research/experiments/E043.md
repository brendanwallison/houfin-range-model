# E043 Temporal supervision: over-sample same-place, cross-year pairs in DESK's metric term
hypotheses: [T1, T4, T11]     parents: [E038, E041, E042]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: rung 4 (4 DESK runs, ~4 GPU-h)
pre-registered: 2026-10-11 11:03 (commit a58b858), before any run; the user chose this line ("Yes, try doing that
over-sampling").

question: restricting DESK's inputs never removed the space-for-time lean at the matched training state (E038b, E041,
E042): the lean is structural to a covariate-to-community map learned from SPATIAL variation. DESK's metric term draws
its 65,536 pairs uniformly over ~80k supervised cell-years, so only ~1 in 2,800 pairs (~24 per step) is two years of
the same place -- the only pairs that carry within-place temporal similarity. Does supervising those far more often
teach DESK how places actually change, and so reduce the lean and improve backcasts?
design: desk_training.spacetime_kernel_loss gains same_cell_frac (config desk.metric_same_cell_frac; 0 reproduces the
old draw bit for bit -- tested): that share of pairs is a point plus another supervised year of the same cell.
desk_tempho_1995 seeds 1 and 2 at same_cell_frac 0.1 and 0.4 (~270x and ~1,100x the natural rate), all else as the
unweighted controls (E022 seeds 1-2), selected and late caches. Evaluation exactly as E038b-E042: backcast attribution
on the common analog direction (--dir-cache the control's selected cache) at selected and late states, paired 6x6-block
bootstrap against the seed-matched controls (attr_pair.py); windowed backcast corr with paired CIs (zone_breakdown
--pairs); spatial fit (best val_kernel); movement profile 1902-2025.
predictions: LEVER -- the full backcast's corr with real change rises (paired CI excluding 0) and its lean on the
common direction does not rise, at BOTH states, more at 0.4 than 0.1; spatial fit may fall a little (fewer
cross-place pairs). NO LEVER -- changes within the seed spread: the stabilizing term already supervises each
cell-year's own ESK target, so pairwise same-cell constraints add little.
decision rule (fixed now): a lever if, for the full backcast, corr(variant) - corr(control) >= +0.02 with CI excluding 0
in >= 3 of the 4 (strength x seed) pairs at the LATE state, and the late-state lean difference <= 0 in >= 3 of 4;
then extend (T0 1986, a third strength). Otherwise record and stop this line.
considerations: M28 (one yardstick, matched states, paired CIs), G6/M9 (selected and late states), G7 (mechanism test,
production unaffected), M17 (within-place supervision could also teach species' regional trends -- check the trend
placebo in zones), P3.

DECISION (pre-registered): NO LEVER. No pair reaches corr +0.02 at the late state (0/4). The lean condition is moot.
Per the rule, this line is recorded and stopped.
WHY (likely):
- DESK's stabilizing term already supervises every cell-year's own ESK target, and is ~90% of the loss, so each
  cell's temporal trajectory is already a training target.
- Adding same-cell cross-year pairs to the metric term adds little temporal constraint beyond that.
- It also spends pairs that would have been cross-place, which is the consistent spatial cost (val_kernel 0.0085-0.0091
  vs controls 0.0075-0.0081).
- The windowed zones measure showed small late-state gains (+0.014 to +0.040, mostly east). On the pre-registered
  full-backcast estimand they vanish. The two measures differ (6-year windows in 1966-83 vs the 1966-86 epoch), and
  the rule used the full backcast.
IMPLICATION (not a decision): the space-for-time lean and the weak temporal skill are not fixed by re-weighting
which pairs DESK's metric loss sees. Temporal supervision would have to enter differently (a temporal target or
readout; see E039/E044), or not through DESK at all.
