# E077 Does the CBC presence term fix the REPRESENTATION's change magnitude on BBS where cover changed most?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-15, before running the arms; a smoke on two CONTROL seeds only, reported below as the noise
# reference)
hypotheses: [CBC (bridge / complement), cover extrapolation]     parents: [E069b, E076, peer E073]     cost: ~1 h cpu
why: the peer (after E076): E073's skeptic found DESK's over-prediction is cross-place overfitting (in-sample k ~1.07
vs spatial-CV k ~0.7); E076 showed the presence term removes DESK's doubled excess backcast error on WINTER lists where
cover changed most. Does the arm's z also shrink DESK's within-cell BBS change magnitude there (k on BBS species change
closer to 1)? If yes, the term fixes the representation; if not, only what the winter lists read.
design (research/exp/e077_bbs_change_k.py): test cells = DESK held-out + buffer cells (never in BBS training nor in the
presence term) with >= 2 surveyed years in 1966-1985 (withheld) and in 2005-2024; observed change per cell and dev
species (log1p, 2005-24 minus 1966-85); predicted change = DESK's z_ema change between the windows through a WITHIN-cell
ridge readout fitted per cache on training cell-years (>= 1996); dev species with >= 30 non-zero test cell-years
(160). k = slope of observed on predicted change after removing each species' mean change over the test cells (k < 1:
over-prediction); corr likewise. Strata: cover top tercile (cover_change_1920_1966.npz) vs rest; Great Plains vs rest.
Arm - control with a 6x6-block bootstrap. Arms: E069b w0.01 seeds 1 and 2 (late primary; selected reported); E076's
withheld-cells arms (pres01x) late, as a replication.

READING (pre-registered): NO REPRESENTATION FIX -- at the late state dk is NEGATIVE in every stratum for both seeds (and
for E076's arms), with intervals below 0; the presence term moves BBS change prediction FURTHER from calibration and
lowers its correlation (-0.04 to -0.05), about equally where cover changed most and elsewhere. The selected states
disagree in sign (s1 -0.05, s2 +0.04, within the seed noise of +0.03 to +0.085).
WHY (likely). The predicted BBS change is not SMALLER under the term (SD ratio 0.99-1.08), so on the breeding-season
readout the term does not shrink DESK's change -- it REDIRECTS it, toward what winter lists of resident species support
and away from breeding-season change of the 160 dev species (migrants included). This refines E069b/E076's "removes
over-movement": on the WINTER readout the backcast moves less harmfully; on the breeding readout its magnitude is
unchanged and its direction worse. Consistent with the season link (winter vs breeding change 0.45-0.52; migrants ~0,
E066c) and with E069b's late-state cost for migrants and all dev species.
IMPLICATION (production, reported): with a breeding-season community and readout, CBC presence supervision fixes the
winter view only; it does not repair DESK's over-predicted breeding-season change where cover changed most. A
representation aligned with winter communities (G11), or a resident-only target set, is the setting where it could.
