# E017 (S2) Where do DESK's backcast errors point -- toward space-for-time analogs?
hypotheses: [S2, S1, T4]     parents: [E016]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~3 min cpu per run
pre-registered: development run first (2026-10-09, tempho1995, numbers below); replications pre-registered at the end.

design: research/exp/analog_pull.py. Held-out cells; analogs = 10 modern cell-epochs whose modern covariates (64 PCs)
are nearest the cell's EARLY covariates (optionally > 200 km away); random cells as the null. pull = projection of a
predictor's backcast move onto the analog direction as a fraction of the noise-free squared distance (halves); align =
the same as a correlation. Predictors: DESK, the linear between / within / pooled covariate maps (E016), the truth.
Neighbour misfit = noise-free true squared distance from the cell's real past to the readout's 20 nearest modern
cell-epochs (in DESK space).

development run (tempho1995 withheld, r24; analogs unrestricted | > 200 km away):
  truth  pull +0.316 [0.257, 0.384] (random +0.129), align 0.279 (random 0.257) | pull +0.235 (random +0.123), align
         0.247 (random 0.249)
  DESK   pull +0.002 [-0.041, 0.046], align +0.003 | pull -0.010 [-0.057, 0.034], align -0.017 (r12, r64 alike: ~0)
  between (the linear space-for-time map; a weak positive control) pull +0.031, align 0.070; within -0.024
  neighbour misfit, r24: DESK 0.165, no-change 0.167, between 0.171, within 0.158; the real past's own nearest 0.066

reading (UNVERIFIED): DESK's backcast moves are NOT toward space-for-time analogs (pull and alignment ~0 at every rank,
near or far): S2's specific mechanism is not supported. The true past is no more aligned with far analogs than with
random places (0.247 vs 0.249) -- no specific space-for-time signal, consistent with E016; the larger pull toward NEAR
analogs is the cell's route-specific modern idiosyncrasy (E014), which its past does not share. DESK's backcast point
borrows from neighbours no better and no worse than the no-change point, both ~2.5x further from the cell's real past
than its best matches. DESK's backcast error behaves like misdirected noise, not analog-structured bias.

predictions for the replications (tempho1985 and tempho1975 withheld, base trained; r24, analogs > 200 km): DESK |pull|
< 0.05 and |align| < 0.06 in every cache; the truth's far-analog alignment within 0.03 of its random-cell alignment;
DESK neighbour misfit within 5% of no-change's in the tempho caches.
