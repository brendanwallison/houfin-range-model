# E027 Is there temporal signal in the covariates beyond regional trend persistence -- at other timescales, or nonlinearly?
hypotheses: [S3, T5, T1, L1]     parents: [E016, E023]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: rung 1, ~15 min cpu
pre-registered: 2026-10-09 ~22:58, before any lagged or nonlinear surrogate was fitted.

question: E016/E023 found that a within-cell linear map from covariates (as DESK sees them: one causal EMA at DESK's
learned ~10.5-year half-life) backcasts decadal community change no better than a placebo of smooth position fields x
year (regional trend persistence), and adds only +0.03-0.06 on top of it. Two things that map cannot see:
(a) other timescales of covariate history (the user's point 3: lags, slow equilibration; L1/S3) -- e.g. a 30-year EMA
    for slow responses, or the un-smoothed states for fast ones;
(b) nonlinear, location-dependent responses -- the same covariate change meaning different things in different
    climates (DESK is nonlinear, but it is trained ~90% on spatial structure; a within-cell nonlinear map is trained
    only on temporal variation).
If neither adds signal, the covariates as represented carry little decadal temporal information beyond what a regional
trend already gives, and DESK-side levers (temporal supervision T1, optimization T11) have a low ceiling: the limit is
the inputs (T5). If either does, there is temporal signal DESK is not extracting.

design: span_reach.py (E023 grid: T0 1996 / 1986 / 1976, spans 10-50 years, backcast windows 1966-71 ... 1990-95 vs
the first ten trained years; same held-out cells; noise-free centered corr), with new within-cell surrogate arms:
- lag<hl>: the covariates EMA'd at half-life hl in {0 (states only), 3, 10.5 (DESK's), 30} years, 32 PCs each;
- lags: all four timescales together (one prior-variance block per timescale), and lags+trend;
- rff: 512 random Fourier features (RBF, lengthscale = median pairwise distance) of the 64 covariate PCs at DESK's
  timescale -- a nonlinear, location-dependent response map -- and rff+trend.
Reference arms as before: trend (placebo), cov, cov+trend. New: PAIRED bootstrap differences on the same 6x6-block
draws for cov+trend - trend, lags+trend - cov+trend, rff+trend - cov+trend, lags - cov, rff - cov.

predictions:
- lags matter (S3/L1): lags+trend - cov+trend > +0.03 with the paired CI above 0 at the farthest window of at least 2 of
  the 3 start years, and a single non-DESK timescale (30 y or 0) beats lag10.5 alone.
- nonlinearity matters: rff+trend - cov+trend > +0.03, CI above 0, at the farthest window of >= 2 of 3 start years.
- null (T5 side): both differences within +-0.03 or CIs spanning 0 in most cells of the grid.
decision rule: either holds -> covariates carry temporal signal that a within-cell learner extracts and DESK does not;
next is a DESK-side test that gives DESK the same access (multi-timescale inputs, or a within-cell temporal objective;
rung 3/4). Null -> the binding limit for temporal extrapolation is the covariate inputs at this grain, not DESK:
prioritize T5 (what is missing from the covariates: which drivers, which grain) and the honest-uncertainty route for
the backcast; deprioritize DESK temporal-supervision runs.
caveat: within-cell targets are noisy observed ESK z at single years; every arm faces the same noise, and the grade is
noise-free, so differences between arms are interpretable but absolute levels are attenuated.

READING (UNVERIFIED): at decadal scales and this grain, the covariates' temporal information for community change is
a shared continental trajectory whose effect depends on a place's environment -- "how a place of this kind responds to
what the whole continent did". A cell's OWN covariate change, beyond that shared trajectory, adds nothing measurable to
a backcast. Caveats: the continental clock can also absorb continent-wide observation trends (observer skill, protocol),
not only habitat; CIs are wide (+-0.05) on ~110 cells; the short-span exception at T0 1996 is unexplained.
Consequences: (1) T5 strengthened at this grain: local covariate change is not where the backcast signal is; (2) the
space-for-time pull (S2) is DESK mapping local covariate change through spatial relationships, which this says carries
no transferable signal; (3) a constructive candidate for the backcast: environment x continental-clock structure,
whose clock for 1900-1940 IS known from the covariates (climate, land use, population history) -- to be tested against
DESK on the same grid before anything else is built.
---- SKEPTIC on E027c (verification d, 2026-10-10 ~03:45; scratch runs reproduce the job exactly) ----

   (246 rows in 1966, 515 in 1967, ~1000 by 1975): composition alone has norm 6.2 in 1966 vs a true 1966-2025

   straight line (PC1 93.5% of variance, R2 on year 0.999) and small against the RFF lengthscale (5.2 vs 20.5), so
   RFF(cell mean + traj(t)) ~ RFF(cell mean) + t x a directional derivative: "where a cell sits in covariate space"
   times LINEAR YEAR.
3. Direct test (T0 1986, span 40): a one-dimensional linear clock along the true direction scores 0.188 / 0.214 /
   0.172 (1966-71 / 72-77 / 78-83); three RANDOM covariate directions differ from it by at most +-0.016, all CIs
   spanning 0. The direction of continental covariate change does not matter.
4. Cell means over cell-specific years: not an artifact (swapping for a fixed-year mean changes nothing).
5. Composition supplied all of the clock's edge at 1972-77, about half at 1978-83, none at 1966-71. The T0 1996
   span-10 exception (rff beats the clock by 0.099) disappears with the clean clock (-0.004 [-0.039, +0.053]).
6. Forking paths: 306 paired contrasts in E027c, 74 with CIs excluding 0; in the clock family (18) only the
   composition-driven exception survives Bonferroni.
7. Over-claim: "within +-0.02" was a point-estimate rule, not an equivalence test. At the longest spans the CIs allow
   rff to beat the clock by up to 0.048 / 0.022 / 0.086 (T0 1996 / 1986 / 1976), against rff+trend's whole gain over
   the trend placebo of 0.038 / 0.081 / 0.076: at two start years the data cannot exclude that a cell's own change
   carries all of rff's gain. RFF-draw variance (sd 0.012-0.025) is outside the CIs. The three T0s share the same
   ~110 truth cells: one replicate.
8. Observation trends cannot be separated by this design (once composition is removed the clock is collinear with
   any linear continental process); the cache has no observer field. Test: pull BBS ObsN per route-year and re-grade
   on observer-continuous cells, or add observer-change / first-year indicators as nuisance blocks.
REVISED READING (supersedes the E027c reading above): at this grain, the covariates' backcast skill is matched by a
placebo that uses them only as a STATIC position (a cell's mean environment) times LINEAR year. Neither a cell's own
covariate change nor the direction of continental covariate change is detectably used: no detectable advantage of a
cell's own covariate change, 95% bound ~0.05 in correlation (~0.09 at T0 1976). This supports T5 more sharply than the
first reading, and it WITHDRAWS consequence (3): the "continental clock" is not a constructive backcast candidate --
for 1900-1940 it reduces to extrapolating a linear trend in environment space over 60-120 years. Whether the linear
trend itself is habitat or observation (observer skill, protocol) is open.
