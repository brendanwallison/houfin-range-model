# E092 Trait-conditioned species-specific similarity: do AVONET traits predict each species' readout prior shape? (stage 0 gate)
# (pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any run)
hypotheses: [readout kernel, species transfer]     parents: [E059e, E085]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: CPU, ~30 min
why: the user (2026-10-17) asked whether species-specific similarity conditioned on AVONET traits is feasible, expecting
little signal and that the losses would only nudge species from the shared structure. E059e: free per-species weights
per z DIMENSION (ARD) did nothing for change (0.089 = iid); what restored change information was each species' own
BETWEEN-place vs WITHIN-place amplitude (split kernel, 0.219). So the species-specific part that matters is that
balance. Traits can only beat free per-species fits where those are noisy (rare species) and are the only route for a
species with no data (transfer). Stage 0 asks the cheap prerequisite: is there trait signal in the fitted shapes at all?
design: fast_arm.py --kernel split (blocks: cell reference-window mean z = between; deviation = within; r24, raw z) on
the six control caches at the SELECTED state (desk_tempho_1995_s1-s4, desk_tempho_1985_s1-s2), now saving each species'
amplitudes. research/exp/e092_trait_gate.py: per species, targets log a_between, log a_within, log(a_within/a_between);
10-fold CV R^2 of ridge on [log prevalence] vs [log prevalence + AVONET traits: log mass, HWI, log beak length / width /
depth, log tarsus, log tail; Habitat, Habitat.Density, Migration, Trophic.Niche, Primary.Lifestyle one-hot]; trait gain
vs 500 permutations of the trait rows. Seed agreement of per-species log amplitudes across the T0 1996 seeds = how
stable the targets are.
reading (pre-registered):
- GATE PASSES if, for log_within OR log_ratio, the trait gain is >= 0.05 AND above the permutation q95 in >= 5 of the 6
  models, AND seed agreement of that amplitude is >= 0.5 (a target that moves with the seed cannot be learned from
  traits). Then stage 1 (pre-registered separately): a hierarchical readout whose per-species amplitudes are shrunk to a
  trait-predicted mean, fitted by summed marginal likelihood, scored leave-species-out (sedentary first: spatial level,
  change skill, attenuation) against free per-species fits and the shared prior.
- FAILS otherwise: traits carry no usable information about the species-specific prior shape; stop.
- Reported regardless: all three targets, migration-class means (sedentary vs migrant shape), sedentary residuals.
considerations: sedentary primary (memory) -- residency is itself a trait here, and its class means are reported;
no House Finch (its traits are not used; it is not among the species); sealed never read.
