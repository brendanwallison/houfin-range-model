**Runtime correction:** “short” referred to draw count, not elapsed time. The real-fit CPU smoke measured 2.97 seconds per gradient. At depth-six saturation, two chains of 128 warmup +128 draws imply roughly 32,256 leapfrog steps (~26.6 CPU hours); 512 warmup +128 draws imply 80,640 steps (~66.6 CPU hours), before other overhead. GPU timings remain unmeasured until the geometry jobs run. Neither these saturated scenarios nor a six-hour timeout predicts completion. One diagonal screen now provides timing calibration first; other sampler jobs are held until measured gradient/leapfrog costs support their configured attempt budget. A compute-cost deferral is not a model-quality verdict.

Warmup and sampling now checkpoint every four transitions using NumPyro's public kernel init/sample API. Full HMC state, RNG and adaptation state are preserved; the full warmup schedule is not shortened or restarted. An independent test interrupts during warmup and reproduces the uninterrupted retained chain and sampler diagnostics exactly after resume. Checkpoints allow continuation after allocation loss or timeout; completed-chunk progress remains available even without posterior draws. The existing older chunked MCMC helper only checkpointed after completing warmup, so it did not cover this risk.

The earlier project diagnostic is src/model/age_hmc_probe.py: the Hessian eigen-spectrum of the negative log posterior in unconstrained HMC coordinates, plus gradient cost and a Laplace metric. src/model/age_hmc_roughness.py separately tests numerical smoothness. These are distinct from ecological growth eigenvalues and from the constrained AutoDelta MAP objective. The new audit reuses the frozen hmc_common potential construction rather than reconstructing the density or dropping nuisance effects.

The practical selection criterion is reliable exploration per unit computation: ESS per postwarmup leapfrog step and per total wall second for ecological parameters, conditional on acceptable numerical/sampler behavior. Geometry explains why a model is difficult and which blocks are involved. It is not a substitute for actual HMC or biological validation. High or varying curvature can produce small steps and divergences, and energy/mixing diagnostics remain necessary. Sources: [Stan diagnostics](https://mc-stan.org/learn-stan/diagnostics-warnings.html), [Betancourt et al., geometric foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5110).

## Exact target and feasible computation

The tested current point has 19,275 unconstrained coordinates; most are zero-sum observer-route nuisance effects. Holding those fixed would change the geometry being assessed. A full dense Hessian would also be expensive to form and diagonalize. The new run_hmc_geometry_audit.py instead uses exact autodiff Hessian-vector products with fully reorthogonalized Lanczos. All model arrays remain JIT arguments, following the project's existing protection against multi-GB constants being embedded in every compilation.

Report raw-coordinate and conditional-prior-standardized Krylov spectra, Ritz residuals, orthogonality, gradient norms by site, and parameter-block loadings of extreme directions. Standardization uses draws from the actual conditional prior distributions followed by NumPyro's support inverse transforms; it is diagonal scale normalization, not posterior whitening or an adopted mass matrix. Priors and data are not changed. The reported positive Ritz ratio is explicitly not a certified condition number; a short Krylov run can miss weak or negative directions. An observed negative Ritz value is evidence of local nonconvexity, not a count of all negative eigenvalues. At a nonstationary point it does not alone establish a saddle-point mode.

The diagnostic also measures central-difference gradient/HVP agreement, potential remainders after removing the linear term over multiple step sizes, and directional curvature at nearby points. Numerical roughness, ordinary smooth nonlinearity, coordinate scaling, prior-only directions and actual posterior dependence must not be conflated. No eigenvalue flooring/absolute-value repair is used to turn unfavorable diagnostics into passes.

## Actual sampler checks

Four full geometry comparisons are queued: relative Allee, absolute Allee, absolute plus native departure, and DESK-v2 absolute plus native departure (seed 41). The absolute control and absolute/native points each have diagonal and block-dense NUTS screens. The block-dense metric adapts the small non-observer block jointly while retaining a diagonal large observer block; it does not allocate a 19k-by-19k dense mass matrix. All latent variables remain in the target.

Each screen has two sequential chains, target acceptance .9 and a depth cap of 6. Diagonal screens use 128 warmup/128 retained draws per chain; block screens use 512 warmup/128 draws. This is a bounded feasibility/performance comparison, not an equal-warmup experiment or a production posterior. Wall-time efficiency includes warmup and compilation; leapfrog efficiency uses retained-draw num_steps. Report every chain's divergences, E-BFMI, acceptance, adapted step size and cap saturation, plus site-wise NumPyro ESS/split-Rhat and efficiency. The latter are not silently labeled rank-normalized bulk/tail diagnostics. Near-MAP starts and a depth cap cannot certify global exploration, even if all draws are finite. Actual inference still needs longer dispersed chains and reliable diagnostics/Monte Carlo error for derived biological estimands.

Sampler jobs are held behind their exact-point geometry dependency and a measured-cost calibration. Geometry jobs are limited to two hours and sampler attempts to six hours; partial results, warmup checkpoints and per-chain outputs are retained. All jobs use the existing TACC shared workers after the biological comparisons. Specifications: HMC_DIAGNOSTIC_RUNS_20261101.json. Live status/results: HMC_DIAGNOSTIC_RESULTS_20261101.md. A dedicated persistent watcher collects outputs, including failures, partial work and compute-cost forecasts. Six hours bounds one attempt; it is not a declaration that both chains will finish.
