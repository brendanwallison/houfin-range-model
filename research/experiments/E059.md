  The user's point applies THERE.

-5.0 vs -8.9 at 24 dims; -4.2 vs -8.1 at 64), a ~50x ratio.
- One iid amplitude is fitted mostly to between-place (spatial) variation, so the readout's temporal coefficients ARE
  its spatial ones (space-for-time). Change predictions come out poorly aimed (0.089) and ~4x too large (k 0.26).
- Reweighting dims toward whitened helps a little (+0.03). ARD on levels does not help: it still fits spatial levels.

  the amplitude ratio and with reweighting/ARD shown not to substitute for it.
