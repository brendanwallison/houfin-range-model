# E073 Anchored delta: should DESK be a persistent spatial field plus a covariate-driven within-place deviation?
hypotheses: [T4, T1, T3, T9, B1, G3-related R/T]     parents: [E039, E016, E018, E059, E069a2]     eval_version: pre-v1
cost: CPU only; cached arrays + yearly covariate states. Part A ~15 min; part B ~1-2 h per cache and target; part C
~1-2 h. Script: research/exp/anchored_delta.py.
pre-registered: 2026-10-14, before any E073 number exists (a planted self-check on synthetic species ran first, as
verification (a); it uses no bird data).

## Why
The user (2026-10-14): DESK clearly has the capacity to be decent spatially, but learning the spatial field causes
problems for inference. Should it learn an underlying spatial field and then deviations from it, in a way that
EXTRAPOLATES rather than interpolates, given that early-20th-century supervision will at best be weak (CBC)? "Test it
comprehensively ... understanding first and performance second."

What is already known (E039, community target, established):
- A map that must share coefficients between and within places (space-for-time) predicts decadal change at
  0.11-0.12.
- A linear map of each place's own covariate changes (within_raw: Mundlak within block, no time or position input)
  scores 0.27/0.35 at T0 1996/1986; DESK 0.24/0.35; DESK's within recalibration 0.29-0.37.
- So DESK learns its change response implicitly, no better than a linear within map.

The proposed form: z(x,t) = zbar(x) + delta(x,t).
- zbar is free to be as spatial as needed (it only interpolates in space).
- delta = g(c(x,t) - cbar(x); kind of place), with g(0) = 0 and no year or position-by-time input, so the only route
  for change is covariate change, which exists back to 1902.
- Linear-in-change with place-dependent slopes extrapolates proportionally.
- Space enters as a prior on the slopes (space-for-time), not as the answer.
- What covariates cannot explain (the trend placebo's edge) is left to honest uncertainty (E072, parked) and to the
  population model (G3).

E073 asks whether the pieces of that design hold up, and WHY, before anything is built into DESK (rung 1 before
rung 4).

## Design (research/exp/anchored_delta.py)
Covariates:
- yearly states 1902-2025 (climate 240, LUH2 land use 33, HYDE 3, BUI 7, static 19), DESK's transforms,
  standardized on training rows;
- causal EMAs at half-lives 0/3/10/25 y from 1902;
- dc = c - cbar(cell), where cbar is the mean over the cell's trained-year rows;
- W = whitened within-PCs of dc per stream group: climate 24, landuse 12, human (hyde+bui) 6, "all" 42.

Estimand (B, C): the standard out-of-community species change, as fair_rivals --target dev / species_readout_probe:
- held-out cells; early epoch vs 2005-25;
- log1p of ABBA half means;
- noise-free pooled corr and k, 6x6-block bootstrap (200), paired differences.

Strata:
- SEDENTARY (primary);
- resident, migratory, all, hofi_like (same similarity table as E059g/E067);
- the community's ESK r24 as the secondary target.

Every map is a per-species BLR (lib.blr, ML amplitudes), fitted on training cells x trained years: within maps on
within-cell deviations, the between map on cell means.

### Part A: exposure and novelty (no bird data; all 3,902 BBS cells; by Great Plains zone west / GP / east)
- A1. Within-place share of each stream's variance, 1966-2025 and 1902-2025.
- A2. Novelty of the 1920-39 deviation from each cell's 1966-2025 mean, against 1966-2025 six-year-window
  deviations:
  - Mahalanobis per dim in the stream's within-PC space;
  - median ratio to the reference median;
  - share beyond the reference 99th percentile.
- A3. Analog support: nearest-neighbour distance of the 1920-39 full state (streams weighted equally, top 20 PCs) to
  OTHER cells' 1966-2025 windows, against the same for modern windows. This asks whether space can stand in for time
  in 1920.
- A4. Direction: the largest land-use differences, 1920-39 minus 1996-2025, by zone.
- A5. Direction overlap: cosine between each cell's 1920-39 deviation and its 1966-71 deviation. Does the BBS era
  start partway along the 1920 -> 2000 path?

### Part B: what drives within-place change? (desk_tempho_1995_s1 and desk_tempho_1985_s1; DESK arms also s2)
- B1 streams: within maps on W_climate, W_landuse, W_human, W_all (h = 10).
- B2 history: W_all at h = 0, 3, 10, 25; h0 + h10 jointly.
- B3 space-for-time:
  - between map on cell means, with the dynamic part projected on the SAME within loadings and static PCs as
    controls;
  - s4t_pure: its dynamic coefficients applied to dc;
  - anchored_prior_s4t: a within map shrunk toward the between coefficients (prior-centred BLR);
  - kappa: least-squares scale of the space-for-time prediction on within deviations.
- B4 context (varying coefficient): W_all x [1, 2 static PCs, 2 mean-state PCs] (vc_cov); W_all x [1, 4 DESK zbar
  dims] (vc_desk).
- B5 nonlinearity with g(0) = 0: RFF(W) - RFF(0), ell 2 and 6, alone and with the linear block.
- B6 DESK:
  - desk_within (within readout);
  - desk_split_level (production-form split readout);
  - W_all + DESK jointly;
  - R^2 of DESK's within z deviation on W_all, and the score of its covariate-linear part vs its residual part.
    Does DESK's change behave like a covariate response, or like something else, e.g. place-by-time shortcuts?
- B7 placebo (position RFF 300 km x time, 96 x 2 draws) and placebo + W_all: how much regional trend lies beyond
  the covariate response.
- B8 attribution: share of W_all's predicted change variance by stream (all, sedentary).

### Part C: does it EXTRAPOLATE in covariate space? (base cache gp_species_base: all years trained; held-out cells)
- Training cells are restricted by their covariate trend (slope per decade of the leading within-PC of landuse, or of
  climate).
- Designs:
  - magnitude: train on |trend| <= 70th percentile; score held-out cells beyond vs inside that range;
  - sign: train on increasing cells only, score decreasing cells, and vice versa.
- Penalty = restricted-fit minus full-fit score on the same held-out cells (paired draws), with k (overshoot or
  undershoot) beside it.
- Arms: W_all linear, W_landuse, W_climate, RFF (ell 2), DESK within readout (its readout restricted the same way;
  DESK's representation saw all training cells, an asymmetry noted in advance).

  0.5). Climate is less novel (< 0.3). Analog support (A3) is better than within-place novelty: space has places

- B1/B6: W_all (linear, no time) >= desk_within - 0.03 on sedentary species at both T0s (E039 community result

- B3: s4t_pure << within (E016, robust); anchored >= within; median kappa between 0 and 1. (moderate)

- NOVELTY: if share beyond p99 > 0.5 in any zone for land use, the backcast's delta is an extrapolation there by
  construction, and its uncertainty must scale with novelty (E072 shape).
- Everything is UNVERIFIED until (b)-(d): (b) the headline W_all re-derived by fair_rivals' within_raw on the same
  cache; (c) both T0s plus the DESK arms on seed 2; (d) a skeptic.

---- PART D DESIGN NOTE (2026-10-14; not pre-registered, a build plan only if B/C support it) ----
Where a two-part DESK (z = zbar + delta) would go (code map, read-only survey):
- Model: MultiStreamAutoencoder (model_arch.py:192-303): per-stream Linear-GELU-2xBMLPBlock (LayerNorm residual),
  mixer to latent 64, decoder, 5x5 masked neighbour conv added as gamma*conv(z) (gamma init 0). No year or coordinate
  input; place enters via the conv, the static streams and covariate levels.
- Training (desk_training.py train_model_ema): one optimizer step per epoch over whole-year grids 1940-2025. Losses:
  - L_stab: MSE of z_ema to per-year ESK targets (training cells, trained years);
  - metric: spacetime_kernel_loss, uniform pairs from training cell-years; same-cell pairs off by default
    (metric_same_cell_frac, E043);
  - recon 0.1. Selection: val_kernel on held-out cells x trained years.
- Insertion points:
  (a) cbar: normalized covariates averaged over trained years only, computed after cov_win is loaded (~dt.py:2776).
      Save it in desk_meta.npz, and use it in build_final_z_cube.py and validate_spacetime.py. NOTE:
      static_channels.py (E041) averages over 1966-2025 INCLUDING withheld years; do not reuse it for cbar.
  (b) heads: zbar from static streams + cbar (once per cell; keep the neighbour conv here). delta = g(x - cbar;
      context) with g(0) = 0 exactly: a bias-free network without LayerNorm, or g(d) - g(0). The output EMA is linear
      with weights summing to 1, so EMA(zbar + delta) = zbar + EMA(delta).
  (c) a within-deviation loss next to L_stab (~dt.py:1910): per-cell trained-year means of target and z_ema removed
      before the MSE. Noise-aware variant: ABBA half cross-products. Add the new keys to the resume fingerprint
      (~dt.py:1604); the tiled path needs the same change, or restrict to spatial_tiles = 1.
- Variants worth one run each, gated by B: inputs to g = no_clock groups only (mgmt / primary-secondary accounting held
  at cbar); g linear-in-d with context-dependent slopes (vc) vs a small MLP; space-for-time prior as a penalty pulling
  dg/dd toward the zbar head's input gradient.
