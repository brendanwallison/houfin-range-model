# 54 km downstream ladder (pre-registered 2026-10-29)

Goal (user, 2026-10-29): the House Finch age model at 54 km with relative Allee, a single habitat field, route-observer
effects, and amplitudes that are not inflated by joint-mode estimation -- reached as a ladder so each change is read
alone. The premise is to LEARN how source-sink dynamics change over time: habitat change must stay learnable in every
step (static habitat is a diagnostic only). Any calibrated constant from another DESK run (e.g. split-readout 0.14) is
re-measured, not reused.

Inputs: research/experiments/GRID54_INPUTS_2026-10-29.md (revision 2). Fit code: tracked copy of the frozen
code_projectionfix1 snapshot, ~/houfin/work/houfin/research/GRID54_LADDER_20261029/code; each step runs from a frozen
archive of one commit under /mnt/e/Datasets/houfin/grid54/runs/<step>/code (CODE_MANIFEST.sha256). Runner:
research/bin/grid54_fit.sh. Local RTX 3090; replicates on TACC gpu-a100-small.

Reference (27 km, same DESK, same readout): shared summer64, research/experiments/DESK_AND_DOWNSTREAM_SYNTHESIS_2026-10-26.md
sec. 9. Cold MAP: lambda .626 -> .820 (+.194), suitable 17.6% -> 35.3%, held-out T -1.895 / S -1.255. Refined: lambda
.656 -> .956 (+.300), suitable 10.5% -> 34.5%, T -1.804 / S -1.210.

Readouts for every step: held-out mean log density, temporal (2021-25) and spatial (pinned 216 km blocks), refined and
MAP; all-land and central-east (95-80W, 25-49N) fundamental lambda 1902-15 / 1940-55 / 2010-25; suitable / source /
sink shares by era; fecundity and survival intercepts and amplitudes; native-range sink and Allee-dead shares (west
sink audit); Great Plains crossing; refine validity (gate, final |grad|). One fit per step; no paired intervals unless
replicates exist, so differences under ~.01 nats/obs are read as "no difference".
