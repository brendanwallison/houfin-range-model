- Each new task has a durable launch intent, detached worker, process identities,
  stdout/stderr log and atomic completion receipt. Restarting the supervisor adopts

- Completion requires explicit result artifacts and finite values. Missing/stale
  logs do not prove process death. An ambiguous launch or scientific failure is
  retained for investigation, with independent registered tasks allowed to proceed.

  released only after its observed process terminates. No TACC jobs are submitted.

  model worktree is edited. Artifact identity is recorded by each fit/readout.

  output, with at least 5 GiB free. These are conservative bounds within the daily

  The active agent continues collecting, interpreting and registering the next
  discriminating work; no user reply is a dependency.
