# E041 Does DESK NEED the land-use clock? Train with management and secondary-land channels held static
hypotheses: [T5, T9, T11]     parents: [E035, E038]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: rung 4 (2 DESK runs, ~2.5 GPU-h)
pre-registered: 2026-10-10 21:39 (commit 4859ffa), before any run.

question: E035e found that most of DESK's real temporal signal in withheld decades is a CLOCK read from continent-wide
land-use management trends (fertilizer, irrigation) and the age of secondary land; those trends are flat before ~1950,
which likely freezes the backcast the age model uses for 1902-1939. If DESK never sees those channels change -- each
cell keeps its own 1966-2025 mean of the 19 management and 2 secondary-land channels in every year -- must it read
change from the 12 class fractions (which drift all century), or does its backcast skill collapse?
design: static_channels.py --channels luh_mgmt luh_secage around run_encoder.py desk, cache.py and epoch_variant.py
(as E038), desk_tempho_1995 seeds 1 and 2 (same splits). Controls: the unweighted seeds 1-4 (E022/E032) and E038's
static-temperature runs.
measures: (1) pooled raw backcast corr over the first three pre-T0 windows (zone_breakdown, all zones) and the
attribution of E035e (--families --luh-split: does the class-fraction part grow?); (2) spatial fit (best val_kernel);
(3) movement before 1950 (production_backcast.py on the new models: does z keep moving 1902-1950?).
predictions: CLOCK NECESSARY -- backcast corr falls well below the controls' seed spread (e.g. 0.12-0.14 -> <= 0.07
pooled) and the class part stays small; CLASSES CAN SUBSTITUTE -- corr within the controls' spread, the class part
grows (>= 0.15), and z keeps moving before 1950 (a candidate for a backcast that is not frozen before 1950). Spatial
fit: little cost expected (the management channels' spatial information is kept as per-cell means).
decision: CLASSES CAN SUBSTITUTE -> escalate as a production candidate (static management channels) together with the
clock finding; CLOCK NECESSARY -> record that DESK's withheld-decade skill depends on post-1950 management trends and
escalate what that means for 1902-1939 (the backcast has little land-use time signal there).
considerations: M26 (uniform trend = clock), M22 (saturation), G6/G7 (mechanism test, production unaffected), M19,
P3.
