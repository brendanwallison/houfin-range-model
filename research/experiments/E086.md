(96 species, 16,000 landmarks); truncation leaves ~14% normalized RMSE on the centred kernel (centred effective rank
~53), so structure exists beyond 64, but going there needs a new ESK fit and DESK retraining. Within 64: DESK's change
predictability is low but flat past 32 (corr ~0.08-0.22); readout gains with rank are spatial (E086e stage 2); the
observed-community oracle still rises at 64 (sedentary ESK48 -> 64 +0.011 / +0.020, E086e); BUT with sparse target data
24 dims beat 64 (E086g) -- extra dims cost exactly where DESK is meant to work. A prior that gives the tail its own
amplitude would let each species' readout use the tail when its data support it and shrink it when they don't. If that
reconciles the two regimes, a higher rank would be worth having only under such a prior.
design: fair_rivals --target dev (score_both dev settings) --commonality --rank 64 --desk-split 24 (DESK dims 1-24 and
25-64 as two blocks with their own ML amplitudes in every fit that contains DESK). Full data and the two sparse regimes
(row 3%, cell 10%; thinning seeds 1-2) on desk_tempho_1995_s1_late and desk_tempho_1985_s1_late. Compared with the same
settings' single-block r24 and r64 runs (E086f full data; E086g / E086h thinned).
reading (sedentary common primary; sedentary all, rare + mid, all dev reported):
- THE SPLIT GETS BOTH if its DESK change skill is within 0.005 of the better of single-block r24 / r64 (or above it) in
  the full-data AND both sparse regimes, at both caches (seed means), for DESK alone and for DESK - strong.
- If it gets both, a higher rank under the same prior is a candidate (ESK refit + DESK retrain, separately
  pre-registered); if the split loses in the sparse regimes, the tail cannot be made safe by the prior and a higher rank
  is not worth pursuing for the downstream regime.
CLARIFICATION to the E086i "why" (2026-10-20, the user: "I thought we are not centering"; before any E086i run):
ESK is UNCENTRED (meta centered: false; contract Z.Z' ~ uncentred Ruzicka). Its truncation diagnostics at rank 64:
uncentred normalized RMSE 0.069 (effective rank 18 exact / 7 approx) -- the kernel actually used; centred 0.141
(effective rank 53 / 16) -- a DIAGNOSTIC of the variation around the mean community, the part a centred readout (BLR with
intercept) and any change estimand can use. The "~14% / ~53" above is the centred diagnostic; the kernel itself is
reproduced to ~7% at 64. Neither says whether structure beyond 64 is covariate-predictable or useful for change.

==== E086l (pre-registered 2026-10-20, before any run) PRODUCTION-FORM ORACLE: does rank 128 help the downstream readout with perfect community knowledge? ====
The user asked whether the rank-128 ESK oracle improves downstream performance "if used directly", and whether that is a
meaningful test. It is an UPPER BOUND: if observed coordinates at 128 do not beat 64 in the age model's readout form,
a rank-128 DESK cannot; if they do, DESK must still predict dims 65-128 (E086k C: weakly). E086k's oracle was a change
readout (within-fitted); the production readout is LEVEL-fitted, where the tail is damped by its small level variance.
design: research/exp/e086l_oracle_level.py -- per dev species, one iid-prior BLR (lib/blr, ML amplitude) of cell-level
log1p abundance (even surveyed years) on the observed community's first k ESK-128 dims (odd years; E086k's
esk128_annual.npy), fitted on training cells' trained-year means; applied to held-out cells' community change (odd
years) and modern level; scored noise-free against species change / level (even years; odd-year half for the noise-free
denominator); k = 24, 64, 96, 128; 6x6-block bootstrap; caches desk_tempho_1995_s1, desk_tempho_1985_s1.
reading: THE TAIL HELPS THE DOWNSTREAM FORM if change (k128 - k64) >= +0.01 with CI > 0 for sedentary common or all dev
at both caches; level reported. If not met, rank 128 is closed for the production readout (an upper bound fails).
