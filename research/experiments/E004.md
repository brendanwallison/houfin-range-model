# E004 Turnover attenuation atlas: where does temporal signal leave the pipeline?
hypotheses: [R1, T0, T1, T6, B3]     parents: [E002, E003]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~10 min cpu per cache
pre-registered: 2026-10-08, BEFORE any local data existed (no number below has been seen)

question: On one set of cell-epochs, how much NOISE-FREE temporal vs spatial community signal survives exact
Ruzicka -> ESK r6/r24/r64 -> DESK (z_ema, raw z), and is temporal signal lost because it is small or because its
directions are truncated?

design: research/exp/atlas.py on the gp_species_base cache (population "trained") and the desk_tempho_1995 cache
(populations "trained" and "withheld"); split halves = ABBA within each cell-epoch (esk_project.py); spatial
pairs disjoint, lags 1/2/4/8/16 cells; block-bootstrap CIs; DESK graded on held-out cells only.

predictions (written before data):
- Temporal signal is SMALL: a cell's ~40-year change equals the spatial turnover of ~1-2 cells (27-54 km)
  (km_equivalent < 80). If > 150 km, the "space dominates time" premise of R1 is wrong at the community level.
- R1 strong form (truncation drops temporal DIRECTIONS): temporal retention at r24 < 0.7 x the spatial retention
  at the same signal size. R1 weak form (temporal change is merely small): ratio within [0.85, 1.15].
  My prior: weak form more likely (0.6), because ESK's leading components are broad gradients and a cell's
  composition shifts mostly along them.
- DESK temporal slope (raw z, r24, held-out) in [0.2, 0.6] with temporal corr in [0.2, 0.5]; DESK spatial slope
  at lag >= 4 in [0.7, 1.1] with corr > 0.7. Logged training diagnostics (rotation ratio 0.29-0.64) imply the
  slope; nothing measured implies the correlation.
- z_ema temporal slope > raw z temporal slope only if the true change is smooth over decades (T6).

decision rule:
- R1 strong supported -> E-next: alternative bases (temporal-deviation basis; era-balanced landmarks) at the ESK
  stage, before any DESK work.
- DESK temporal corr >= 0.4 but slope <= 0.5 -> under-movement with roughly right direction: T0 (is this the
  MSE-optimal shrinkage given target reliability?) then T1 (loss reweighting surrogate) and B1 (readout block
  prior) -- the downstream lever may suffice.
- DESK temporal corr < 0.3 -> direction failure: T3/T4/T5 (inputs, space-for-time) before anything else.
- withheld-population numbers far below trained -> extrapolation in time, not representation, is the bottleneck.
