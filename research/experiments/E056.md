# E056 Does a longer training span help DESK's backcast? E024/E032's span and time-weighted models on today's yardstick
hypotheses: [N2, T1]     parents: [E023, E024/E024b, E029/E029b, E032, E039h-o]     eval_version: pre-v1
cost: ~2 h cpu, no training
pre-registered: 2026-10-11 22:10, before any of these re-scorings exist. Prompted by the user:
- the full span of BBS should carry far more temporal signal than the 30-40-year spans the validation leaves;
- "do the span-dose test as well".
What already exists (raw backcast correlation, the older estimand):
- E023 (linear surrogates): at a fixed reach a SHORTER span next to the target backcasts better (0.22 vs 0.08, 20 vs
  40 years).
- E024/E024b (DESK, two seeds): a 20-year span next to the target (1986-2005) beat the 40-year model (1986-2025) by
  +0.038 [+0.020, +0.057] at T0 1986. At T0 1996, 1996-2015 vs 1996-2025: +0.010 (one seed).
- E029/E032 (DESK, 4 seeds): all years trained, years near the target up-weighted (half-life 10 y): +0.032 at T0 1986,
  +0.016 at T0 1996 (final epoch).
- So adding decades FAR from the target did not help and may hurt. Proximity, not span, carried the signal (likely
  because decadal trends drift, as E023 showed for the surrogates).
- The user's case, adding decades NEAR the target, is the withheld window itself and cannot be tested inside BBS.
- What is new here: none of those models were scored against the fair rivals (trend placebo, strong base) or for
  held-out spatial skill. E039 showed raw backcast correlation can be matched by a trend placebo.
DESIGN (no training; existing caches):
- E056a: span vs long, paired.
  - Modern reference epoch 1996-2005, which every model trained on (the suite's 2005-25 lies outside the span models).
  - Early epoch: the suite's withheld 1966-86.
  - Pairs: T0 1986: span seed 0 vs long seed 2; span seed 1 vs long seed 1. T0 1996: span seed 0 vs long seeds 1 and 2.
  - Selected and late states. ESK epoch groups from esk_project.py --modern 1996 2005 (written to the job dir; caches
    only read).
  - Readout and rivals are fitted on the SPAN model's training rows for both arms (a matched readout).
- E056b: time-weighted (yw10) vs uniform, seed-matched, 4 seeds x 2 T0s x 2 states, on the suite's default epochs.
- Quantities:
  - (a) raw backcast change (short - long, or weighted - uniform);
  - (c) DESK's gain beyond the trend placebo, paired;
  - space_level of each.
PREDICTIONS / RULE:
- E056a:
  - SPAN HELPS (the user's hypothesis, in the direction testable here): long - short >= +0.02 on (a) with CI > 0 in
    both T0 1986 pairs.
  - PROXIMITY HELPS (E024b replicates on this yardstick): short - long >= +0.02 with CI > 0 in both T0 1986 pairs.
  - Otherwise: no span effect.
  - T0 1996 is reported (E024: ~0).
  - Whether either survives beyond the placebo, (c), is reported alongside.
- E056b: TIME WEIGHTING HELPS if weighted - uniform >= +0.02 with CI > 0 in >= 3 of 4 seed pairs at the late state

  +0.02 late); (c) about half of that, since part of the gain may be a trend the placebo also has.
