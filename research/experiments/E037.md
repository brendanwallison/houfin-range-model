# E037 Repair the covariate GP baseline (defect A13) until it is an acceptable baseline
hypotheses: [A13]     parents: [E006]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: rung 0-1 (harness), then a suite re-run (~2 h gpu each)
pre-registered: 2026-10-10 ~20:30 (the user: "Wouldn't the solution be to iteratively revise the covariate GP approach
until it is an acceptable baseline?"), before any variant was run.

why it matters: a GP straight from the covariates to each species, with no community embedding, is the most direct
test of whether DESK adds anything; with it broken, that question was unanswered. The spacetime GPs share the bound
problems (E006: product time lengthscale at its upper bound; spacetime_sum degenerate) and are repaired next.
diagnosis (code map, 2026-10-10): (1) lengthscale bounds come only from the ~3 contiguous patches the shape is fitted
on; a channel barely varying there is capped near e^-7 (my own cd4fb91 fix for an earlier crash produced exactly this),
while on prediction rows such channels (land use c4per, flood) span ~20 sd and dominate distances; (2) 302 collinear
channels, no reduction; (3) a nearly flat kernel with an inflated amplitude (global-mean centring, a 1e3 start
multiplier, an absolute log-s2 cap) -- unstable, absurd means; (4) a line search that aborts.
design: research/exp/covgp_harness.py reproduces the suite's covariate arm with its own routines (fit_shared_shape,
blocked_scales, predict_local) on the gp_species_base cache and changes one piece at a time: features (302 channels |
32 whitened PCs), bounds (shape rows | all training rows in units of each feature's spread, [0.05, 20] sd), shape
sample (a few contiguous blocks | many small random blocks), amplitude (uncapped | s2 <= 10 var_y, no 1e3 start).
Variants: v0 suite (reproduce the failure), v1 bounds only, v2 PCA + bounds, v3 PCA + bounds + stratified sample,
v4 = v3 + amplitude cap.
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (fixed now): A1 fit health -- converged, or stopped by the iteration cap with < 10% of
lengthscales at a bound; A2 sanity -- < 0.1% of held-out predicted log1p means outside [-1, max observed + 1] and the
median ratio of max predictive variance to the species' variance < 3; A3 leave-one-out level skill on training rows,
median over species > 0; A4 held-out level skill, median > 0, and not worse than a linear (BLR) covariate model on the
same features (median GP - linear >= -0.01).
decision: the first variant meeting A1-A4 is ported into validate_gp_species (as options, then defaults), unit tests
added (constant / heavy-tailed channels, prediction range), and E006 re-run on both models; if none passes, diagnose the
failing criterion and iterate (kernel choice, centring per block, multi-start shape fit) before anything else.
