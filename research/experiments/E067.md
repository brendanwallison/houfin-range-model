Pre-registered:
- MIGRANTS HURT SEDENTARY TARGETS: NOT met. Dropping the community's migrants does not help sedentary targets; at
  T0 1996 it hurts (-0.093, CI < 0).
- MIGRANTS HELP: NOT met at both T0s (Cres - C96 < 0 only at T0 1996; adding migrants helps at neither).
- BROADER HELPS:
  - the mixed broadening (C96_A) is not significant for sedentary targets;
  - the resident broadening (Cres_resA - Cres) is met: +0.071 / +0.059, CI > 0 at both T0s.
- REALISM: on sedentary B the surrogate is within 0.019 / 0.058 of DESK, just outside the 0.05 bar at T0 1986.
  Composition conclusions need a DESK retrain before they count.
READING (likely, not verified):
- What helps sedentary targets is MORE training species to learn habitat from, not FEWER migrants. A 69-species
  resident community learns less; a 121-species resident community recovers it (~= C96 at T0 1996, better at T0
  1986).
- So DESK's sedentary advantage (E059h) is more likely BIOLOGICAL (local, year-round habitat the covariates see) than
  an artifact of the migrant penalty.
- Level is insensitive to composition (+-0.01).
IMPLICATION (production): a resident-only community would not help sedentary prediction unless it is also LARGER. If
the community is rebuilt on residents (e.g. for CBC comparability, E066c: residents' season link 0.52 vs 0.45),
target ~120 residents, not 69.
