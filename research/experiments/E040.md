# E040 Repair the spacetime GP baselines (product and sum kernels) until they are acceptable baselines
hypotheses: [A5]     parents: [E006, E037]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: rung 0-1 (harness), then a suite re-run
pre-registered: 2026-10-10 21:34 (commit 664b1cc), before any full run; one 400-row, 3-iteration smoke run of the
sum + cell configuration was made to check the code (s2/var 111, lengthscales ~5000 km: not a result).

why it matters: the spacetime GPs are the suite's "honest bar" -- what observed abundance alone predicts from
neighbouring places (space) and from a place's own other years (time). E006 found both degenerate: the product
kernel's time lengthscale at its upper bound and unconverged; the sum kernel's persistent field ~0.99 correlated across
the continent with its change component collapsed to an uncorrelated per-cell drift. Those are the two artifacts E037
found in the covariate GP: fitting blocks of neighbouring places centred on the continental mean (a flat, inflated
kernel absorbs each block's regional level) and a place's repeated years taken as independent (its lasting quirk
taken for field structure).
design: research/exp/stgp_harness.py on the desk_tempho_1995 cache (1966-1995 withheld everywhere), the suite's own
fitting and prediction routines, one change at a time: samplers (contiguous blocks | random cells with 4 years each
for the shape, blocks of whole random cells for the scales), bounds (the suite's | physical: 10-10,000 km, 1-1,000
years), and a same-cell term rho [same cell] exp(-|dt| / l_cell).
variants: s0 product, suite (reproduce); s0sum sum kernel, suite; s1 / s1sum random cells + physical bounds; s2 /
s2sum = s1 / s1sum + the same-cell term.
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (fixed now; all on level skill vs the training mean, median over species, 3,000 rows per set):
B1 fit health -- converged, or stopped by the iteration cap with < 10% of parameters at a bound; and no degenerate
component (sum kernel: change share and change lengthscale not at their bounds);
B2 sanity -- < 0.1% of predicted log1p means outside [-1, max + 1] in every set; median max predictive variance < 3x
the species' variance;
B3 SPACE (held-out places, trained years) >= the inverse-distance average of the 8 nearest training places - 0.01;
B4 TIME (trained places, 1966-75) >= persistence (each place's own trained-years mean) - 0.01;
B5 SPACE_TIME (held-out places, 1966-75) >= the inverse-distance reference - 0.01.
predictions: if the two E037 artifacts drive the degeneracy, s1/s2 fix B1 (no flat continental field; the sum
kernel's change part regains spatial correlation) and s2's cell term carries a place's own persistence into TIME (B4)
without shortening the spatial fields; if B3/B5 still fail with healthy fits, a constant mean is the problem (large
continental gradients pushing the kernel flat) -- next: a spatial trend surface as the mean function.
decision: the first variant (per kernel) meeting B1-B5 is ported into validate_gp_species (as options, then
defaults) with unit tests, and the suite re-run; if none passes, diagnose the failing criterion and iterate.
considerations: G7 (a repair judged by fixed criteria, not by what it does to DESK's standing), M23 (leave-one-out is
not transfer -- not used here), M24 (fitting samples), M19, P3.

its floor, so neighbouring places are treated as unrelated and held-out places get the continental mean (skill 0).
Fitted on contiguous blocks, each cell's ~30 years dominate the likelihood: cells are very persistent while
neighbours are only moderately alike, and a product kernel cannot be both, so it drops the spatial part (artifact (b)
of E037, in its spatial form). Random cells fix the fit (s1 passes B1, B2, B4, B5) but s1 misses B3 by 0.028: its
77 km lengthscale is a compromise between a place's own persistence and regional similarity. The same-cell term
(s2) is the pre-registered next step.
