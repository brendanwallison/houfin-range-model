# E050 Two missing habitat drivers, tested where they should act: forest age/succession and CRP grassland
# (pre-registered 2026-10-11 18:07, before building the covariates or seeing any result)
hypotheses: [T5, M26, M6]     parents: [E049c, E047b]     eval_version: v1     cost: ~350 MB downloads + ~40 min cpu
why: the user approved (2026-10-11) the two public candidates from the missing-products review.
- Forest age/succession: eastern bird change is tied to farm abandonment, regrowth and maturation. LUH carries only
  a modelled secondary-forest age (secma).
- CRP: the Conservation Reserve Program retires cropland to grass cover. CropAT excludes cropland pasture and idle
  land, so CRP is invisible to it, yet it is a known grassland-bird driver.
data (public, no login; checksums recorded beside the files under /mnt/e/Datasets/houfin/external/):
- Forest stand age, Pan et al. (USFS RDS-2015-0043): 2006 CONUS and 2011 CONUS+Canada+Alaska, 1 km. Published sha256
  checked.
- CRP cumulative acres under contract by county, 1986-2008 (FSA cumulative08.xls, saved 2009). The FSA site is
  unreachable from here, so this is the Internet Archive copy (2025-12-20 snapshot), converted to CSV with xlrd in a
  separate tools venv.
- Census 2018 cartographic county boundaries (500k), to map counties to the 27 km grid.
covariates (research/exp/aux_covariates.py, 27 km grid, per year):
- FOREST AGE, back-cast from the 2011 map. A pixel of age A in 2011 is age A - (2011 - y) in year y, or not yet
  established if that is negative. Per cell-year: established-forest share; shares aged 0-20, 20-60 and 60+; mean
  age of established forest.
  - Assumption: only the latest stand-replacing establishment is known. Earlier states of a pixel (e.g. older forest
    later cut) are unknown, so this records succession since the last disturbance, not full history.
- CRP: county acres under contract / county land area, area-weighted to cells. Zero before 1986; 2008 held to 2025.
test: guild_dose.py --pooled --rival-all with the aux channels in place of CropAT. Gain = (all covariate change +
aux change) - all covariate change, within type and kernel-local; 8 moved-aux nulls; guild-pooled; both T0s.
predictions / rules (within type; kernel-local reported alongside):
- E050a FOREST AGE ACTS WHERE IT SHOULD: forest-guild gain > max null, with net CI > 0, at both T0s. Grassland gain
  ~0 (CI includes 0). Woodland is expected positive too.
- E050b CRP ACTS WHERE IT SHOULD: grassland-guild gain > max null, with net CI > 0, at both T0s. Forest gain ~0.
- Otherwise: report sizes. A driver that acts in the wrong guild, or equally in all, is read as a regional proxy
  (M26), not habitat.
caveats:
- The guilds are small (12 grassland and 53 forest species pass the filters).
- The T0s share cells, so they are not independent.
- Forest age is a back-cast from one snapshot.
- CRP covers only 1986-2008, so its "change" is essentially the modern CRP map, since the early epoch had none.
considerations: M26, M6, corrected M17, T5, E049c design.

WHY (likely, not tested):
- CRP: the epoch "change" is just the modern CRP map, since enrollment began in 1986. That map is concentrated in
  Plains cropland counties, and county-level shares are small. It is plausibly collinear with the crop and climate
  covariates DESK already sees. Any grassland-bird response may be local (field-scale) or lagged, below what county
  totals on a 27 km grid can show.
- Forest age: back-cast from one 2011 snapshot, most of the epoch-to-epoch change is stands growing older everywhere
  (+~39 years). That is near-uniform within a type, so it is centred out or overlaps LUH's modelled secondary-forest
  age (secma). The back-cast cannot see a pixel's state before its last disturbance, so early-epoch forest is wrong
  wherever stands were cut and regrew in between.
- The woodland hint (+0.03 at T0 1986, beating all nulls, CI including 0) is the only forest-age signal; it does not
  meet the rule.
IMPLICATION (not a decision): neither CRP county totals nor a single-snapshot stand-age back-cast adds habitat signal
beyond DESK's covariates at 27 km. Census crop types (E049) remain the only new covariate with a guild-specific
signal. A real forest-succession test would need observed forest-age history: FIA plot remeasurements, or Annual NLCD
1985+ for disturbance and regrowth.
