# E024 Does DESK trained on a SHORT span next to the target backcast better than DESK trained on a long one?
hypotheses: [N2, S3, T1]     parents: [E023, E019]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: 3 x ~75-100 min gpu + a smoke test
pre-registered: 2026-10-09, before any of these models exist.

question: E023 found that for linear surrogates, at a fixed backcast reach, LONGER training spans make the backcast
worse (later decades' temporal relationships do not describe earlier ones), and that recalibrating the existing DESK
models on their nearest decade lifts their long-reach backcast. Does DESK itself, trained only on a 20-year span next to
the target, backcast that target better than the tempho model trained on the same start year through 2025?

design: three DESK runs at HEAD (seed 0; same basis, spatial split and selection as the tempho runs; every year outside
the span withheld from the objective; anchors pinned inside the span): 1976-1995, 1986-2005, 1996-2015 (20 years each)
-- set against tempho1975 (1976-2025, 50 y), tempho1985 (1986-2025, 40 y), tempho1995 (1996-2025, 30 y). Graded by
span_reach.py (E023b) on the same held-out cells: the change of 1966-1971 (and the other pre-T0 windows) against the
first ten trained years, raw and recalibrated per span. A 3-epoch smoke test runs first (training a span that ENDS
before 2025 exercises a code path the tempho runs never did; desk_training's late anchor now takes the latest TRAINED
year).

predictions:
- raw DESK backcast of 1966-1971, short span vs the tempho model with the same T0: short > long in at least 2 of the 3
  start years (tempho raw: 0.110 at T0 1976, 0.045 at 1986, 0.057 at 1996).
- the short-span model's own recalibrated backcast >= the tempho model recalibrated on the same 20-year span (E023:
  recal:t1975 0.162, recal:t1985 0.200, recal:t1995 0.137 at span 20) in at least 2 of 3.
- the short-span models' SPATIAL fidelity (best val_kernel) is worse than the tempho models' by < 15%.
decision rule: N2 holds for DESK (2 of 3) -> the production-relevant lever is how the earliest BBS decades are weighted in
learning DESK's temporal behaviour: design a time-weighted DESK (all years; loss weight or a dedicated early-period
term) judged on a held-out early decade. Short span no better -> the surrogate result does not carry to DESK; DESK's
temporal behaviour is set by something other than where its training mass sits (look at T1/T3).
---- (appended after the runs) ----
SMOKE TEST LESSON (2026-10-09): desk_training never withholds its label year (2025: the eBird-based metric loss lives
there and the training window ends there; desk.label_year), so a "1976-1995" DESK run actually trains on 1976-1995 PLUS
2025. The three span runs were cancelled before doing real work; a DESK span that truly ends early would need a DESK
redesign. What is feasible: "span + 2025", i.e. removing the middle decades.
Surrogate analog (span_reach.py --plus-year 2025, development run, trend / cov+trend, backcast of 1966-71): T0 1996:
spans 10/20/30 (+2025) 0.079 / 0.089 / 0.063 and 0.083 / 0.128 / 0.094 (without 2025: 0.177 / 0.104 / 0.063); T0 1986:
spans 10-40 (+2025) 0.111 / 0.161 / 0.121 / 0.084; T0 1976: 0.152 / 0.188 / 0.182 / 0.130 / 0.094. Including the far
anchor removes most of the short-span advantage, but 20-year spans (+2025) still beat the full span by +0.03 to +0.09.
REVISED DESIGN AND PREDICTIONS (pre-registered before the re-run): the same three runs, honestly named "span+2025"
(1976-1995+2025, 1986-2005+2025, 1996-2015+2025), seed 0, compared with the tempho models at the same T0 -- the archived
ones and the HEAD seeds of E022 (tempho1995 / tempho1985), which give the seed spread.
- raw DESK backcast of 1966-71: span+2025 > the same-T0 tempho model in at least 2 of 3 start years, by more than the
  E022 seed spread where it is measured;
- surrogate-predicted size of the gain +0.03 to +0.09 in corr; a gain within the seed spread is "not distinguishable".
decision rule as before: holds -> a time-weighted DESK (all years, early decades up-weighted) is the production lever to
build and test; fails -> the surrogates' non-stationarity does not carry to DESK's learned mapping.
