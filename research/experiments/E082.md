# E082 Can per-species calibration of CBC counts close the winter-breeding gap that harms sedentary species?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-16, before running)
hypotheses: [season link (G11), CBC]     parents: [E066c, E079, E081]     cost: ~15 min cpu
why: E081 -- any CBC supervision (presence or Ruzicka) harms breeding-season change of out-of-community sedentary
species where cover changed most; likely because winter and breeding similarity disagree on change (~0.5, E066c).
The user (2026-10-16): more nuanced calibration might remove the harm. Part of the disagreement could be species
WEIGHTING (CBC counts flocking / feeder species in large numbers) rather than ecology. If per-species calibration of
CBC counts to the BBS scale raises winter-breeding agreement on change a lot, a calibrated CBC term deserves a retrain.
design: E066c's T1 (e066_similarity_links.py t1; standardized CBC; ABBA; disattenuated), --calibrate:
  none      no calibration (baseline on the same split)
  scale     one multiplier per species = mean BBS / mean CBC over the calibration cell-years
  quantile  per-species quantile mapping of CBC values onto the BBS distribution (mid-ranks for ties)
Calibration fitted on a random HALF of the co-located cells (seed 82), scored on the OTHER half only (all methods on the
same evaluation cells). Species: SEDENTARY (AVONET 1; primary) and residents (1-2).
reading (sedentary, TEMPORAL disattenuated agreement, evaluation half):
- CALIBRATION CLOSES A SUBSTANTIAL PART of the gap if scale or quantile is >= 0.15 above none -> a retrain with
  calibrated CBC counts (E082b, E081's design and rule) is warranted.
- NO MEANINGFUL EFFECT if both are < 0.05 above none -> the gap is not species weighting; calibration is unlikely to
  remove E081's harm.
- In between: report (a retrain is optional).
Spatial agreement reported. Values read only where both replicate Spearmans are >= 0.3.

READING (pre-registered): NO MEANINGFUL EFFECT -- per-species scale (+0.011) and quantile mapping (+0.014) both raise
sedentary agreement on change by < 0.05; quantile mapping LOWERS it for residents (-0.08) and spatial agreement falls
slightly under both. A calibrated-CBC retrain is not warranted by the rule.
WHY (likely). The winter-breeding gap is not in how much each species weighs (its overall level or count distribution)
-- removing those changes almost nothing -- but in WHERE and WHEN species are relatively common in each season: the
relative pattern, which calibration keeps by design. That is the kind of difference real seasonal ecology (E079: worst
in built-up cells) or survey-ground mismatch produces. So E081's harm is unlikely to be removed by calibration.
IMPLICATION for the shared-trunk design (user, 2026-10-16): since calibration cannot reconcile the seasons, a design that
lets each season keep its own structure (separate heads on a shared trunk, a tied basis with a learned season offset)
is the remaining way to use CBC without pulling the breeding head toward winter structure; the anchor would then measure
a real seasonal difference, not a nuisance.
