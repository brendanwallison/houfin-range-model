# Unified working synthesis — House Finch fits, 2026-11-01

**Later correction:** the morning preference is not a completed whole-configuration ranking. The regional audit shows materially worse Plains Allee-dead coverage with native departure, and the proposed later established-state initialization remains untested on the current configuration. See CONFIGURATION_CHECKLIST_CORRECTION_20261101.md for the complete P1–P15 reassessment and REGIONAL_ESTABLISHMENT_20261101.json for all cached regional measurements.

This synthesis combines the historical structural ladder, dispersal experiments, auxiliary-data pilots, the first overnight comparisons and the full stored-run audit. It distinguishes measured results from working causal explanations. The evidence snapshot is the morning of November 1; ongoing arms are not treated as completed. The audit covers 63 accepted real-data GRID54 fits, with detailed demographic replays for 31, front replays for 19 and fixed-point precision replays for four. Earlier non-GRID54 work enters through its recorded experiments, not a new replay of every archive.

The leading explanation is **a mismatch between the model's joint requirements for native persistence and slow colonization, with abundance data allowing several compensating demographic explanations**. The fitted model can trade reproduction, adult and juvenile survival, movement, establishment, founders and habitat history. Float64 optimization can exploit these trades more thoroughly. There is no evidence that one numerical precision change or one dispersal parameter explains the whole syndrome.

That interpretation yields a practical target: retain the biologically defensible simplifications already made, separate the few remaining mechanisms that have distinct spatial or temporal signatures, and constrain net growth and movement with observations that measure different life-cycle components. A successful configuration should improve several concerns without recreating others or requiring many extra fields. It need not remove genuine sinks, real habitat change or all uncertainty.

## 1. Low-density growth, establishment and local replacement are being traded against one another

**Measured:** changing only the Allee law on the CBC+NestWatch yearling base changes modern model-population-weighted median adult survival from .399–.400 to .560–.570 and the resident juvenile/adult survival ratio from .995 to .465–.479. The latter reproduces in two seeds. Realized fecundity at nest locations stays near 3.57–3.58, and held-out predictive scores change little. However, whole-domain positive modern survey records with K below .05 route-count units increase from .165% in the relative-law seed-41 reference to 1.325% in absolute-law seed 41. Positive-record Allee-dead share rises from zero to 1.012%.

**Working explanation:** the absolute-density establishment threshold supplies a constraint that the relative-to-K law does not supply in the same way. The optimizer can therefore support a different survival/growth/establishment combination with nearly the same count predictions. That makes the survival change evidence that the structural assumption matters; it does not make the new survival estimates independently verified. Under relative Allee, reducing K also lowers the physical-density threshold corresponding to a given N/K; that coupling is a plausible contributor to the alternative demographic solution, not a mechanism isolated by the current fits.

**Candidate solution:** use absolute mate-finding density as the preferred structural hypothesis, retain the relative law as a diagnostic control, and inspect K and establishment at observed locations rather than judging land minima. Test establishment versus post-establishment reproduction separately. Do not repair K with an arbitrary lower-bound penalty and declare the problem solved: that may move the same pressure back into survival or habitat history.

## 2. The model must both sustain native populations and reproduce a much slower invasion

**Measured:** older learned-kernel fits moved roughly 60% of juvenile mass beyond 483 km, with mean distance about 625 km, and reduced fecundity without repairing frozen-habitat front speed. Reducing juvenile kernel means to 110/200 km increased fitted fecundity and founders and worsened the recorded paired count scores. In current absolute-law candidates, fitted-front median error at the saved 10% threshold remains about three years early; frozen-habitat release-only replay remains about 36–38 years early. These timing numbers belong to this diagnostic protocol and should not be pooled directly with earlier threshold definitions.

**Working explanation:** movement is doing several jobs: colonizing the introduced range, redistributing abundance, and sustaining populations in modeled native sink habitat. A global shorter kernel or added travel mortality interferes with all three, so free reproduction and founders compensate. A kernel that mixes permanent dispersal and seasonal movements could additionally make colonization too efficient. Season-filtered BBL observations support investigating that possibility, but encounters are selected by reporting/recapture opportunity and are not an unbiased movement sample.

**Candidate solution:** distinguish native retention from travel/colonization rather than changing every movement rate together. Retest breeding-to-breeding movement kernels once demographic compensators are constrained; include the stayer mass, season/age selection, multiple elapsed years and encounter opportunity. The earlier short-kernel experiments show that shortening alone is inadequate, not that a shorter permanent-dispersal kernel is biologically excluded. A full seasonal state model remains a later option; it is not required to perform the current matched movement tests.

## 3. Native departure is a useful, parsimonious mechanism, with an establishment tradeoff

**Measured:** under absolute Allee, adding one native-departure scalar reduces western fitted-population sink share from about 10.3–10.4% to 1.6–2.2%. In the independent weighting by positive western survey records, the seed-41 absolute reference has 24.0% in fundamental sinks, versus 7.0% and 3.1% in the two native-departure fits. Thus the improvement is not solely a consequence of reweighting by fitted abundance. Adult survival stays .555–.577 and the juvenile/adult ratio .437–.479. Paired spatial-score gains relative to the fixed absolute-law parent are +.0164 [+.0030,+.0286] and +.0096 [-.0115,+.0296] (90% intervals).

The cost is real: positive-record Allee-dead shares increase to 1.85–4.58%, realized fecundity at nests rises to 3.80–4.00, and the frozen release front remains early. Allee-dead cells with observed birds are an establishment diagnostic, not a logical impossibility: immigration and averaging require examination. Sink classification alone also does not measure the actual required immigration flux.

**Working explanation:** a geographically distinct native-retention effect gives the likelihood a signature that a continent-wide mortality coefficient lacks. The fit uses it to reduce dependence on native-range replacement by immigrants. Some pressure then moves into establishment and reproduction.

**Candidate solution:** keep absolute Allee with native departure as a leading candidate, alongside the simpler absolute-law control. Adoption depends on whether its sink improvement survives flux, establishment, auxiliary-assumption and habitat-basis checks. The benefit is specific; it is not an overall pathology clearance.

## 4. Auxiliary observations help, but their annual life-cycle mapping can still determine the answer

**Measured:** yearling breeding addresses the known delayed-first-breeding structure that accounted for much of the older extreme raw Fmax. NestWatch supplies information on realized reproduction rather than directly equating nest output with Fmax. Yet, under absolute Allee, changing fixed attempts per female from 2.5 to 3.125 changes adult survival to .379 and the resident juvenile/adult ratio to .980, with 68.3% of model population above the .95 ratio threshold. At 2.0 attempts those medians are .534 and .641. Predictive-score differences are small relative to their paired uncertainty. Founder bracketing moves the demographic solution less dramatically in the completed absolute-law comparisons.

**Working explanation:** per-attempt nesting measurements do not by themselves identify annual offspring production per breeder. Attempts, replacement nests, breeding participation, sex ratio, census timing and survival exposure mediate the conversion. A precisely fixed conversion can constrain the wrong combination of demographic parameters. That does not mean the observations are unhelpful; it means their information and their structural assumptions must be separated.

**Candidate solution:** retain the joint nest information, but externally constrain the annual conversion where possible, and carry its uncertainty with a small, explicit nuisance model. Freely fitting attempts from the same per-attempt nest data would simply introduce another fecundity confound. Compare fixed-attempt brackets and reporting/substrate sensitivities. Add adult BBL survival through a likelihood with reporting, censoring and movement represented. The coarse adult pilot (.488 West, .514 central-East, .589 East; Plains sparse) supports useful adult information, not a tight continental survival prior or juvenile Q estimate. Avoid multiplying Sj by Q: Q replaces Sj on the mover branch. Keep the CBC founder band as a derived uncertain anchor unless the original CBC detections/nondetections are modeled jointly; do not reuse its source records as independent validation.

## 5. Historical habitat change is partly an inference target and partly a potential timing compensator

**Measured:** early eastern growth and modern growth differ substantially across current configurations. On the current detailed eastern regional land summary, the relative CBC+nest reference changes about .162→1.792, while the absolute seed-41 reference changes about .285→1.239. Better native DESK inputs and independent v2 training change downstream scores and demographic solutions; v2 absolute fits differ across seeds. However, no current simple dispersal change has repaired the early frozen release-only front. The historical static-habitat outside-truth experiment did not automatically produce habitat/growth inflation. The sharper slow-front experiment initially encountered an observation-likelihood divergence; its corrected refined result must be collected before making a strong general claim from that branch.

**Working explanation:** real habitat change and compensation for imperfect dynamics may coexist. Neither 'all change is real habitat' nor 'the GP inevitably invents change' follows from the current evidence. Shared-field space-for-time transfer can also be imperfect even when modern community similarity is predicted well. If survival, fecundity and K must all follow the same habitat ordering, a locally inadequate response shape could force K to express absence that another process should explain. That is a working hypothesis, not a demonstrated reason to restore several free fields.

**Candidate solution:** preserve frozen, independently evaluated community inputs and compare mechanisms across v1/v2. Use early CBC surveyed detections and nondetections to inform the pre-BBS spread history with uncertain detection and winter/breeding correspondence. Validate the observation design first. If residuals implicate the shared response itself, test one targeted response/structure change against the constrained baseline; do not immediately add independent temporal demographic fields or shrink all historical change toward zero.

## 6. Some historical escape routes are already constrained; remaining scale and observation issues must be separated

The current candidate uses yearling breeding, one shared demographic habitat field, fixed observer scale, fixed band weights, no settlement regathering and no free decoupled Q field. These restrictions remove particular historical routes to P10–P13. They do not prove biologically correct realized movement or survival. The current absolute and native-departure fits also have saved K-trend weights exactly zero and multiplier one: the generic extra K-trend term is not causing their present historical-growth contrast. DESK-driven historical variation remains.

Amplitude/coefficient-norm compensation (P14) is still possible; stored parameter norms are now available for all 63 real-data fits, but effective field magnitude and prior-covariance comparisons are not yet consistently interpreted. The old free route-effect-scale explosion should not be presented as an active property of candidates with fixed scale. Conversely, observation concentration can matter: the slow static-truth experiment's diverging density-power concentration was a demonstrated observation-model failure, specific to that test design, rather than a demonstrated failure of real-data habitat dynamics.

**Candidate solution:** audit effective field strength, coefficient norms and observation residuals together. Where hierarchy/parameterization creates redundant MAP freedom, test a better-identified or marginalized representation; do not fix an amplitude alone and ignore a growing coefficient norm. Expand the observation model only for a demonstrated effort/detection signature, and keep observation corrections from becoming an unrestricted substitute for population dynamics.

## 7. Separate numerical validity, experiment validity and biological identification

Four fixed-point float32/float64 comparisons show zero median arrival shift at the saved 10% threshold and no common-arrived cells shifted by five or more years. This weakens precision alone as the explanation of these particular fitted histories. It does not show that optimizers find the same basin or that rare establishment transitions are numerically harmless in every configuration. Float64 can simply find biologically poor points with better objective values along weakly constrained directions.

Two experiment-contract problems require explicit handling. First, segmented native path features passed a median-only reproduction check but have localized discrepancies and about 2% sampled relative L2 mismatch; a float64 probe did not resolve them. All eight new native compounding fits remain held. Second, the audited NestWatch null/recovery simulations recompute occupied-habitat centering after replacing counts. At the recorded generating parameters, the fitted centering does not reproduce the saved generating means; restoring the original center reproduces them. Thus at least these synthetic recovery objectives do not test exactly the recorded planted model. Their exact recovery/gate scores cannot be used as clean quantitative certificates of identification or its absence until the contract is repaired. This qualification must not be generalized automatically to every older simulation without auditing it.

**Candidate solution:** preserve numerical/source/input validation, fix the generating/centering contract, and then rerun targeted recovery and cross-compensation tests cumulatively with the auxiliary observations. Keep the real-data evidence of compensation: the matched Allee, attempts, founder and seed tests already establish that similar count prediction can accompany different demographic explanations. Incomplete identification is a reason to improve the data/design and retain structural uncertainty, not a veto on a biologically justified mechanism.

## Working configuration and decisive comparisons

The most defensible current small candidate set is:

1. Yearling breeding + shared habitat field + absolute Allee + real nest likelihood + uncertain CBC founder anchor.
2. The same with one native-departure scalar.
3. Relative-Allee controls, auxiliary-conversion brackets and v2 versions for diagnosis, rather than additional competing fields by default.

The strongest candidate intervention is native retention, with the absolute-law control essential for measuring its costs. Annual nest conversion and native-range survival are the highest-priority remaining constraints. The next decisive comparisons are observed/immigration-weighted replacement deficits, establishment in observed-positive cells, effective mover/stayer survival, breeding-to-breeding displacement, and early detection-aware colonization history. Profile or sample the joint weak parameter combinations after repairing experiment contracts; two MAP seeds and paired predictive intervals are not parameter uncertainty intervals.

Success would mean jointly reducing ceiling survival, demographic distortion, implausible movement/immigration demands, and dependence on artificial habitat or K gating, with acceptable prediction and fewer mechanisms. Tradeoffs must remain explicit. We should neither select solely by predictive score nor require every ecological cell to be a source.
