# E058 Does the eBird trend product agree with BBS on CHANGE, cell by cell? (pre-registered 2026-10-12 ~11:05,
# before any statistic was computed)
hypotheses: [eBird-places / eBird-trend (new), T5, M3]     parents: [E057, E031, E034, b6e451d]     eval_version: pre-v1
cost: ~15-30 min cpu
why: the user (2026-10-12) asked whether to pre-train or jointly train DESK on eBird, first on the Status & Trends
trend product and then on raw data. Any TEMPORAL use of the trend product -- as supervision, or as an evaluation of
DESK's 2012-22 change at the ~8,500 land cells BBS never surveys -- rests on it agreeing with real change. August's test
(b6e451d) measured agreement on LEVELS only (median per-species corr 0.405 over paired cell-years). Agreement on
change was never measured.
data: eBird S&T trends, version year 2022 (window 2012-2022), downloaded 2026-10-12 for the 96 reference + 273 dev
species only (never the sealed half; House Finch excluded by construction) to /mnt/e/Datasets/houfin/external/
ebird_trends_2022: 301 of 302 available products (flsjay failed the downloader's size check). Only "breeding" and
"resident" season products are compared with breeding-season BBS counts; nonbreeding and migration products are
skipped and counted.
design: research/exp/ebird_change_agree.py on the desk_tempho_1995_s1 cache (its keys, counts, route-years; no DESK
output is read). Per species and 27 km cell with >= 2 surveyed years per ABBA half in both BBS windows (2010-2014 and
2019-2024, 2020 unsurveyed; centre gap ~9.5 y, M7): BBS change in cross halves (late A - early B, late B - early A), so
the noise-free change variance is their covariance (M2). eBird prediction: gap x log(1 + abd_ppy/100), gridded by the
production rasterizer (preprocess/ebird_trend.rasterize_parquet). Statistics pooled over species, centred (place-
specific) and uncentred (M4), with block-bootstrap CIs (6x6 cells):
- E: noise-free corr of eBird with BBS change, and k (slope of BBS change on eBird, M1);
- R: the same for the regional BBS field (leave-one-out 300 km Gaussian average of OTHER cells' change) -- what the
  observed neighbours' trend predicts, the yardstick for how much cell-level change is predictable at all;
- E|R: eBird residualized on R per species -- eBird information beyond the neighbours' trend;
- a moved-eBird null (M19): the eBird field shifted (20-70 cells) or mirrored, 8 moves, no refitting;
- by observer continuity between the windows (unchanged / partial / replaced, M3/M18) and prevalence tier (M14);
- species-level: noise-free corr across species of mean change;
- LEVEL agreement (eBird abd vs BBS late-window level, single measurements, not noise-free) for comparison with 0.405.
considerations: M2/M3 (cross halves remove survey noise, not observers; report the observer strata), M4 (centred and
uncentred), M7 (27 km cells, ~9.5-year gap), M13 (report CI bounds; this is one decade, one product version), M14
(tiers), M19 (spatially structured null), M17-corrected (agreement with a trend says nothing about cause), M29 (judge a
source by what its curators trust: S&T trends are a model product with its own covariates and smoothing, so agreement
can be part shared smoothness -- hence R and the moved null).
predictions (written before any statistic):
- species-level (continental) agreement is clearly positive (corr_nf >= 0.3): range-wide trends from the two surveys
  are known to agree moderately.
- cell-level, centred: E lower than species-level; my point guess 0.10-0.25. E below R (the BBS neighbours' trend is
  measured by the same survey, so it shares BBS's observer and route conventions).
- E|R small (<= 0.10): most of whatever eBird gets right is the regional trend.
- E exceeds the moved-eBird null's max (the agreement is specific to where eBird says change happened).
- E higher in observer-unchanged cells than replaced (turnover adds change nobody predicts, as E034b).
decision rule (centred, all cells; "CI > 0" = bootstrap lower bound above 0):
- USABLE FOR TIME: E >= +0.20 with CI > 0, E > moved-null max, and E|R >= +0.05 with CI > 0 -> the trend product carries
  place-level 2012-22 change consistent with BBS, beyond the neighbours' trend. It can serve as an EVALUATION of DESK's
  modern change at non-BBS cells, and is a candidate temporal-supervision signal for the modern decade only (its
  relevance to a 1902-1939 backcast is still bounded by N2/E056: near-target data mattered only as trend).
- REGIONAL ONLY: E >= +0.10 with CI > 0 but E|R CI includes 0 -> it carries the regional trend and no more; usable as
  regional context, not as place-level supervision.
- NOT USABLE FOR TIME: E < +0.10 or CI includes 0, or E <= moved-null max -> eBird's value for DESK is spatial only
  (places), which E057 sizes.
- Report all strata and tiers regardless.
