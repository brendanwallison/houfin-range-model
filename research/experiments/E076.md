WHY (likely, now with direct support). The larger gain where cover changed most is the term REMOVING DESK's larger
over-movement there: the control backcast is twice as wrong in high cover-change cells (-0.18 vs -0.08), and with the
term the backcast is equally good everywhere (+0.13-0.14). The term does not make high-change places better than the
rest; it removes the excess error DESK makes where covariates (cover) changed most -- the place where E073 found every
representation over-predicts. The correction is learned from OTHER places (withheld = in-sample), so it is a general
property of the covariate -> z mapping, not memorized lists.
IMPLICATION (production, reported): CBC presence supervision corrects DESK's excess backcast response to large land-cover
change, transferring to CBC places it never saw -- for WINTER composition. Whether the corrected backcast is also better
for breeding-season targets there is not shown (E069b: sedentary BBS change unaffected overall); the season link (E066c)
remains the caveat.
