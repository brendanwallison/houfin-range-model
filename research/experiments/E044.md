- unique_T: change the covariates do not carry. Candidates are species/community dynamics, observer drift, OR habitat

## E044d Within a type, did places whose land use changed MORE also change more in birds? (pre-registered 2026-10-11

design: research/exp/type_dose.py on desk_tempho_1995_s1 and desk_tempho_1985_s1. No DESK involvement; the seeds share

- Families: all, land (classes + management + HYDE + BUI), luh_class, luh_mgmt, climate.

  diagnostic of a relationship, not a model backcast.
