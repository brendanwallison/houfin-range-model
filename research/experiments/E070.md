# E070 Which early-CBC route survives REALISTIC early damage, place included, for SEDENTARY species?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-14, before any fit; 20-step code smokes of the new model options only)
hypotheses: [CBC (bridge / complement)]     parents: [E065, E066b, E068c, E069a/a2, plans/raw_surveys.md section 8]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: 28 fits x ~30 min cpu (4 parallel, ~3.5 h) + ~5 min
why: the user (2026-10-13/14) asked whether, once the species-wide decline is understood (E068c: a circle-mix effect),
abundance / Ruzicka can be used directly instead of presence only, and asked that the design follow the project's
SEDENTARY focus. E065's known-truth test had two gaps: each degraded copy used ONE mechanism (thinning OR omission),
while real early counts sit between them (E065 F), and its degraded counts were all in MODERN circles, so it could not
see the place problem E068c found. Its absolute bias bar also suited counts but not detection (E065 skeptic).
copies (research/exp/cbc_pseudo_early2.py; built, calibration recorded in pseudo_early2.json): 1966-1985 counts
degraded by BOTH mechanisms at once -- records omitted (median keep q 0.46) and individuals thinned (median pi 0.21),
calibrated to the real early data: median non-zero count ratio 0.43 (target 0.45, E065 F) and median benchmark
completeness 0.26 (1920-39); party-hours removed and party-distance removed for 40% of those counts (as 1920-39);
PLACE: circles made early-only (all counts after 1985 removed) until they hold 60% of the window's counts (real: 54-66%
of pre-1940 counts sit in circles without >= 5 modern counts) -- RICH copy: circles chosen in proportion to the rank
of their later mean list length (as early circles near population centres; the inflation E068c found); RANDOM copy:
uniformly. Field counters untouched.
species: every RESIDENT community or dev species CBC carries after name harmonization (cbc_prepare --species-set
residents; 155 species, 59 of them reference; 19 split-affected dropped; sealed species and the House Finch are outside
the pool by construction). PRIMARY stratum SEDENTARY (AVONET 1), secondary RESIDENTS (1-2). Dev species are used here
only to validate an index method; nothing from this experiment reaches DESK.
variants (cbc_effort_model.py): C1 counts, counters effort (E064 model); C2 = C1 + party-distance (log, with a
missing-distance offset); P1 detection model + benchmark completeness (E065 V3); P2 = P1 + party-distance.
circle handling: RIDGE (circle effects shrink to 0, as before); NEIGHBOUR (shrink toward the mean of the 5 nearest
circles within 150 km, sd 1, plus the ridge -- so an early-only circle in a rich area is read as rich); PERSISTENT
(only circles with >= 5 counts in 1966-85 AND in 2005-24).
truth: each variant's RIDGE fit to the real data (all circles; completeness referenced from 1966). Degraded fits
reference completeness from 1986.
evaluation (research/exp/e070_eval.py; fixed now), on the change 1966-85 -> 2005-24:
  SPECIES ROUTE: rel_bias = median |species bias| / sd(true species change); species corr; stratum corr (centred).
  RUZICKA ROUTE: per stratum, Ruzicka (log1p of the window-mean indices, stratum's species) between the windows;
    degraded vs truth across strata: Spearman, rel_bias = median |diff| / sd(true similarity).
rule (sedentary stratum; a variant x handling PASSES a route only if it passes in BOTH copies):
  species route: rel_bias <= 0.35 AND species corr >= 0.8 AND stratum corr >= 0.6.
  Ruzicka route: Spearman >= 0.7 AND rel_bias <= 0.35.
  SENSITIVITY: C1-ridge must FAIL the species route in the RICH copy; if it passes, the copies are not hard enough and
  E070 is reported as uninformative.
decision: a COUNT variant (C1/C2) passing both routes with some handling -> abundance and model-standardized Ruzicka
are usable for early CBC with that handling (presence-only becomes a fallback); only P1/P2 pass -> early CBC enters as
detection indices (or as E069's within-list presences); nothing passes -> no early index; the within-list presence
route (E069), immune to place and effort, is the only one left. Among passing handlings, NEIGHBOUR is preferred over
PERSISTENT when both pass (it keeps the data).
limits: the copies are modern circles with modern observers; the 1901-1919 level (completeness 0.14) is not tested;
real turnover read as incompleteness (M30) is not reproduced; the unexplained rare-vs-common pattern (E068c) is not
modelled; the season link (winter vs breeding change 0.51 for sedentary species, E066c) bounds what any route gives a
breeding-season representation.
considerations: M28 (one scale-free yardstick for count, detection and similarity routes), M29, M30, G11 (sedentary
primary; user 2026-10-14), M2/M13 (point values with n; a skeptic round before any finding), no House Finch, sealed
never read.

READING (pre-registered): SENSITIVITY met (C1-ridge fails in the rich copy: 0.82 / 0.75). ONLY THE DETECTION VARIANTS
(P1, P2) PASS, on the species route, with the ridge circle handling, in both copies -> early CBC enters as DETECTION
indices (or as E069's within-list presences); abundance indices and model-standardized Ruzicka similarity do not
survive realistic early damage. No route passes the Ruzicka bar.
WHY (likely).
- Counts fail because real early damage shrinks count SIZE (the copies' non-zero counts fall to ~0.43 of undegraded)
  and no available effort measure recovers it: counters are untouched by construction, and distance (C2) recovers about
  a third of the bias (rel 0.82 -> 0.57) but not the ranking (corr 0.77-0.79). Detection models discard count size and
  model completeness, so they keep the species ranking (corr 0.95-0.96). Distance adds nothing to detection (P2 = P1).
- The Ruzicka route on count indices shows the E066b signature: early communities read as LESS similar to modern ones
  (shift -0.08 to -0.13), a size gap read as dissimilarity. On detection indices the shift is small (+0.02) but the
  stratum-level similarity is noisy relative to its spread (rel_bias ~0.5); the neighbour prior cuts that to ~0.4.
- PLACE: the rich and random copies give nearly the same numbers, and ridge beats the neighbour prior on the species
  route; persistent-circle restriction is worst everywhere (it discards 60% of the window's counts). So in these copies
  the place manipulation costs little -- weaker than E068c's real-data effect (-0.36 -> -0.05), which means the copies
  under-represent it (likely: real early circles differ from later ones in more than list richness). The handling
  choice for real early data therefore stays open; E068c's persistent-circle check remains the real-data guard.
LIMITS (as pre-registered): modern circles and observers; 1901-1919 level (0.14) untested; real turnover read as
incompleteness (M30) not reproduced; season link (sedentary 0.51) bounds what any route gives a breeding-season z.
IMPLICATION (production, reported): together with E069b, the PRESENCE route is the one that holds for early CBC --
as detection indices per stratum (E070) or as a within-list presence term in DESK (E069b). Abundance and Ruzicka on
early CBC should not be used without an effort measure early counts do not have.
