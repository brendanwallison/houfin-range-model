# E068 Why do real early CBC counts show species DECLINING out of 1910-39, rare species most?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-13 ~19:45, before running; no smoke beyond the E065 code)
hypotheses: [CBC (bridge / complement)]     parents: [E065 (C), E066]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: 2 fits x ~25 min cpu + ~3 min
why: E065's C check found, in every variant, most species declining from 1910-39 to 1950-65 (V0 median -0.36, 23%
rising; V3 -0.15, 16%), rarer species declining more (Spearman with modern prevalence +0.56 to +0.67). Incomplete
early lists predict the opposite (a spurious rise, rare species most), which the pseudo-early copies do produce (V0
+0.67 under thinning). Until this is understood, no early-era CBC index is used (E065 reading). Two candidates are
cheap to test; the user (2026-10-13) asked to keep testing.
(a) EFFORT EXTRAPOLATION -- early counts have median 2 field counters (modern 18); the effort term p[s] * log(E / mean E)
    is extrapolated far below the data's centre, and the index is read at MEAN effort. If p[s] is larger for rare
    species, their early indices are pushed up more -> a larger apparent decline afterwards.
    Test: per fit, Spearman of species change with p[s], and partial correlations (rank residuals) of change with p
    given prevalence and with prevalence given p; plus REFITS with one fixed exponent for every species (--p-fixed: V0
    at 0.73, E064's counters median; V3 at 0.32, its own median), where (a) cannot act.
(b) SELECTIVE (notable-first) REPORTING -- early lists naming the interesting species and skipping common ones.
    Test: in circles counted both before 1940 and from 1966, per list-length bin (5-14, 15-24, 25-39 species), each
    species' inclusion rate on early vs modern lists of the same length (modern weighted to the early circle mix);
    Spearman of log(early/modern) with modern prevalence. Notable-first reporting predicts rare species over-included,
    most strongly on SHORT lists; a real decline of rare species predicts it in every bin alike.
reading (fixed now; research/exp/e068_early_decline.py):
- (a) SUPPORTED if in V0 |Spearman(change, p)| >= 0.5 AND the fixed-p V0 refit cuts Spearman(change, prevalence) by at
  least half (<= +0.33) AND moves the median change toward 0 by >= 0.15. Partly: one of the two refit conditions.
- (b) SUPPORTED if Spearman(log ratio, prevalence) <= -0.3 on the shortest bin AND at least 0.2 more negative there than
  on the longest bin. If all bins are within 0.1 of each other and <= -0.3: a prevalence-related difference that does
  not depend on list completeness -> real change or a place effect, not reporting.
- Neither: the cause stays unknown; next candidates are where early circles were (place) and real mid-century change.
decision: (a) supported -> the effort exponent must not be extrapolated to early effort (fix or shrink p, or model
early effort per count as a latent), and E065 is re-read with that model before any early use. (b) supported -> early
lists are presence-biased toward notable species: absences AND relative inclusion are unreliable, so early CBC can enter
only as presences conditioned within list (the user's presence-only route, E069 design) or not at all.
considerations: M29 (curators' limits), M30 (site-relative completeness), M2/M13 (point values with n; skeptic round),
no House Finch (not in the species set), sealed species never read.
