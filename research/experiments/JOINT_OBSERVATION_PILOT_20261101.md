## Next dependency — shared census/readout connection

We are not waiting for the US extraction to begin population integration. CBC and Canadian breeding/winter source pilots are already available; US full-source preparation continues independently. The next controlled comparison is the existing annual population/ecological readout versus the simple source baselines, followed by an isolated joint breeding-observation fit.

The census contract was checked directly in frozen d4435fb: BBS breeding observations use previous-year total density (seeded initial population for year zero) plus fitted weight times current-year juveniles, scaled to route-count units and floored at 1e-6 before observer effects. Therefore simply using end-of-year total simulated density for eBird would change the observed quantity. The new census.py adapter reproduces the frozen statements exactly in tests. export_census.py now replays the accepted absolute-Allee baseline on CPU, exports adult/young/total pools and the reference breeding readout, and requires numerical parity with all saved pre-observer BBS predictions, including pseudozero years. This verifies implementation fidelity; the biological/source-specific visibility interpretation still requires explicit comparison. BBS observer multipliers will not be transferred to eBird.

Then refit the annual breeding model with eBird in isolation. CBC/winter-eBird require the seasonal mapping and date/exposure contract before being allowed to constrain breeding establishment.
