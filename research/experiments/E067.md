# E067 Community composition vs SEDENTARY targets: do migrants in the training community help or hurt?
hypotheses: [R5, R6, E2]     parents: [E059g, E059h, E039g]     eval_version: pre-v1
cost: ~5 min cpu + ~1.5 h gpu (10 species-target scorings)
pre-registered: 2026-10-13 19:05, before any surrogate is scored. The user, after E059g: sedentary species may be
easier because the community selection favoured sedentary species, or because they are inherently easier. If
sedentary species are the target (the House Finch is sedentary), does migratory information in the training
community help, or drag the representation away from what we care about?
known before this experiment:
- E059g: sedentary out-of-community species are not easier for the baselines (strong base 0.287 / 0.471 vs
  migratory 0.331 / 0.457), but DESK ties the strong base for them only.
- The community holds 46 sedentary, 23 partial and 27 full migrants.
design (research/exp/composition_rrr.py):
- Half B of the dev species, split at random within each migration class (seed 7), is never trained on: 137 species,
  31 sedentary / 22 partial / 76 migratory. Half A may join a training set.
- A reduced-rank ridge surrogate of DESK per training set: standardized input-EMA covariates -> log1p abundance of the
  training species, rank 24, fitted on training cells x trained years.
  - C96: the 96.
  - Cres: the community's sedentary + partial species (69).
  - C96_migA: the 96 + half A's migrants (76).
  - C96_A: the 96 + all of half A (136).
  - Cres_resA: the community residents + half A's residents (52).
- Scored with the yardstick (fair_rivals --target dev --z-keys-only --save-species; within readout, strong base,
  pooled level vs linear). Both T0s, selected-state analogue (the surrogate has no epochs).
- Strata from species_strata --species-filter (half B only), with PAIRED contrasts vs C96 (same held-out cells and
  draws).
- DESK seed 1 (E059h runs) on the same B species gives the surrogate's realism check.
PRIMARY: change in the SEDENTARY B stratum (n = 31). Secondary: residents (sedentary + partial, n = 53), all B, level.
RULE (change, sedentary B):
- MIGRANTS HURT SEDENTARY TARGETS: Cres - C96 >= +0.02 with CI > 0 at both T0s, OR C96_migA - C96 <= -0.02 with
  CI < 0 at both.
- MIGRANTS HELP: C96_migA - C96 >= +0.02 with CI > 0 at both, OR Cres - C96 <= -0.02 with CI < 0 at both.
- BROADER HELPS: C96_A - C96 >= +0.02 with CI > 0 at both; and, for residents only, Cres_resA - Cres >= +0.02.
- Otherwise: NO DETECTABLE COMPOSITION EFFECT for sedentary targets (|diff| < 0.02 or CIs include 0).
- REALISM: if the C96 surrogate differs from DESK seed 1 by > 0.05 on sedentary B change, any composition effect
  needs a DESK retrain before it counts.
PREDICTIONS: no single direction is favoured.
- If composition explains E059g, Cres > C96 > C96_migA for sedentary targets.
- If biology explains it, the sets are within ~0.02 of each other.
considerations: M28 (one yardstick, paired); the user's sedentary-target premise (E059h makes it the primary stratum).
