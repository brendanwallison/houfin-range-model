# K floor as a component of combined models

**Execution update:** the combinations are now implemented and18 new matched fits are published on TACC. See [KFLOOR_COMBINATION_EXECUTION_20261102.md](KFLOOR_COMBINATION_EXECUTION_20261102.md). The original design reasoning below is retained; this update supersedes its final unqueued-status sentence.

The four standalone floor fits are the first calibration, not the entire scientific purpose. Blocking arbitrarily small K removes an attractive way for the optimizer to preserve high potential growth while making cells persistently empty under absolute mate limitation. Re-test useful mechanisms that previously traded other improvements for that artifact. The desired outcome is a different joint compromise, not a guarantee that every original benefit survives or every cell becomes viable.

## Prioritized combinations

| Priority | Combination | Historical evidence worth preserving | What the floor could reveal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Floor x crowding power1/2, under gradual Veit and rational age-specific departure | Current curvature/departure hypotheses directly target reproduction and successful exchange. These remain untested for biological improvement. | Whether reducing the unused reproductive ceiling survives when the fit cannot restore sparse ranges by pushing K toward zero. An unfloored curvature result alone is not the completed hypothesis test. |
| 2 | Floor x modest process/reproductive variation | E070b lowered the reported delayed-life-cycle potential lambda from about2.04 to1.40, with nearly unchanged prediction, but increased Plains Allee-dead area from about6% to23% and shifted survival to Sa.78/Sj.11. Later54-km modest reproductive variation reduced historical lambda change by about.08 in two prediction-neutral seeds. These are distinct growth summaries, not interchangeable numerical effects. | Whether useful variation can support lower growth once persistent emptiness must be expressed through genuinely poor demographics, observation patchiness or credible exchange. Extreme survival may persist; that would identify an independent failure. Test one bounded variation term at a time. |
| 3 | Floor x limited settlement of arriving dispersers only | E074a retained the Plains below the flanks and kept Plains K higher, but left western Allee-dead area around7%, high fecundity and a predictive cost. E074c preserved higher Plains K with a weak learned fitness weight, but western Allee-dead area was around16%. | Whether local placement plus a floor can preserve the regional growth structure without making surrounding productive habitat artificially empty. Keep residents fixed; avoid perfect-information fitness equalization. Preserve the old physical search scale when porting from27 to54km, rather than blindly using the same block dimensions. |
| Secondary | Floor x native retention | Current absolute-Allee native-departure fits reduced modeled occupied-west intrinsic sink shares and raised local breeder retention, but Plains K/Allee nonviability increased from about1.27% to4.87% in the matched1940-initialization comparison. Regional contribution and regional immigration dependence did not change by comparable amounts. | A particularly direct test of whether the retained local-population benefit was traded against the tiny-K absence mechanism. Keep secondary because the user has explicitly deprioritized a separate native-departure process. |

## Designs and stopping points

First use the standalone1.25x and4x floors to assess the size of the intervention, prediction and relocation of absence pressure. For a combined test, fix the same absolute floor across its component arms. Do not select a different floor for each model solely to produce a desired sink map. Carry the other floor as a sensitivity for a promising combination.

The main next factorial should use one common fixed floor in all four combinations of crowding power1/2 and departure Veit/rational-adult20, with seeds41/42. Existing baseline floor runs supply the floored Veit/power1 controls. Three additional configuration types would require six fits once a floor is chosen. The currently queued unfloored comparison remains informative and should not be discarded or edited while running.

For the older variation hypothesis, distinguish the within-life-cycle potential-growth reduction in E070 from the smaller historical-change reduction of the54-km reproductive-deviation fits. Port the mechanisms to the current yearling/NestWatch/1951 base rather than comparing old lambda numbers directly with current ones. Read both a small bounded reproductive-deviation version and, if justified by residuals, the older abundance-process version as separate experiments; do not combine both immediately.

Use the same East/Great Plains/West fixed supports and complete scorecard throughout. In particular, monitor whether absence pressure moves from low K into broad true sinks in observed native populations, implausibly low juvenile survival, inflated reproduction, historical habitat gating, excessive dispersal loss or observation corrections. A floor can expose these alternatives, which is useful even when it does not rescue a configuration.

## Cases the floor does not particularly motivate

- Relative-Allee and no-Allee fits already eliminate the named absolute-Allee dead-cell mechanism by construction. Their remaining pathologies are informative controls, but the floor is not the missing component in those original results.
- E075c's low-lambda split-readout result already had little Allee-dead area. Its principal problems were the flattened/inverted regional contrast and extreme temporal responses. A floor alone is not a targeted remedy for those failures.
- Strict overflow has an independent failure: departure is tied to a nominal K above disease-modified equilibrium. Increasing K can make that mismatch worse. The two already queued floor-plus-overflow fits remain diagnostics, rather than the first hoped-for rescue.
- Withdrawn route-scale results, invalid gradients and unconverged fits are not rescued by a K floor. Repair their experiment contracts before interpreting biology.

No additional fits were queued by this note. The four standalone floor fits have already been restored to the front of the pending queue. This document changes the interpretation of their role and specifies the subsequent combination comparisons.
