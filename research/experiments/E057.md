# E057 Do more training PLACES help DESK in space and in time? A places learning curve (pre-registered 2026-10-12
# ~10:45, before any of these models exist)
hypotheses: [T11, T12 (over-fitting / selection noise), eBird-places (new, below)]
parents: [E054, E055a, E055f/i/k, E039n/E046]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: 4 x ~65 min gpu + ~40 min cpu
why: the user (2026-10-12) asked whether eBird pre-training or joint training should be tried now that DESK is known to
over-fit. What was rejected in August (b6e451d) was eBird as a second target in the SAME latent (BBS-eBird median
per-species corr 0.405; ~80% of eBird's spread unexplained by BBS), not pre-training or a separate head.
- DESK over-fits PLACES: held-out-place error bottoms at epochs ~80-110 while training error keeps falling; seeds
  over-fit differently (the seed ensemble helps, E055f/i). BBS supervises 3,902 of 17,209 land cells; eBird reached
  12,412 when wired in (d6dbc0c). More places is the textbook remedy for memorizing places.
- E055a: space peaks at epoch ~70 and falls while time rises to epoch 490. If more places stop the place over-fit, the
  late state's verified beyond-trend gain (+0.02-0.04, E039n/E046) might come without its ~0.025 spatial cost.
- This experiment bounds that payoff with BBS alone, before any eBird plumbing: REMOVE places and see what DESK loses.
  It cannot test what eBird places would add (a different survey), only whether DESK's space and time scale with places.
question: with the held-out cells, readout and rivals fixed, how do DESK's held-out spatial skill, raw backcast change
and beyond-trend gain change when its training places are cut to 1/2 and 1/4?
design:
- desk_tempho_1995 (T0 1996), training seeds 1 and 2, desk.trend.train_frac 0.5 and 0.25 (whole 6x6 training blocks
  dropped after the split is drawn; the held-out and buffer cells are byte-identical; the dropped blocks come from the
  SPLIT seed, so both training seeds drop the same blocks -- paired, but one realization of which places go).
- Controls: E022 desk_tempho_1995 seeds 1, 2 (train_frac 1.0), same split; selected and late (epoch 490) caches.
  E055j seeds 5-8 (train_frac 1.0, same split) give extra seed-spread reference where they exist.
- Caches at the selected and late states (as E054). The cache's training rows are set by the split (holdout/buffer),
  so the readout and every linear rival are fitted on the SAME rows as the controls', including the dropped cells:
  only DESK's representation changes.
- Evaluation (E054's yardstick): fair_rivals --incremental --n-pos 96 --pos-seeds 1-5 --strong-base --ref-cache <same-
  seed control, same state>, paired. Quantities: space_level (held-out spatial skill); (a) raw backcast change vs
  control; (c) gain beyond the trend placebo vs control; DESK beyond the strong base. Plus the training-log over-fit
  gap (held-out / training error, epochs 130-170 and 450-499) as the manipulation check.
considerations: M28 (one yardstick, paired, matched states), G6/M9 (selected AND late; selection is noisy), M10 (two
seeds; seed spread on (a) ~0.024, on (c) ~0.01), M11/M20 (adequate placebo), M17-corrected (beyond-trend is
information outside the observed trend, not "habitat vs dynamics"), M23 (held-out places only), sealed-species guard
(the yardstick grades community change; no species readout).
predictions (rivals):
- PLACES LIMIT SPACE: space_level(1.0) - space_level(0.5) >= +0.01 at the selected state for both seeds, and larger
  again at 0.25. Expected (likely).
- PLACES LIMIT TIME: at the LATE state, (c) at 0.5 is below its control by >= 0.01 with CI < 0 for both seeds, and
  further at 0.25. The prior is weak: temporal levers have all been null and low-capacity linear rivals match DESK in
  time (E039i), but E055a ties the late temporal gain to the capacity that over-fits places.
- OVER-FIT PENALTY SCALES WITH PLACES: the late-state spatial loss (space_selected - space_late) is larger at 0.5 and
  0.25 than at 1.0 (the gap ratio rises as places fall).
decision rule (fixed now; both seeds must agree for a branch):
- TIME SCALES (PLACES LIMIT TIME holds): eBird places are a candidate TEMPORAL lever -> pre-register an eBird
  pre-training / separate-head DESK (raw EBD, guards below) as the next GPU line.
- SPACE ONLY (space scales, time flat: (c) CI includes 0 at 0.5 for both seeds), and the over-fit penalty scales:
  eBird places are a candidate lever for SPACE and possibly for training to the late state without the spatial cost
  -> the eBird design targets that (late state, spatial skill at matched states); time is not expected to move.
- SPACE ONLY without the penalty scaling: eBird's expected value is spatial coverage beyond the BBS footprint only.
- NEITHER scales: places are not DESK's limit; eBird pre-training drops in priority.
- Otherwise: mixed, report sizes.
eBird design guards (recorded now for whatever follows): no House Finch in any eBird input (contract); training species
restricted to the 96 reference + dev species, or the sealed set is declared spent; held-out and buffer cells excluded
from all eBird supervision (else the spatial test breaks AND the 2005-25 modern end of every backcast leaks);
validation, selection and grading on BBS only; >= 2 seeds per arm, paired.

  training log's held-out kernel loss (median kernel_val_sp over epochs 100-200 and 450-499 vs the control's);
- the TIME branch is unchanged (late state).
