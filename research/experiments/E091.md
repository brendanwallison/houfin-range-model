hypotheses: [covariate content]     parents: [E050, E052, E049c]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: CPU, ~1 h
why: E050's forest-age covariate (a 2006/2011 age map aged backward) and E052's observed land COVER (NLCD) gave forest
birds exactly 0. The literature (Hunter et al. 2001; Northrup et al. 2019) points to stand AGE / successional stage,
which cover fraction does not see and a back-cast cannot know (any stand disturbed after the early epoch is undefined).
The user asked to try it (2026-10-17). FIA's periodic inventories measured stand structure in the early epoch itself.
data: research/exp/fia_cells.py -> /mnt/e/Datasets/fia/fia_27km.npz (FIA DataMart ENTIRE_* CSVs downloaded 2026-10-17,
sha256 in the folder). Per 27 km cell, forest conditions restricted in both epochs to unreserved productive forest
(timberland), area-weighted: seedling/sapling share (STDSZCD 3), large-stand share (STDSZCD 1), mean stand age,
share aged <= 20. Early = each state's periodic inventory nearest 1976 (1966-88; 34 states, years 1972-1988); modern =
annual inventories 2010-19. 2,976 cells with >= 5 conditions for every field in both epochs (median 21 / 42).
Coverage: South, Lake States, Plains fringe, southern New England, CO / WY / AZ / NM; NOT PA, NY, OH, WV, ME, MO, IA,
the Pacific states, ID, MT, NV, UT.
design: research/exp/guild_dose.py --pooled --rival-all (the E049c / E050 estimator: one shared ridge map per guild on
held-out cells; rival = ALL covariate change DESK sees, residualized within environment type; gain = (all + FIA) -
all; null = the FIA change field moved in space, 8 draws), with --aux-mask-key fia_mask (train, score and draw nulls
only on FIA-covered cells: uncovered cells must not enter as zero change). Caches desk_tempho_1995_s1 and
desk_tempho_1985_s1 (two start years). Strata: SEDENTARY (AVONET Migration 1) PRIMARY, then all species.
Guilds (AVONET Habitat): Forest and Woodland (where structure should act), Shrubland (early-successional; the
seedling/sapling share should matter most), Grassland and Human Modified as NEGATIVE CONTROLS.
reading (pre-registered; type-residualized "type" mode primary, "type+kloc" (150 km local) reported):
- STRUCTURE ACTS ON FOREST BIRDS if, for sedentary Forest OR Woodland birds, the gain is > 0 with its bootstrap CI > 0
  AND above >= 7 of the 8 moved-null gains, at BOTH start years; the negative controls' gains not above their nulls.
- PARTIAL if that holds at one start year only, or only for all species (not sedentary).
- NOT RESOLVABLE for a guild x stratum with fewer than 8 species with resolvable change.
- Otherwise NO SIGNAL: observed structure change, at this coverage, adds nothing beyond DESK's covariates.
- Reported regardless: the gain per guild x stratum x mode, null quantiles, species counts, west / east halves.
caveats stated now: periodic and annual FIA differ in plot design (variable-radius clusters vs the national 4-subplot
design) and some periodic inventories sampled timberland only (handled by restricting both epochs to timberland);
stand-size class is algorithmic from stocking (expected steadier than stand age); age is mostly missing in early
NH / VT and size class partly missing in early CT / MA / RI (the >= 5-condition rule drops most of those cells); public
plot coordinates are fuzzed (~1.6 km) and some swapped within county. A design artefact that is uniform within a state
would look like a state-wide change; the moved nulls keep the field's smoothness but not its state boundaries.
considerations: M6 (regional change can be habitat), sedentary primary (memory), no House Finch; sealed never read.

READING (pre-registered): NO SIGNAL by the letter. Sedentary forest birds' gain is positive at both start years and
above 8/8 nulls by the count, but its CI includes 0 in the primary mode, and ONE moved null (a field shifted 20 rows
south) matches it almost exactly (+0.0258 vs +0.0259; +0.022 vs +0.023) -- a misplaced field of the same smoothness
does as well, so the placement of structure change is not shown to matter. All forest / woodland / shrubland birds: 0.
WHY (likely): what the FIA field adds for sedentary forest birds is broad-scale (a south-wide fall in early-successional
share and rise in stand age), which a shifted copy of the field still carries; local structure change adds nothing
measurable at 27 km with ~20-40 plots per cell. UNKNOWN whether that is FIA's plot noise per cell, the periodic /
annual design change, or birds not tracking local structure at this scale. Coverage limits: 644 cells, mostly South,
Lake States, Plains fringe; the guilds are small (8 sedentary forest species), so a small real effect is not excluded.
