# E039 Does DESK beat a WORKING direct covariate model? (the suite re-run with the repaired covariate arm)
hypotheses: [A13, T5, F1]     parents: [E006, E037]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~2-4 h per model (cpu)
pre-registered: 2026-10-10 21:34 (commit 664b1cc), before any run.

question: E006 read DESK as "the best arm the suite has on held-out places" -- but its direct covariate rival was
broken (A13), so the most direct test of whether DESK adds anything over its own inputs was never made. E037 repaired
the covariate GP (v8 passed the fixed criteria in the harness). This re-runs the corrected suite (E006's settings:
--n-boot 1000 --thin none) with --cov-mode repaired on both models (gp_species_base, desk_tempho_1995), adding two
arms: covariate (the repaired GP) and covariate_linear (BLR on 64 whitened covariate components -- the SAME model class
as the desk arm, so desk vs covariate_linear compares representations with the readout held fixed; the age model's
readout is exactly this BLR form).
measures: level skill vs intercept and change skill vs no_change, median over species (the suite's own estimands and
bootstrap), desk vs covariate and desk vs covariate_linear directly, for every set (SPACE on gp_species_base; TIME and
SPACE_TIME on desk_tempho_1995); the covariate arm's fit health (convergence, rho, scales).
predictions: (1) if DESK's community structure carries habitat information that the raw covariates do not offer a
species readout directly, desk beats covariate_linear on held-out LEVEL by a margin beyond the CI (it compresses 302
covariates into what predicts 96 species jointly); (2) the repaired GP narrows or closes that gap (nonlinearity);
(3) on CHANGE all arms sit near no change (E027: a place's own covariate change carries little), so covariate vs desk
on change is expected within +/-0.02. A covariate arm beating desk on held-out level would contradict E006's reading.
decision: covariate (or covariate_linear) >= desk on held-out level AND change -> escalate: DESK's value as the
habitat representation over a direct covariate readout is in question (a downstream BLR could read covariate
components instead). desk > covariate_linear clearly -> DESK earns its place as the representation; the GP tells how
much of that is nonlinearity anyone could fit. Either way the harness numbers (E037) are verified or not by this
independent implementation on the suite's own rows (verification b for E037).
considerations: G1/G2 (the anchor is the downstream BLR on DESK features: representation comparisons are made at a
fixed readout), M9 (level and change separately), M21 (skill >= 0 is a low bar), G6 (gp_species_base is the
production-like model), G7 (this tests whether DESK adds information, not a fix).
