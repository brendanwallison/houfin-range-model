# E074 Does a PLACE-DEPENDENT readout let early CBC lists support more of DESK's change? (pre-registered 2026-10-15)
# (before running; no smoke beyond the E069 scorer)
hypotheses: [CBC (bridge / complement), readout form]     parents: [E069a, E069a2, peer E073, E059e]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~4 x 15 min cpu
why: E069a2 -- even with a between/within split readout, early CBC lists support only ~20-30% of DESK's within-cell
change (best alpha 0.2-0.3; backcast - modern -0.108 / -0.020). The peer's E073 (2026-10-15): reading DESK's existing
change with PLACE-DEPENDENT slopes (the within-cell change times 4 dims of the cell-mean z, "kind of place") beats the
single-slope readout for sedentary species (+0.07-0.10 corr) at both T0s and seeds. If part of the over-amplitude is a
one-slope-fits-all readout, a place-dependent readout should let the lists support more of DESK's change.
design: e069_presence_score.py --readout interact: ridge on [zbar, dz, dz x s] (s = the first 4 dims of zbar,
standardized on training rows), a separate penalty per block chosen jointly by 5-fold CV over spatial blocks (grid
1e-3..10 each); everything else as E069a2 (same lists, offsets refitted, placebos, bootstrap, alpha grid). Caches:
DESK seed 1 AND seed 2, T0 1995 (E022 controls); the split readout re-run on seed 2 for comparison (seed 1's is E069a2).
reading (primary eras 1953-65 and 1966-95, all species; per seed):
- THE PLACE-DEPENDENT READOUT CARRIES THE REMAINING OVER-AMPLITUDE if backcast - modern is >= 0 or its interval covers 0
  in BOTH eras AND the best alpha >= 0.5, for both seeds.
- IT HELPS PARTLY if backcast - modern improves on the split readout by >= 0.02 in both eras for both seeds, or the best
  alpha rises by >= 0.2.
- Otherwise: no detectable readout contribution beyond the split; the over-amplitude is in z (as E069a2 concluded).
Residents reported. The readout's own overall quality (log-likelihood at alpha 0) reported per readout.
considerations: M28 (same lists, same score, one readout change at a time), M17-corrected, G11 (winter lists vs a
breeding-season readout), no House Finch, sealed never read.

READING (pre-registered, E074 and E074b alike): the place-dependent readout does NOT carry the remaining over-amplitude
and does not help partly -- it is WORSE than the split readout in both eras for both seeds (backcast - modern about
twice as negative; best alpha 0-0.1 instead of 0.2-0.5), and its modern-placebo fit is also worse (alpha-0 log-
likelihood -3.35 vs -3.26). The context definition (z_ema dense vs raw trained rows) makes no difference.
WHY (likely; not tested). (1) Extrapolation: the interaction terms multiply DESK's change by place features; early
changes are larger than the trained-year changes the slopes were fitted on, so place-specific slopes amplify the
backcast further where it is already too large. (2) Season: the readout is fitted to BREEDING abundance; a more
flexible readout fits breeding-specific structure that winter lists do not share -- its between-cell part (penalty
0.01 vs 0.1) also fits winter lists worse with no shift at all. So this does not contradict E073, which scored the
readout on breeding-season dev species within the BBS era; it says the place-dependent readout does not transfer to
winter lists or to the 1950s.
CONCLUSION across E069a2/E074: the over-amplitude early CBC lists see is in DESK's z change (and partly the level
readout); no readout tried here recovers it. Season remains the standing caveat for any winter-list yardstick.

## E074c Does any of the place-dependent part survive if shrunk with REACH? (pre-registered 2026-10-15, before running)
why: the peer (E073: flexible arms degrade most beyond the trained range; the interaction slope k fell 1.05 -> 0.86
when cover change exceeded it) suggested shrinking the interaction block toward zero with reach. Low cost.
design (research/exp/e074c_reach_shrink.py): the E074 interaction readout (z_ema context), interaction features of each
prediction scaled by exp(-reach / L), reach = max(0, 1996 - year), L in {0 (interaction off), 5, 10, 20, inf (= E074)};
offsets refitted per L; seeds 1 and 2. Backcast - modern per era with 95% bootstrap over circles.
reading: SOME OF IT SURVIVES if, for both seeds, the best L in 1966-95 is finite and > 0 and beats L = 0 by >= 0.01.
Also reported: L = 0 (the readout's own between + within part) against the split readout (E074: -0.020 / -0.009 in
1966-95) -- if L = 0 is still clearly worse, the interaction readout's problem is its between/within fit, not only
the interaction block.

L = 0 vs the split readout: less over-movement (backcast - modern -0.061 / -0.010 vs split -0.108 / -0.020 for s1)
but a worse fit overall (modern placebo -3.36 vs -3.26). WHY (likely): with interaction features present, CV chose a
heavy within-cell penalty (1 vs the split's 0.01) and a light between-cell one (0.01 vs 0.1); the heavy within penalty
shrinks DESK's change in the readout -- the same direction the lists ask for (E069a2's best alpha 0.2-0.3) -- while
the light between penalty fits breeding-season spatial structure that winter lists do not share.
