- E039i-o compared DESK with a covariate-free position x time trend. That measures what DESK adds OUTSIDE the

question: of the community change that extrapolating the observed regional trend predicts, how much do covariate-only
predictors also predict? They use no bird data from the held-out places' neighbourhoods over time.
design: research/exp/fair_rivals.py --incremental --n-pos 96 --pos-seeds 1 2 3 4 5 --strong-base --commonality on the
eight E039 caches. Covariate-only joint fits:
- cov_lin: kind x time 32 + the supervised cross-fitted within-map + EMA within PCs, all linear;
- cov_all: cov_lin + DESK z deviations;
- desk_within: alone.
Commonality on the noise-free corr^2 scale, per bootstrap draw, against T = placebo_300 (5-draw mean):
- shared = R2(T) + R2(C) - R2(T u C);
- unique_C = R2(T u C) - R2(T);
- unique_T = R2(T u C) - R2(C).
WHAT IT CAN AND CANNOT SHOW (fixed before results):
- shared: change that both the trend and the covariates predict. NOT attributable from these data. It is regional
  habitat change if the covariates' alignment with it is causal. It is a coincidence of two smooth fields, or a
  covariate clock (M26), if not. A large shared part is CONSISTENT with habitat-driven regional change and refutes
  "the trend is something the covariates cannot see". It does not prove habitat.
- unique_C: a LOWER bound on covariate-carried change (E039n measured it as a gain).
- unique_T: change the covariates do not carry. Candidates are species/community dynamics, observer drift, OR habitat
  change missing from the covariates (forest maturation, pesticides; T5). It is not "dynamics" by default (M6,
  corrected M17).
- cov_lin is linear, so a uniform covariate shift cannot reach its centred score. Its shared part comes from
  place-specific covariate change, not from a clock. DESK's (cov_all minus cov_lin) can include a nonlinear clock
  (M26; E035h: DESK's management response is ~70% clock-like even where there is no cropland).
predictions (4 selected-state pairs; late reported alongside):
- P1: shared_frac_of_T (cov_all) >= 0.5 in >= 3 of 4 (from the one number above).
- P2: shared_frac_of_T (cov_lin) >= 0.3 in >= 3 of 4. Place-specific covariate change aligns with a substantial part
  of the trend.
- P3: unique_T > 0 with CI > 0 (some trend-predictable change is not carried by the covariates).
reading:

- P2 fails while P1 holds: the overlap runs through DESK's nonlinearity, where a clock (M26) is possible.
