## Verification (`grid54_verify.json`)

**(1) Z cube (established).**
* The mean-embedding identity z_bar_B . z_bar_B' = mean child-pair z_i . z_j holds to a maximum relative
  error of 6.5e-5 over 3000 sampled pairs (float32).
* Truncating then averaging equals averaging then truncating: maximum difference 0.0 (linear).
* \|z_bar\|^2 / mean \|z_i\|^2 has quantiles q1 0.83, q5 0.91, q50 0.987, and a minimum of 0.59.
  * Mechanism: averaging shrinks the norm where the children's communities differ. That is mostly coasts
    and ecotones; heterogeneous blocks lose up to 40% of their kernel self-similarity.

**(2) Observations (established).**
* With snapping, the real-observation count and the count sum are preserved exactly (123,191 / 586,896).
* Coastal cells snapped: 2,308 observations (781 positive; 13,921 birds, 2.4%) from 73 fine cells,
  moved to 70 coarse land cells. Mean snap 54.6 km, maximum 108 km (25 observations moved 2 cells).
  The largest are Long Beach CA (4,125 birds), Santa Barbara, Rochester NY, Racine WI and the Chesapeake.
* **Pseudo-zeros:** 1,961 coarse cells, with 0 subset violations.
* **After `model_inputs`** with `n_sites` 1814:

  | | Pseudo sites | Pseudo observations | Area |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 27 km | 1,814 | 68,932 | 1.32M km² |
  | 54 km | 1,814 | 68,932 | 5.29M km² |

  * Revision 1 used the density rule and got 44,688 pseudo observations over 3.43M km².

**(3) Land (established).**
* Every coarse land cell has at least 2 land children; operator rows sum to 1.
* The native 27 km rebuild reproduces the existing mask exactly (0 differing cells).
* The coarse fraction equals the mean of the four fine fractions (difference 1e-16).
* 182 cells have land children but are not natively land. They hold 259 fine land cells, which are
  dropped from Z.

**(4) Z_disp.**
* Rebuilt 54 km values against the block mean of the 27 km values (31 years): per-feature correlation
  has a minimum of 0.996 and a median of 0.998. Over all kernel x feature pairs the minimum is 0.989.
* Per-kernel relative RMS difference is 2-6%, largest for the 0-155 km bands. This includes the switch
  from point to integrated kernels.

**(5) Kernels.**

## Open choices / needs a decision

1. **Grid semantics** (likely, for interpretation). Front densities are cell-averaged over 4x the area,
   so the Allee n50 (route-count scale) and the release-cell exemption act on thinner densities.
2. **Equal absence count, larger footprint.** The 68,932 absence observations now assert absence over
   4x the 27 km area per site. Read the pre-invasion constraint as covering more ground than at 27 km.
3. **Snapped observations.** 25 observations moved 108 km. Each snapped route's counts are attributed
   to a neighbouring cell's habitat.
4. **Ensemble/seed cubes.** The 128-d mean2 cube and the per-seed cubes are not local. The 54 km Z
   derives from the land-gathered 27 km Z, which equals the cube on land.
5. **Not built.** `obs_route_ids.npz` and `subcell_strata.npz` are not built (s64 does not use them).
   Any per-observation route/observer arrays the other agent adds to the BBS npz will flow through
   `coarsen_model_grid.py` once the npz is regenerated, but this input was built before they existed.
