# E063 Can CBC "bridge" years BBS cannot see? CBC regional change vs extrapolating BBS's later trend backward
# (pre-registered 2026-10-13 ~14:10, before any statistic was computed)
hypotheses: [CBC (bridge), T5]     parents: [E061, E058, E062, E039i-o]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~15 min cpu
why: the user (2026-10-13) was skeptical that CBC cannot help temporal skill. E061 tested CBC only INSIDE the training
era (winters 2001-2024) and graded "beyond the trend placebo", which by construction discounts the regional trend that
is CBC's content. CBC's distinctive value is that it covers years BBS lacks -- in the research setup the withheld
1966-1995, in production (via raw circle data) potentially back toward 1900. This rung-0 check asks, before any DESK
run, whether CBC's regional change over the withheld era carries information about BBS change in that era that the
best BBS-only alternative -- extrapolating BBS's own later trend backward, what the trend placebo does -- lacks.
design: research/exp/cbc_bridge_check.py on the desk_tempho_1995_s1 cache (keys, counts; no DESK output).
- Truth: BBS change per species and cell, 1966-1986 -> 2005-2025, cross ABBA halves (noise-free).
- CBC_withheld: the cell's CBC stratum change over the SAME era, log1p(mean index, winters ending 1967-1986) ->
  log1p(mean index, 2005-2024); strata from the BBS USGS/CWS state x BCR polygons (as E061's amendment).
- BBS_trend: the species' BBS change over the trained era (1996-2005 -> 2016-2025) as a leave-one-out 300 km field of
  other cells -- what extrapolating the observed later trend gives (corr is scale-free, so linear extrapolation is a
  rescaling).
- CBC_trained: the CBC stratum change over the trained era (1996-2005 -> 2016-2024).
- Noise-free corr pooled over species (centred and uncentred), CBC_withheld beyond BBS_trend, paired bootstrap
  differences (6x6-cell blocks), prevalence tiers, species-level corr of mean change. Species: reference + dev with a
  medium/high-suitability CBC product; this is a diagnostic of data agreement (no training), so dev species are
  allowed as in E058/E062; sealed species never.
considerations: M2/M3 (BBS halves; observers not removed), M4 (centred = place-specific; uncentred keeps each species'
continental trend, which a 1902-1939 backcast also needs and which BBS cannot supply before 1966), M6/M17-corrected
(predicting a regional trend says nothing about its cause), M14, M29 (the CBC trend product is the curators' own,
effort-modelled, 1966+; pre-1966 use would need raw circle data with our own effort model), season (winter counts
track breeding-season change mainly for residents and short-distance migrants).
predictions (written before any statistic):
- uncentred: CBC_withheld > BBS_trend (CBC sees the withheld era's continental and regional trends directly; BBS's
  later trend need not match the earlier one, N2).
- centred: both small (E058/E062: cell-level agreement between surveys is weak), CBC_withheld - BBS_trend within
  +-0.05.
- CBC_withheld > CBC_trained (the withheld-era coverage, not CBC as such, is what helps).
decision rule (CBC_withheld - BBS_trend, paired; "beyond" = CBC_withheld residualized on BBS_trend):
- BRIDGE PROMISING: uncentred difference >= +0.05 with CI > 0 AND CBC_withheld beyond BBS_trend >= +0.05 (uncentred)
  with CI > 0 -> CBC carries withheld-era trend information that extrapolation lacks; next: the DESK bridge test (CBC
  head through the withheld winters, lower weight, a CBC-only readout as the rival), and the raw-CBC effort model for
  pre-1966 years (see research/plans/raw_surveys.md).
- PLACE-SPECIFIC TOO: the same in the centred form -> CBC also informs where change happened within the era.
- NOT PROMISING: uncentred difference CI includes 0 or < +0.05 -> CBC's regional change over the withheld era is no
  better a guide than extrapolating BBS's later trend; the bridge test is not worth GPU time.
- Report all forms and tiers regardless.
