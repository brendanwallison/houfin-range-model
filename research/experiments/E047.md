# E047 Does county-census-based cropland history carry local change signal that LUH lacks? (pre-registered
# 2026-10-11 15:54, before downloading)
hypotheses: [T5, T9, E044d]     parents: [E044d, E045, E035g/h]     eval_version: v1     cost: 2.2 GB download + ~30 min cpu
why: the user approved (2026-10-11) the feasibility check for historical covariates with real local resolution.
- DESK's land use is LUH, which allocates state- and national-level statistics spatially, so its local change is
  largely an allocation pattern.
- E044d: within environment type and region, local covariate change predicts community change only weakly
  (land use 0.04-0.06).
- CropAT-US (Ye, Cao & Lu 2024, ESSD 16:3453; figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.22822838.v2, CC BY 4.0) gives annual 1 km
  cropland density and crop types, 1850-2021. It is built from COUNTY-level crop inventories and census data, and
  matches county acreage within 0.2 kha in >75% of counties.
- It covers the 1902-1939 backcast window. Cropland is only one land use, but it is the one with the best
  county-level history.
- Other sources checked: HISLAND-US (Li et al. 2023) builds on STATE-level census tables, so it has the same weakness
  as LUH. USGS FORE-SCE 1938-1992 is modelled, with natural classes allocated passively by ecoregion. ICPSR 35206
  county tables need a login, which is excluded.
design: research/exp/cropat_test.py.
- CropAT-US cropland density aggregated (area mean) to DESK's 27 km grid (ESRI:102003). Epoch means per cell: early
  1966-86 vs modern 2005-21 (CropAT ends 2021).
- LUH cropland = c3ann + c4ann + c3per + c4per + c3nfx, raw fractions from the yearly states, same cells and years.
- Held-out cells and truth halves as E044d. TYPE = 32 PCs of mean covariate levels; type and type+region (96 RFF,
  300 km) residualization fitted on training cells.
- Ridge / OLS maps fitted on training cells, graded on held-out cells by noise-free corr. Paired 6x6-block bootstrap
  of CropAT - LUH.
- Arms (within-type, and within type+region):
  - crop change from CropAT (1 feature) vs from LUH (1 feature);
  - both;
  - CropAT crop-type changes (if the crop-type map gives fractions) vs LUH's 5 crop classes.
- Concordance: correlation of CropAT and LUH crop CHANGE across cells, overall and within type; levels for reference.
predictions / rule (T0 1996 and T0 1986 caches, which are near-identical in cells):
- LOCAL SIGNAL: within type AND region, CropAT crop change - LUH crop change >= +0.03 in corr with CI > 0 at both
  T0s. The census-based history carries local change information LUH does not. That supports investing in historical
  county-based inputs, and the next step would be to add CropAT channels to DESK.
- NO GAIN: the CI includes 0 or the difference is negative at both.
- The concordance is reported either way. Low within-type change concordance would confirm that LUH's local change
  is an allocation pattern.
considerations: M26 (where it acts: cropland), M6, T5, T9 (pre-1950 inputs), G4.

WHY / reading (likely):
- (i) LUH's local cropland CHANGE does not match the county-census-based record (~0 concordance). This is direct
  support for "LUH's local change is largely an allocation pattern". LUH's crop change does correlate with community
  change unresidualized (0.12), so it behaves like a smooth regional pattern, not local information.
- (ii) Cropland AMOUNT change alone carries almost no within-type community-change signal in either source. Total
  crop fraction is probably the wrong variable. Birds respond to WHAT is grown and what it replaced (row crops vs hay,
  pasture and grassland; CRP; abandonment to forest), not to net cropped area.
- Unknown: whether CropAT's crop TYPES (corn/soy expansion, hay and small-grain loss) carry the local signal.

## E047b Crop-TYPE composition change: does CropAT add local change signal? (pre-registered 2026-10-11 16:01)
why: E047. Net crop amount carries ~no within-type signal in either source. Birds plausibly respond to WHAT is grown
(row-crop expansion vs small grains, hay and pasture).
- CropAT's CropType maps are categorical, the dominant type per 1 km pixel. The paper's legend lists, in order: corn,
  soybean, winter wheat, spring wheat, durum wheat, cotton, sorghum, barley, rice, and "others" (incl. hay, oats;
  excluding idle/fallow and cropland pasture).
- Codes 1-10 are taken in that order; the 2000 pixel counts are consistent (rice smallest, "others" largest).
design: cropat_aggregate.py --types adds, per year, each type's fraction of cell area (density x [type == k], area
mean). cropat_test.py --types fits ridge maps (lambda by 6x6-block CV) on training cells and grades held-out cells.
Arms:
- A: CropAT's 10 type-fraction changes;
- B: LUH's 5 crop-class changes (c3ann, c3nfx, c3per, c4ann, c4per);
- C: all 33 LUH land-use channel changes;
- D: C + A.
Each is graded within type and within type+region, as E047.
rule (both T0s): LOCAL SIGNAL if D - C >= +0.02 with CI > 0 within type+region at both T0s (CropAT adds to LUH's full
land use), or A - B >= +0.03 with CI > 0 there. Otherwise NO GAIN. Within-type numbers are reported alongside.

WHY (likely):
- County-census-based crop-type history carries community-change information that LUH lacks. It shows at the scale
  of regions within an environment type, such as the replacement of small grains and hay by corn and soy across the
  northern plains and Midwest. It does not show at 150 km neighbourhoods: local differences in crop-type change at
  27 km do not track local community change.
- LUH's crop classes (C3/C4 annual/perennial/N-fixing) do not separate these crops. Its local change does not match
  the census record (concordance ~0).
- Net crop AMOUNT is the wrong variable: birds respond to what is grown.
IMPLICATIONS (not decisions):
- (1) Census-based crop-type history is a concrete, modest covariate improvement, about +0.04 corr within type. It
  extends to 1850 with county-level crop types, so it covers the 1902-1939 backcast window, when crop shifts were
  large (wheat frontier, cotton belt).
- (2) It does not make local land-use change predictive at 27 km. The local bottleneck stays.
- (3) Adding CropAT density and type channels to DESK is a reasonable GPU line if pursued. The expected gain is modest.

   - The census is every 4-5 years, with gaps filled from state trends.
   - "LUH's local change is an allocation pattern" holds at COUNTY scale. Below county, CropAT is a different
