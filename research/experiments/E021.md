# E021 (O1, defect A14) How much of the "true change" every metric counts is observer turnover?
hypotheses: [O1]     parents: [E018, E020]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~5 min cpu
pre-registered: AFTER the skeptic's check and a development run (2026-10-09); the job re-runs it for the registry.
design: research/exp/observer_turnover.py. ABBA halves share an epoch's observers, so observer changes between epochs
pass the noise subtraction. Within the modern epoch (2005-14 vs 2016-25), the place-specific cross-half change energy
for cells whose observer set (BBS ObsN of QC runs) was unchanged / partly changed / replaced; the replaced-minus-
unchanged excess against the early -> modern change energy is the observer share. Species (dev, resolvable) and the
reference community in ESK (r24, r64).
development run (base cache): within-modern cells unchanged 565 / partial 1314 / replaced 500; 81% of cells share no
observer between the early and modern epochs. Observer share of the early -> modern change energy: species 29% [25,
33]; ESK r24 28% [24, 32]; ESK r64 29% [25, 33].
reading (UNVERIFIED; observational -- cells that change observers may differ otherwise): roughly 30% of what every
change metric here counts as true change is observer turnover, which no habitat model should predict. Every ceiling,
captured share and correlation is diluted by it (a perfect habitat model's temporal R2 is capped near 0.7), but it
cannot create cross-model or cross-arm differences on the same cells. Remedy for later metrics: an observer-continuity
stratum, or observer terms in the target (the age model may already have first-year observer effects).
CORRECTION (skeptic round 3): "observer turnover cannot create cross-arm differences" is wrong for any arm whose
FEATURES share observers with the target -- the observed-community arm of E013 gains 33-45% of its numerator from
observer co-movement, DESK none.
