# E083 Does a WINTER community block add information for sedentary species beyond the breeding block?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-16, before running; no DESK training)
hypotheses: [representation (concatenation vs middle ground), season (G11)]     parents: [E066c, E079, E081, E082]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~1 h cpu
why: the user (2026-10-16): instead of a middle-ground basis for BBS + CBC (+ eBird), concatenate them -- a year-round
community description, possibly more expressive and better at generalization. E082: the seasons differ in WHERE and
WHEN species are relatively common, which calibration cannot remove; a concatenated representation keeps that
difference. Before building DESK heads: does the winter block carry information about sedentary species' BREEDING
abundance and change (the age model's target) beyond the breeding block and beyond extra dimensions alone?
design (research/exp/e083_concat_readout.py): cell-years with both BBS and CBC (1966-2024). Blocks: B = ESK coordinates
of the observed BBS community (24 dims, the age model's r24); C = a winter basis -- uncentred Ruzicka kernel PCA of CBC
communities (Nystrom, 2,000 landmarks from training cells, 24 dims); Cs = C shuffled across cells within year
(dimension control). Arms B, B+C, B+Cs, C. Targets: log1p BBS counts of dev species. Ridge readouts (CV over spatial
blocks on training cells), scored on DESK's held-out + buffer cells: LEVEL (per-species Spearman across test cell-years,
median) and CHANGE (1966-85 -> 2005-24 window means per cell; ridge on feature change fitted on training cells). 6x6
block bootstrap for B+C - B and B+C - B+Cs.
reading (SEDENTARY primary; residents, migratory reported):
- WINTER BLOCK ADDS INFORMATION (concatenation worth building) if B+C - B+Cs > 0 with the interval excluding 0 AND
  B+C - B >= +0.02, for LEVEL or for CHANGE (both reported; CHANGE is the backcast-relevant one).
- NO ADDED INFORMATION if B+C - B+Cs has an interval covering 0 for both LEVEL and CHANGE -> a breeding-only or
  middle-ground representation is enough; the multi-head design keeps CBC only as a separate head, not a readout block.
caveats: co-located cells only (winter block exists only where CBC does); ESK coordinates of the OBSERVED community are an
upper bound on what DESK's prediction of each block carries; the change test may be thin (cells with both surveys in
both windows among held-out + buffer cells).
considerations: sedentary primary, G11, M28, no House Finch, sealed never read.

---- E083b RESULT (2026-10-16 ~07:07; job E083/e083b-concat-change-cv-ef6e210d007d) ----
297 co-located cells with both windows, 5-fold CV over spatial blocks; median per-species Spearman of change:
  sedentary (19 spp): B 0.112, B+C 0.134, B+Cs 0.077, C 0.097; B+C - B +0.021 [-0.061, +0.110]; B+C - B+Cs +0.057
     [-0.059, +0.120]; 13 of 19 species higher with the winter block (e.g. tuftit 0.54 -> 0.61, moudov 0.39 -> 0.49,
     rehwoo 0.36 -> 0.46, carwre 0.60 -> 0.66).
  residents (42): B+C - B +0.014 [-0.031, +0.056]; migratory (76): +0.021 [-0.015, +0.048].
READING (same rule): NOT ROBUST -- with every cell tested and more species, the winter block's change gain shrinks to
+0.01-0.02 with intervals covering 0 in every class. E083's +0.13 on 6 species / 113 cells did not hold up.
WHY (likely): the six species of E083 were among the best-measured; across more species and cells change prediction is
noisy (median 0.11), and the winter block's contribution, if any, is small. Direction is consistent but not shown.
CONCLUSION: a winter block carries at most a SMALL amount of extra information about sedentary breeding-season change in
co-located cells; none for spatial level. It does not justify a large winter block in the readout representation.
