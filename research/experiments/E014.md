reading (UNVERIFIED):
- The nugget is LARGE (two routes < 10 km apart differ by 0.29, most of the 25-km difference): the skeptic's premise
  holds. But ESK keeps ~24% of it, not ~0.
- At equal effort and equal raw size, temporal retention equals spatial (0.280 vs 0.273 at 20-30 km).
- ~55% of a route's 40-y change is shared with routes < 10 km away, ~19% with routes 200-300 km away; ESK r24 keeps
  45-71% of the shared part -- as well as or better than structured spatial differences (~0.34-0.52 once the nugget
  is removed). The route-specific remainder (~45%) is kept at ~6%.
- So truncation does not drop temporal DIRECTIONS (R1 strong form not supported); it drops route-level idiosyncrasy,
  temporal or spatial, and keeps regionally coherent change. At 27-km cells averaging ~1.1 routes, a cell's change
  is a route's change, so ~45% of it is the idiosyncratic part no 27-km covariate could predict anyway.
