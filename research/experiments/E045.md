# E045 Does a cross-place CHANGE map fitted inside the trained era transfer to the withheld decades? (pre-registered
# 2026-10-11 15:24, before running)
hypotheses: [T1, T4, B7, T5]     parents: [E044d, E039i-n, E016]     eval_version: v1     cost: ~5 min cpu
why: E044d. A covariate-change -> community-change map fitted ACROSS PLACES carries a within-type dose-response.
Maps trained on annual within-cell deviations do not. Those include the row-level within-map (skeptic Part B 0.03),
and, effectively, DESK's per-row stabilizing target. E044d's change map used training cells' withheld-era
communities, so it is not a backcast.
question: fitted only on change WITHIN the trained era (training cells, w1 -> w2), does such a map predict held-out
cells' backcast change (1966-86 -> 2005-25, E039h's estimand) better than DESK?
design: research/exp/change_map.py.
- T0 1996: w1 1996-2005, w2 2016-2025.
- T0 1986: w1 1986-1995, w2 2016-2025.
- Training cells need >= 2 surveyed rows in each window.
- Arms:
  - chg:<fam>: ridge, lambda by 6x6-block CV, families all / land / luh_class / climate;
  - type: what this kind of place did in the trained era;
  - type+chg:all.
- Paired bootstrap against DESK's raw change on the same cells. References from E039k, same cells: placebo
  0.299/0.421, within_sup 0.286/0.342.
- Seed-1 caches. The covariate arms do not depend on the seed. DESK's seed 2 and late states are also reported.
predictions / rule (T0 1996 and 1986):
- TRANSFERS: chg:all - desk >= +0.05 with CI > 0 at both T0s. Cross-place change supervision inside the observed era
  carries temporal information DESK lacks. That would argue for a change-supervised DESK target or readout as a GPU
  line.
- DOES NOT: chg:all - desk <= 0 at both.
- Otherwise: in between, report sizes.
- Also report whether chg:all beats the placebo (0.30/0.42).
- Caveat: a change map fitted on 20-30-year trained-era change and applied to 39-year backcast change assumes
  linear-in-time responses. Corr is scale-free, so only the direction matters.
considerations: M11, M17 (corrected), M26 (a type x time pattern is in "type"), M6, G3/G4.

Pre-registered: IN BETWEEN.
- At T0 1996 the cross-place change map ties DESK.
- At T0 1986 it is WORSE than DESK, by 0.04-0.06 (CI < 0 for s1).
- No TRANSFER.
WHY (likely):
- Cross-place change supervision inside the observed era recovers about DESK's temporal skill and no more. Its
  extra content (E044d's within-type dose-response) is too small to show here, especially when fitted on 20-30
  years and applied to a 39-year backcast.
- What beats DESK is "type": what each kind of place did in the observed era, extended backward. It scores like the
  trend placebo (0.30/0.42), and it is the environment-type x time form (M26). In the backcast window it extrapolates
  a TIME trend, not a response to the covariates of 1902-1939.
- Climate-change maps transfer worst (0.16-0.19), even though climate carried most of E044d's within-type
  dose-response.
IMPLICATION (not a decision): a change-supervised DESK target is unlikely to beat DESK on covariate-driven temporal
skill. Its likely gain is type trends, which help on 1966-86 and amount to a clock for 1902-1939. Not recommended as
a GPU line on this evidence.
