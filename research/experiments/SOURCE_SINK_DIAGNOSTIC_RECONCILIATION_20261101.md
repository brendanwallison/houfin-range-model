A historical Plains barrier is expected; neither low occupancy nor an establishment impediment is itself a pathology. The concern is the mechanism: the optimizer can assign positive potential growth but extremely low K so absolute mate limitation prevents self-replacement at every density. A deterministic density model can then maintain fractional populations through immigration without representing discrete colonists, pairing events or demographic stochasticity. ## What already exists

Verified against frozen step34_abs_natdep_s41 code on TACC, rather than assuming the current Windows checkout is the production source:

1. **Potential growth:** `local_growth_lambda` excludes crowding, mate limitation and movement. Frozen yearling code uses the appropriate yearling branch. This is one component of habitat quality, not a sufficient source/sink classifier.
2. **Capacity–Allee viability:** `allee_viability` searches realized reproduction over density and tests whether a positive local equilibrium exists. The frozen code includes yearling breeding, aggregated mate finding and the alternative reproductive form. `allee_dead_mask` specifically means fundamentally suitable but no viable positive equilibrium under this test. Consequently the reported Plains fractions already concern a K/Allee-induced failure of replacement despite positive potential growth; they are not just a count of all inhospitable habitat.
3. **Growth at K:** the old at-K threshold is explicitly deprecated. K is parameterized through replacement, making thresholding its growth rate near one sensitive to the numerical guard. The stored `realized_source_sink` block reports fold viability and suppression summaries, not an observed-population demographic budget. Its mean growth-at-K value must not be substituted for actual realized source/sink status.
4. **Barrier operator and propagule pressure:** `barrier_crossing` follows dispersal and descendant production in a corridor under an Allee-optimistic linearization; the Allee factor is set to one. The stored absolute-reference corridor spectral radius is 1.752; absolute/native seeds 41/42 are 1.506/1.513. The implementation reports self-sustaining optimistic operators and leaves crossing gains/propagule-pressure quantities null in this regime. These are existing results that the prior summary omitted. A supercritical optimistic operator does not establish that the actual nonlinear population crosses, nor does it alone establish a viable route all the way across. Native departure and other later movement changes also require explicit contract checks before treating this operator as a faithful comparison across current variants.

The source labels and stored verdict text can overstate what these objects establish. In particular, the optimistic spectral result alone does not prove that all actual barrier behavior is caused by Allee/K rather than habitat quality; it indicates that the optimistic restricted system contains sustaining dynamics.

## What the previous audit did not implement

**Explicitly agreed metric omitted from the reconciliation:** Runge, Runge & Nichols (2006), contribution C^r, is registered as S4 in SDM_BASELINE_PLAN_2026-10-30.md, section 5. That plan also registers S2 realized local replacement, S3 BIDE and S6 apparent growth. C^r is not an optional newly invented diagnostic: it is part of the existing agreed suite. Repository searches found the registration but no implementation or reported C^r results in the reviewed audit. Its omission from this reconciliation was a further omission of prior work.

The original paper defines C^r using survival/transition probabilities over a complete breeding cycle: a focal population receives credit for its surviving individuals and recruits wherever they end up in the metapopulation, including successful emigrants, while immigrants from other origins are not credited to it. This distinguishes contribution from local abundance growth, potential growth, local retention and a no-immigration intervention. Citation: https://doi.org/10.1086/503531, section "The Contribution of a Local Population". The paper explicitly addresses the problem of counting emigrants as deaths.

For this project the implementation must establish a common breeding census and recruitment definition before applying the metric to adult/juvenile states. Arbitrarily summing newborn juveniles and adults or inserting reproductive-value weights is not automatically the original metric. The registered plan correctly leaves the stage/currency issue open pending literature verification. Source attribution must follow movement, journey survival and reproduction in the actual census order, with density-dependent rates evaluated in the full fitted environment. An infinitesimal Jacobian of the nonlinear simulator is a marginal response and is not automatically realized per-capita contribution; likewise, rerunning each origin alone changes mate finding and crowding. A frozen-environment origin-attribution operator needs accounting checks against the full annual update.

C^r, its retained component R^r and successful export C^r-R^r should be reported alongside local demographic balance, actual immigration and the K/Allee mechanism classification. This would prevent replacing an exporting source with an apparent sink simply because its residents leave, and distinguish imported abundance from contribution. It does not independently validate the low-K mating model or stochastic colonization behavior.

The comprehensive inventory collected the existing metrics, parameter summaries and maps, but the synthesis reduced source/sink discussion largely to potential-growth categories. It neither synthesized the existing viability/crossing results nor completed the promised population-budget diagnostics. The missing calculations are:

- **Actual annual demographic balance at fitted states:** surviving residents, births, juvenile recruitment and deaths, respecting the model's census order and yearling reproduction. Report local production before transport separately from changes in total abundance.
- **Transport accounting:** adult/juvenile arrivals and departures, movement survival and off-domain or numerical mass loss, separately. Gross immigration, net immigration and local demographic deficit are different quantities. A finite cell's export/import status is also scale dependent.
- **Dependence on immigration:** local replacement deficit and the incoming flux supporting it, then targeted no-external-immigration replays with resident survival and local retention represented consistently. Holding surrounding populations fixed answers a different question from cutting immigration across an entire regional boundary; both must be labeled.
- **Mechanism separation:** distinguish intrinsically unfavorable cells, intrinsically favorable but K/Allee-nonviable cells, viable cells below their threshold, and self-replacing occupied cells. Map these with K, mate scale, actual abundance and observed support; do not pool them into one sink percentage.
- **Colonization robustness:** localized finite propagules and pairing/within-cell aggregation sensitivities, then a deliberately scoped demographic-stochasticity check if testing chance establishment. The current deterministic outputs cannot establish a probability of eventual crossing or distinguish a low crossing probability from an absolute deterministic prohibition. Extremely small fitted K must also be translated to biological counts and spatial support before interpreting such a check.

These diagnostics should use the exact frozen forward updates, with mass-balance checks, rather than a second approximate demographic model. In particular, no literal individual-level stochastic process is supplied merely by adding noise to fractional grid densities. The seasonal observation layer and later initialized founder state address other mismatches and do not replace this budget audit.

## Consequence for current assessment

The measured 4.87%/11.65% Plains Allee-dead-cell fractions in the absolute/native seeds are specifically the positive-potential-growth/no-positive-equilibrium category. They identify where to inspect the low-K/absolute-Allee mechanism; their increase alone is not a biological ranking. Neither favorable western potential-sink fractions nor unfavorable Plains mask fractions establishes the quality of the configuration. A defensible assessment must combine the mechanism, actual demographic/transport budgets, colonization behavior and historical pattern. The promised flux and replacement work remains unfinished; the previous responses should have said so while reporting the existing non-intrinsic diagnostics.
