# E038 Train DESK with temperature held static: does the space-for-time error go, and at what cost?
hypotheses: [T4, T11]     parents: [E035, E032]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: rung 4 (4 DESK runs, ~4.5 GPU-h)
pre-registered: 2026-10-10 20:37 (commit a705070), before any run.

question: E035b-d found that DESK's backcast leans toward the space-for-time analog through its TEMPERATURE reading,
while the part of its backcast that matches observed change comes from regional land-use change. Holding climate fixed
at inference removes most of the lean in already-trained models. This asks the training-time version: if DESK never
sees temperature change -- each cell gets its own 1966-2025 mean of every temperature channel in every year, so
temperature still says WHERE a place is but cannot say WHEN -- (i) does the lean disappear, (ii) does the backcast keep
its real signal, (iii) what does spatial fit cost?
design: research/exp/static_channels.py wraps run_encoder.py desk, cache.py and epoch_variant.py and replaces
covariate_io.load_state_stack for the process (every DESK path loads states through it; checked: 180 temperature
channels -- Tave/Tmax/Tmin, DD5/DD18/DDsub0/DDsub18, NFFD, Eref -- become identical across years where finite, all
other 122 channels untouched). Same overlays, seeds and held-out cells as the unweighted controls:
desk_tempho_1995 seeds 1, 2 and desk_tempho_1985 seeds 1, 2 (controls: their existing caches, plus seeds 3, 4 from
E032 for the seed spread). Both the epoch-selected and the late (epoch-490) states are cached.
caveat (fixed now): CMD and CMI are counted as moisture but are computed from Eref, so part of the temperature signal
still moves through them; a lean that survives may travel that way (the attribution script can swap them separately).
measures (the same tools and settings as the controls): (1) backcast attribution (E035b): alignment of the backcast
with the analog direction vs the random-rotation null, and noise-free centred corr of the full backcast and its parts
with observed change; (2) pooled raw backcast corr over the first three pre-T0 windows with its block bootstrap CI
(zone_breakdown, all zones); (3) spatial fit: best val_kernel from run_summary.json.
predictions: if temperature change is the route of the lean, the static models' analog alignment falls below half the
controls' (seed-matched) and near the null, while backcast corr stays within the controls' seed spread (land use
carries the signal) -- possibly rising, since temperature no longer competes to explain within-cell change. If the lean
survives at >= half, DESK reaches the analog another way (CMD/CMI, or land use's spatial correlation with
temperature). Spatial fit: a small loss is expected (year-to-year temperature explains some year-to-year community
variation); a loss beyond the 4-seed spread of the controls is a real cost.
decision: lean gone + corr held + spatial cost within seed spread -> escalate "static temperature in training" next to
the already-escalated inference-time climate freeze (no production change without the user). Otherwise record which
route the lean takes and test it (CMD/CMI swap) before anything else. Either way this is a mechanism test (G7), not a
fix adopted on its own.
considerations: G6 (production is the epoch-200 state; tempho runs are epoch-selected, so both their selected and late
states are reported), G7 (mechanism, not fix), M9/M10 (noise-free centred corr; the analog alignment is read against
its random-rotation null), M19 (spatially blocked bootstrap), M22 (backcast saturation before ~1960 is not tested here),
P2 (pre-registered).
