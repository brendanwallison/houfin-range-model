# Community Z: prior-work map and design boundary

The model-development target is a community representation. Learn its covariate, spatial, temporal and seasonal flexibility using community data, then freeze the checkpoint/transformation/cube before House Finch inference. House Finch uses this fixed Z with a shared space-for-time readout; it does not fit a species-specific temporal residual, kernel metric, seasonal weighting or encoder modulation. Earlier population-model drift experiments are context and diagnostics, not the proposed implementation.

The earlier phrase 'sparse species borrow shared residual structure' means that a community-trained residual has already been incorporated in the frozen features. It does not mean jointly updating that residual from House Finch observations.

## Consequences for proposed tests

Correction after deeper root-cause audit: E055g/h already tested known places/withheld times and identified modern-end memorization through endpoint swaps. E086r/r-b already tested conditioning on each cell's own CBC change, including an epoch-scale correction for annual attenuation. E014 directly measured route-level change coherence, and E086u/v/v-b traced the target-era information gain and its failure under early-quality observation routes. These must precede further architecture proposals. See DESK_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT_2026-10-26.md, including a fresh exact reproduction of E055h. Calling these cases untested was incorrect.

The new scientific question is not whether separating space/change, rotating a basis or adding time is possible. Those ideas already have substantial prior work. The unestablished comparison is whether **community-trained**, low-complexity temporal conditioning or a learned similarity metric improves current clean seasonal Z under winter-supported, summer-hidden validation, with Z then frozen for the population model.

Before a new expensive test, record the nearest existing experiment/artifact, its actual final status and corrections, which code/data machinery is reused, and the precise unresolved difference (representation, training objective, supervision period, validation task or numerical defect). Reproduce an existing baseline where it is comparable. A new name or new basis alone does not justify rerunning an old failed readout proposal.

The earlier recommendation of a change-ordered prior did not adequately incorporate E086e's negative stage2 result; that omission is corrected here. The initial modern breeding-only metric calibration is a limited diagnostic, not a completed community-wide historical calibration.
