# E023 Training span vs extrapolation reach: does more history improve a backcast at a FIXED reach?
hypotheses: [N2, S3, E3, T9]     parents: [E016, E019]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~5 min cpu per run
pre-registered: development runs first (2026-10-09, prompted by the user: the tempho models confound span and reach --
longer reach always comes with fewer trained years -- while production trains on 60); the jobs re-run them.

design: research/exp/span_reach.py. Cheap surrogates refitted on training spans [T0, T0+L-1] (training cells only); the
SAME held-out cells and backcast windows W before T0 graded for every span, so at fixed (T0, W) -- fixed reach -- only
the span changes. Scored change: W against the first ten trained years [T0, T0+9] (inside every span), noise-free and
centered. Surrogates (within-cell): trend (position fields x year; no covariates), cov (64 covariate PCs), cov+trend;
DESK (the tempho model trained on [T0, 2025]) raw, and recalibrated by a within-cell BLR fitted on the span's rows.

reading (UNVERIFIED): for these surrogates, MORE history at a fixed reach makes the backcast WORSE, and with a short
span next to the target the reach barely matters. The relevant variable is how far the training data lie from the
target, not how many years there are: decadal trends / temporal relationships are non-stationary, later decades do not
describe earlier ones, and long fits average them in (not just by weight -- balancing years changes nothing). E019's
"degradation with reach" therefore partly reflects where the tempho models' training mass sits. For DESK, the model
itself is fixed here, but recalibrating its temporal deviations on the decade nearest the target lifts its 27.5-y
backcast from 0.057 (raw) / 0.111 (full-span recalibration) to 0.187 with calibration ~1: DESK's features carry more
transferable temporal information than its full-span behaviour shows.
consequence for production (60 training years, target 26-64 years before the BBS): more history by itself is not
expected to help; the earliest BBS decades are the most informative, and their weight in whatever learns the temporal
mapping is the lever to test -- E024 (DESK trained on short spans near the target, matched reach) next.
