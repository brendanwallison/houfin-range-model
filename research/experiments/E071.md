why:
- E059h: for sedentary out-of-community species DESK trails trend + covariates by only ~0.05 (migrants ~0.10-0.14).
- E067 (reduced-rank surrogates):
  - dropping the community's migrants does NOT help sedentary targets;
  - growing a resident community (69 -> 121 species) does (+0.06-0.07, CI > 0, both T0s), roughly matching (T0 1996)
    or beating (T0 1986) the current 96.
- E066c (other session): residents' winter (CBC) and breeding (BBS) communities agree on change (0.52); migrants'
  do not (-0.06). A resident community is also the CBC-compatible one.
- E071 is the DESK test of the surrogate result.

design:
- Community: research/E071/resident_community.csv, 121 species (77 sedentary).
  - the current community's 69 sedentary + partial species;
  - the 52 resident (AVONET migration 1-2) dev species of E067's half A (seed 7).
  - No sealed species, none of half B, no House Finch (asserted when the list was built).
- Evaluation: half B (research/E071/eval_species_B.txt, 137 species: 31 sedentary / 22 partial / 76 migratory),
  never in either community. The same species as E067.
- Pipeline, via overlays only, every output under ${E071_BASIS} or the job dir (no existing artifact touched):
  - run_encoder bbs-points + spacetime-esk (E071/build);
  - DESK desk_tempho_{1995,1985} seeds 1 and 2, identical to the E022 controls apart from the community / basis;
  - research caches *_res121 (+ _late).
- Score: fair_rivals --target dev --save-species on the new caches.
  - species_strata --species-filter half B, against the existing E059h runs of the same seeds and states (the
    current DESK).
  - Paired contrasts are valid only if the new caches' keys equal the old ones'. Rows where no community species
    was recorded are dropped, so a different community can change a few rows. The eval job prints the keys check.
  - If keys differ, the comparison is unpaired (each run's own CI) and the contrasts are discarded.

PRIMARY: DESK within-readout CHANGE on SEDENTARY half-B species, resident DESK minus current DESK, same seed and state,
selected state. 4 pairs (2 seeds x 2 T0s).
RULE:
- RESIDENT COMMUNITY HELPS SEDENTARY TARGETS: mean over the 4 selected-state pairs >= +0.02, with >= 3 of 4 pairs
  positive (CI > 0 if paired).
- HURTS: mean <= -0.02 with >= 3 of 4 negative.
- Otherwise: NO DETECTABLE EFFECT.
- Reported alongside: residents (n = 53), all B, migratory B (expected flat or worse), the late state, the gap to
  the strong base, spatial level (DESK pooled vs linear), and the community-target sanity checks (val_kernel).
PREDICTION (from E067): small positive, ~+0.01-0.04 on sedentary B, larger at T0 1986 than at T0 1996; migratory B
flat or slightly worse.
IMPLICATION if it helps: the community should be rebuilt on (many) residents, a production-relevant change, reported
as an implication. It also aligns with CBC comparability (E066c).
