# E069 Early CBC presences as an effort-immune check (and, later, supervision) of DESK's z
# E069a pre-registered 2026-10-14, before running; no smoke (running the script is the test)
hypotheses: [CBC (bridge / complement)]     parents: [E065, E066, E068, plans/raw_surveys.md section 8]
eval_version: pre-v1     cost: ~10 min cpu
why: the user (2026-10-13) asked whether DESK's z could be lightly supervised by early CBC PRESENCES alone, if
effort-corrected abundance cannot be trusted. The principled form is presence-only conditioned within list: given that
a list recorded a species, which one (softmax over species of readout(z) + species offset), so a list's own effort and
completeness cancel (point-process view of presence-only data: Warton & Shepherd 2010; Renner et al. 2015; Fithian et
al. 2015 for many species with a shared sampling bias). The same likelihood scores EXISTING z without training: if
DESK's backcast already predicts early lists better than the modern community does, early CBC is a yardstick for DESK
before 1966 and supervision has less to add; if not, there is room for a training term (E069b).
known: E068c -- early CBC's species-wide decline was a circle-mix effect; within-list conditioning removes it (each
list is compared only with predictions for its own place and year). E068b -- early lists behave like under-sampling,
not notable-first reporting, so ONE set of species offsets for all eras is defensible (an era-specific offset would
also absorb real species-wide change). E066/E066c -- winter vs breeding agreement on change is 0.45 (all) / 0.52
(residents): the season link caps how much any winter list can say about breeding-season z.
design (research/exp/e069_presence_score.py; fixed now): DESK seed 1, T0 1995 (research cache desk_tempho_1995_s1;
BBS 1966-1995 WITHHELD from training). Readout: ridge z_ema -> log1p BBS abundance of the 83 CBC-matchable reference
species, on training cell-years from trained years (>= 1996), lambda by 5-fold CV over spatial blocks (BBS only).
Species offsets fitted on CBC lists of 2005-2024. CBC lists (>= 2 listed species) in the cache's 3,902 cells. For each
list: BACKCAST (z of its cell and year), MODERN placebo (the cell's mean z 2005-2024), REGIONAL placebo (modern + the
mean backcast shift over lists of the same stratum and year). Score: mean log P(species | record) per listed species;
paired backcast - placebo, 95% bootstrap over circles. Within-list AUC reported. Eras 1953-1965 (before BBS) and
1966-1995 (withheld BBS years) are primary; 1940-1952 is EMA burn-in and reported only. Species sets: all (primary),
residents (AVONET 1-2, secondary).
reading (per primary era, all species):
- BACKCAST CARRIES EARLY COMPOSITION if backcast - modern > 0 with the 95% interval excluding 0.
- ...LOCALLY if also backcast - regional > 0 with the interval excluding 0 (else: what it carries is regional).
- NO DETECTABLE early composition if backcast - modern has an interval covering 0 or is negative -> room for a
  presence term in training (E069b, GPU; yardstick: held-out circles' presences and modern readout generalization).
considerations: M17-corrected (beating a placebo shows predictability, not cause; regional change can be habitat --
the regional placebo separates local from regional, it does not label either as dynamics); M28 (one yardstick, paired,
one readout for every predictor); G11 (season; residents reported); M30 (place relative -- here by construction);
no House Finch (not among the reference species; asserted); sealed species never read.

## E069a2 Is the over-amplitude the READOUT or DESK's z? (pre-registered 2026-10-14 ~01:50, before running)
why: the peer session (E059e): a LEVEL readout of z (one amplitude, the production form) over-moves DESK's change on
out-of-community species (change slope k 0.26, corr 0.089); a SPLIT kernel -- separate amplitudes for the cell-mean z
and the within-cell deviation -- fits between >> within (~50x) and gives k ~0.87, corr 0.22. A level readout applies
BETWEEN-cell coefficients to a cell's change over time; if those are ~50x too large for temporal deviations, the
readout, not DESK's direction, makes the backcast too big. E069a used a level ridge.
design: e069_presence_score.py --readout split: ridge on [zbar_c, z - zbar_c] (zbar_c = the cell's mean z_ema over
trained years 1996-2025) with SEPARATE penalties chosen jointly by 5-fold CV over spatial blocks (grid 1e-3..100 each);
everything else as E069a (offsets refitted for this readout; same lists, placebos, bootstrap). The level readout is
re-run in the same job. SECONDARY (now pre-registered for both readouts): log-likelihood with the backcast shift scaled
by alpha in {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5}; the best alpha.
reading (primary eras 1953-65 and 1966-95, all species):
- THE READOUT CARRIES THE OVER-AMPLITUDE if, with the split readout, backcast - modern is >= 0 or its interval covers 0
  in BOTH eras, AND the best alpha is >= 0.5 in both.
- DESK's z CHANGE ITSELF IS TOO LARGE / NOT SUPPORTED if backcast - modern stays negative with the interval excluding 0
  AND the best alpha is <= 0.3, in both eras.
- Otherwise: report (partly readout, partly z).
Reported: the between / within coefficient-norm ratio of the split fit (cf. E059e's ~50x amplitude ratio).

## E069b A presence-only CBC term in DESK training (pre-registered 2026-10-14 ~03:00, before any run)
why: E069a/a2 -- DESK's backcast predicts early CBC lists WORSE than the modern community (lists support ~20-30% of its
change). The user (2026-10-13) proposed lightly supervising z with presences; the term (desk_training.py,
desk.aux_presence; tests/test_desk_training.py::test_presence_term_trains_logs_and_resumes) is the within-list
presence likelihood, -log softmax_s(z_ema[t, cell] @ W + b + delta), with W, b a FIXED level readout fitted on ESK z
(research/exp/build_cbc_presence.py) and delta learned, so the term can only be lowered by moving z.
data: 95,634 CBC lists 1940-2024 of the 59 RESIDENT reference species (sedentary focus; dev species never used),
cbc_presence_res.npz; the trainer drops held-out, buffer and train_frac-dropped cells and invalid cell-years. Lists in
the WITHHELD years 1966-1995 are KEPT (include_withheld): supervision from an independent source where BBS is withheld,
graded on held-out cells only.
arms (GPU, T0 1996 as E022): seed 1 and seed 2 at weight 0.01 (expected loss share ~15% at epoch 150, from E061's
loss scale), seed 1 at weight 0.04 (~40%, dose check). Controls: E022 desk_tempho_1995_s1 / _s2 (same seeds). Selected
and late (epoch 490) states, each against the control's same state.
evaluation:
- PRIMARY: HELD-OUT CBC lists (DESK held-out cells, never in the term; research/exp/e069b_heldout_lists.py; 383 lists /
  68 circles in 1953-65, ~2,130 / 142 in 1966-95): gain = arm (backcast - modern) - control (backcast - modern), per
  listed species, resident species, 95% bootstrap over circles. Seed noise measured beforehand (smoke, control seed 2
  vs seed 1, same script): backcast differences +0.013 to +0.028, gain differences +0.017 to +0.035.
- SECONDARY: BBS yardstick in the withheld years -- dev species, SEDENTARY stratum primary (species_strata contrasts,
  paired against E059h's same-seed control scorings), residents and all reported; community (ESK) target via
  fair_rivals vs the same-seed control.
rule:
- PRESENCE TERM IMPROVES EARLY COMPOSITION (held-out places) if, at weight 0.01, the LATE-state gain is > 0 with the
  interval excluding 0 in both primary eras for BOTH seeds, AND the mean gain over the two seeds is >= 0.035 (above
  the largest seed-to-seed control difference). Selected state reported.
- HELPS THE BBS TARGET if the sedentary dev change contrast (arm - control, late) is >= +0.02 with CI > 0 for both
  seeds; HURTS if <= -0.02 with CI < 0 for both; else no detectable effect. Community change beyond placebo and space
  level reported (space drop > 0.02 flagged as a cost).
- Dose (weight 0.04, one seed): reported against weight 0.01 seed 1.
- OFF-DESIGN if the presence share at epoch 150 is outside 5-60%.
considerations: M28 (one yardstick per claim; held-out lists AND the BBS yardstick); M17-corrected (gains vs a placebo
are predictability, not cause); G11 (residents; winter vs breeding change 0.52); E060/E061 (aux heads did not
regularize; the user: judge by readout generalization -- here, held-out places' lists and dev species); no House Finch;
sealed never read.
