# E079 Does winter-vs-breeding agreement depend on URBANIZATION (circle-vs-route habitat mismatch)?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-15, before running)
hypotheses: [season link (G11), CBC]     parents: [E066c, E077b revision (candidate 2)]     cost: ~15 min cpu
why: the user (2026-10-15) asked whether cell-level urbanization should change CBC-BBS agreement. CBC circles are often
centred on towns and water, BBS routes run on rural secondary roads; if winter-specific ecology near towns (feeding,
suburbs) drives the winter-breeding disagreement in change, the mismatch should be worst where a cell is mostly rural
but holds a town, not simply rising or falling with urban share.
data (research/exp/e079_urban_strata.py -> urban_strata.npz; built before this file): 972 cells holding both a CBC
circle (1966-2024) and BBS routes; HISDAC built-up intensity within-cell median (bui_q50) and 99th percentile
(bui_q99), and LUH2 urban fraction, means over 1990-2010. Strata: urbanization terciles by bui_q50 (345 / 303 / 324
cells); TOWN-IN-RURAL = bui_q99 above its median AND bui_q50 at or below its median (81 cells) vs the other 891; LUH2
urban terciles (324 each, reported).
design: E066c's T1 unchanged (e066_similarity_links.py t1, standardized CBC, ABBA, disattenuated), restricted to each
stratum's cells (--cell-mask); species SEDENTARY (AVONET 1; primary) and residents (1-2).
reading (sedentary, TEMPORAL disattenuated agreement; point values with their n -- T1 has no interval):
- MISMATCH SUPPORTED if town-in-rural is lower than the other cells by >= 0.15.
- URBAN LEVEL: a monotone rise from the lowest to the highest bui_q50 tercile by >= 0.15 = "more urban, more agreement";
  a monotone fall by >= 0.15 = the reverse; otherwise no clear urban-level effect.
- Values are read only where both replicate Spearmans are >= 0.3 (as E066).
caveats: 81 town-in-rural cells; cell-level urbanization is a proxy for what each survey unit samples (the circle's and
the routes' own surroundings are not measured here); placement of circles is not random.
considerations: G11, M30, sedentary primary, no House Finch, sealed never read.

READING (pre-registered, sedentary, temporal): TOWN-IN-RURAL mismatch NOT SUPPORTED (0.452 vs 0.515: -0.06 < 0.15;
77 cells). URBAN LEVEL: a monotone FALL from the least to the most built-up tercile by 0.16 (0.555 -> 0.394) --
"more urban, LESS agreement"; LUH2 urban fraction agrees (0.593 -> 0.407), residents more so (0.585 -> 0.327).
WHY (unknown; candidates). (1) Real winter-specific ecology of residents tied to urbanization -- feeding and suburban
growth since the 1960s changing winter abundance near towns more than breeding abundance (no migration needed); (2) BBS
routes in urbanized cells change in what they sample (disturbance, noise, discontinued and replaced routes); (3) region:
urban cells concentrate in the east, so the gradient is partly geographic. Not separable here.
NOTE for E077b: the urban gradient sits in the most built-up (largely eastern) cells, while E077b's sedentary harm was
concentrated where 1920-66 COVER changed most (largely Great Plains and west); this gradient does not explain that harm.
Finer resolution would not separate the surveys (E079 question): the disagreement grows with urbanization at the cell
scale, and the survey units themselves are cell-sized aggregates.
