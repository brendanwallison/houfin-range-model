   Decided on SEDENTARY COMMON (primary stratum); all species reported. If sedentary common and all species point to
   different D, the rule is ambiguous and is reported as such rather than resolved (P3).
1. **Native vs averaged**: the winning-D 54 km field vs the block-averaged 27 km field_d_interp (`fdinterp27_avg54_s1`)

The first dry run's floor compared the ABSOLUTE land weight with 1e-4. The plan's amendment (and the 181-pair count in
