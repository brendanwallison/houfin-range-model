# E026 Is the metric term's pair count (gradient noise) a lever on DESK's temporal extrapolation?
hypotheses: [T1, T11]     parents: [E019, E022, E023, E025]     eval_version: pre-v1     cost: 2 x ~60 min gpu + ~45 min cpu
pre-registered: 2026-10-09 ~22:50, before either model exists.

question: current-code retrains of tempho1995 backcast ~50-100% better than the archived tempho1995 model (reach_curve
1966-71 window: archived 0.12 vs seeds 0.20 / 0.18; span_reach raw at T0 1996: 0.057 vs 0.122 / 0.105). The recorded
training differences between the archived checkpoints (code ~0288236, 2026-08-21) and HEAD are (a) the metric term's
pairs per optimizer step, hardcoded at 4,096 then, 65,536 since a3633d2 (2026-08-25; 4x less gradient noise), and
(b) epoch selection on val_zmse (then) vs val_kernel (now). The runs' logs say (b) moves magnitude, not direction:
val_zmse would have picked epochs 65-95 with the same withheld-pair direction cosine (E025 notes). DESK takes ONE
optimizer step per epoch (spatial_tiles 1), so 500 steps in all, and the metric term carries all of DESK's cross-year
supervision. If its gradient noise limits what DESK learns about temporal structure, more pairs would extend
extrapolation, and this is a cheap knob.

design: two DESK runs at HEAD, tempho1995 setup (1966-1995 withheld; same basis, spatial split, selection), seed 1 (same
initialization as E022 t1995 s1), differing only in desk.metric_pairs: 4,096 (the archived value) and 262,144 (4x HEAD).
Compared with E022 t1995 s1 / s2 (65,536; seed spread 0.02-0.03) by span_reach (raw + recalibrated backcast of
1966-71, 1972-77, 1978-83, 1984-89 against 1996-2005) and reach_curve, at the selected epoch and at epoch 490 (E025's
late states), plus the training logs (dcos_withheld, rot_ratio_withheld, val_kernel) epoch by epoch.

predictions:
- T11 (gradient noise in the metric term limits temporal learning): mp4096 backcasts below BOTH 65k seeds by more than
  the seed spread at 1966-71 and 1972-77, landing near the archived model; its logged dcos_withheld plateaus lower.
  mp262k >= both 65k seeds, and above them by more than the spread in at least one of those two windows.
- null (pair count is not the lever): mp4096 and mp262k within the seed spread of the 65k seeds; the archived gap has
  another cause (then: the Aug 22-26 commits other than a3633d2, one at a time, cheapest first).
- spatial side effect: best val_kernel within the 6.6% floor of the 65k seeds for both (more pairs should not hurt).
decision rule: T11 holds (mp4096 down AND mp262k up) -> the temporal extrapolation of DESK is optimization-limited, not
only data-limited: next, the optimization budget along the same axis (more steps per epoch via spatial tiles, or more
pairs again) on two seeds, judged on the same backcast; a production config change would be an ESCALATION (pause for the
user). Only mp4096 down -> 65k is past the knee; the archived gap is explained; no further pairs. Neither -> null.

with seed 1. Pair count moves the backcast by at most ~0.01, while changing the seed moves it by 0.024. Training
outcome is set far more by the seed (initialization, augmentation) than by the metric term's pair sampling. This also
makes E026's null tighter than its original one-seed design suggested. Spatial skill: all within 0.01 at the
selected state; all lose the edge over linear at the late state.
