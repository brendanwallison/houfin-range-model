# E049 Does crop-type change act where it should: on grassland birds rather than forest birds? (pre-registered
# 2026-10-11 17:01, before running)
hypotheses: [T5, M26, M6]     parents: [E047b, E044d]     eval_version: v1     cost: ~20 min cpu
why: M26 says a real driver acts WHERE IT ACTS. Community-level tests (E044d, E047b) average over guilds, which
dilutes a guild-specific habitat effect. Ecological expectation: grassland and open-country birds respond to what is
grown (row crops replacing hay, small grains and pasture); forest birds should not respond to crop types. The
species level is also what the age model's habitat readout works at.
design: research/exp/guild_dose.py on desk_tempho_1995_s1 and desk_tempho_1985_s1. The covariate arms do not depend
on DESK.
- Species: 96 community + 273 dev species (never the sealed half), with AVONET (BirdLife) habitat: Grassland,
  Forest, Woodland, Shrubland, Wetland, Human Modified.
- Per species: log1p abundance change over the species' range cells, early withheld epoch vs 2005-25.
- Predictors, residualized within type; type+kloc also reported:
  - CropAT 11 (density + 10 types);
  - LUH crop 5;
  - LUH all 33;
  - LUH 33 + CropAT 11.
- Per-species ridge (block-CV lambda) fitted on training cells, graded on held-out cells.
- Guild-pooled noise-free corr over species with resolvable change; paired 6x6-block bootstrap.
predictions:
- P1: GRASSLAND guild, within type: LUH33 + CropAT - LUH33 > 0 with CI > 0 at both T0s.
- P2: FOREST guild: the same gain ~0.
- P3: the grassland gain exceeds the forest gain (difference CI > 0).
rule: "CROP TYPES ACT WHERE THEY SHOULD" if P1 and P3 hold at both T0s, within type. That would be the M26 signature
of a real habitat driver at the species level, and it would support CropAT as habitat information for the age
model's readout, not a clock. Otherwise report sizes. Kernel-local results are reported alongside without a rule.
caveats:
- Guild sizes are small (~38 grassland species, fewer with resolvable change and >= 30 held-out range cells).
- AVONET habitat is a coarse global class.
- The two T0s share most cells, so they are not independent.
considerations: M26, M6, corrected M17, G3/G4, sealed-species guard.
SMOKE (T0 1996, 30 draws; seen before the full run, disclosed):
- Only 12 grassland and 53 forest species pass the filters (resolvable change, >= 60 training and >= 30 held-out
  range cells).
- Within type, the joint per-species ridge (LUH33 + CropAT11) is WORSE than LUH33 alone in both guilds: grassland
  -0.081, forest -0.183. With 44 features fitted on a few hundred range cells per species, the estimator overfits.
  P1 fails at T0 1996.
- CropAT11 alone vs LUH crop5: grassland +0.105 [+0.041,+0.172], forest -0.091.
