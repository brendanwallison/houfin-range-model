# E062 Does RAW eBird agree with BBS -- on level and on change -- better than the S&T product did?
# (pre-registered 2026-10-13 ~08:40, before any statistic was computed)
hypotheses: [eBird-places, eBird-trend, T5]     parents: [E058, E060, E061, E057]     eval_version: pre-v1
cost: ~10-20 min cpu per grid variant
why: the user approved the cheap raw-data check (2026-10-13) after E060/E061: neither the eBird S&T head nor the CBC
head regularized DESK or added beyond-trend information, and E058 found the S&T TREND agrees with BBS cell change only
weakly (centred corr 0.10; species level 0.59). Raw eBird is a different proposition: real checklists at ~14,000 cells
(BBS: 3,902), aggregated by us, with no smoothing across held-out areas. Before any GPU time on a raw-eBird design
(including the user's "eBird z in the BBS basis"), measure whether its signal is cleaner than the product's.
data: the EBD release Aug-2026, US + Canada, reduced by src/data/preprocess/ebd_reduce.py (complete checklists, May 25
- Jul 15, 2002-2025, <= 5 h, <= 5 km, <= 10 observers, study box; 8.13 M checklists; 96 reference + 273 dev species,
sealed species and House Finch refused at read time), gridded by research/exp/build_ebird_raw_grid.py to 27 km
cell-years (168,111 cell-years over 13,955 cells). Variant "short" (<= 60 min and <= 2 km; 5.36 M checklists) checks
effort: eBird grew ~25x from 2008 to 2024, and its checklist mix (hotspots, urban, short counts) changed with it.
design: research/exp/ebird_raw_agree.py on the desk_tempho_1995_s1 cache (keys, counts, route-years; no DESK output
read). The BBS side is E058's exactly (2010-2014 vs 2019-2024, cross ABBA halves, observer classes, 300 km regional
field). eBird side: smoothed log reporting rate log((det + 0.5) / (checklists + 1)) per cell, window and year-parity
half; cells need >= 10 eBird checklists in every window-half. Statistics pooled over species, centred and uncentred,
6x6-block bootstrap:
- E_att: eBird change vs BBS change, BBS noise removed only (comparable with E058's 0.103);
- E_dis: both surveys' noise removed (eBird's parity halves) -- separates "eBird is noisy" from "the surveys disagree";
- R (BBS regional field), eBird beyond R, moved-eBird null (8 moves), observer strata, prevalence tiers, species level;
- LEVEL in the late window: eBird log reporting rate vs BBS log1p mean count, attenuated and disattenuated.
considerations: M2/M3 (cross halves; observers not removed -- strata reported), M4, M7 (27 km; ~9.5-year gap), M13 (CI
bounds), M14 (tiers), M19 (spatially structured null), M17-corrected (agreement with a trend says nothing about cause),
M29 (judge a source by what its curators trust: the EBD guidance is complete checklists with effort limits, as here;
eBird's own trend products use 2007+ data with effort modelling, which this simple index does not do).
predictions (written before any statistic):
- LEVEL agrees well: disattenuated centred level corr >= 0.5 (both surveys see where species are common).
- CHANGE: E_att centred 0.05-0.20 -- raw cell indices are noisier than the smoothed product, but direct; E_dis well above
  E_att (eBird's own noise is large at the cell level), yet still below the BBS regional field R.
- eBird beyond R small (<= 0.10); E_att above the moved-null max; higher where BBS observers stayed (as E058).
- "short" changes little (<= 0.03 in E_att).
decision rule (centred, all cells, "all" grid; the same thresholds as E058, so the two products are judged alike):
- USABLE FOR TIME: E_att >= +0.20 with CI > 0, above the moved-null max, and eBird beyond R >= +0.05 with CI > 0 ->
  raw eBird carries place-level decadal change consistent with BBS beyond the neighbours' trend. Next: the user's design
  (eBird communities projected into the BBS ESK basis as extra targets at non-BBS cells), pre-registered for the GPU.
- REGIONAL ONLY: E_att >= +0.10 with CI > 0 but beyond-R CI includes 0 -> regional context only.
- NOT USABLE FOR TIME: E_att < +0.10, or CI including 0, or <= the moved-null max -> raw eBird's value would be spatial
  (more places), which E057 says buys space but not time, and which E060's smoothed-target head did not deliver.
- Anything else: report sizes. E_dis decides the WHY (noise vs disagreement); LEVEL decides whether a spatial use
  (raw eBird communities as targets at non-BBS places) is even consistent with BBS.

Predictions: LEVEL >= 0.5 YES (0.59); E_att 0.05-0.20 NO (0.038, below); E_dis well above E_att NO (0.044 vs 0.038);
beyond R <= 0.10 YES; above the moved null YES; higher where observers stayed NO (reversed); short within 0.03 YES.
DECISION (pre-registered, same thresholds as E058): E_att +0.038 < +0.10 -> NOT USABLE FOR TIME. Raw eBird's value for
DESK, if any, is spatial: its LEVEL agrees with BBS (0.59; 0.76 for common species).
WHY:
- established for this design: the weak change agreement is NOT eBird's sampling noise -- removing it changes nothing
  (E_dis 0.044 vs E_att 0.038). The two surveys' decadal cell changes genuinely disagree, beyond a small specific part
  (+0.037 beyond the BBS trend, above every moved null).
- likely: this simple index has no effort model, and eBird's checklist mix changed with its ~25x growth; the species-
  level change correlation is NEGATIVE (-0.19, vs +0.59 for the effort-modelled S&T trend), the signature of a
  species-wide shift in reporting rates that is effort/composition, not birds. Restricting to short checklists did not
  remove it (-0.16). An effort-modelled index (checklist-level covariates: duration, distance, time of day, observer
  skill, as S&T does) is what raw eBird would need before any temporal use (M29).
- BBS cell change is itself largely route-specific (E014), so cell-level agreement has a low ceiling for any source.
IMPLICATIONS (not decisions): raw eBird, aggregated simply, carries no usable decadal change signal at 27 km; its
spatial content is consistent with BBS. The user's "eBird communities in the BBS basis" design would be a SPATIAL
regularizer at non-BBS places -- consistent with BBS on level, but E057 says places buy space, not time, and E060's
smoothed eBird head bought neither. A temporal use would first need an effort-modelled eBird index.
