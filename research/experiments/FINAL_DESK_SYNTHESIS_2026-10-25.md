# DESK finalization synthesis

Updated after user corrections. This is an assessment of existing experiments and a prospective decision plan, not a production adoption. The preferred endpoint is one space-for-time readout with credible multi-decadal geometry in Z, not a split readout by default. All additional expressive capacity is learned at the community level and frozen into Z before House Finch inference; species-specific temporal residuals or focal-data tuning of the metric are outside this plan. See COMMUNITY_Z_EXPERIMENT_MAP_2026-10-26.md for the audited prior work and corrections to proposals already tested in E073/E086e.

DESK is a useful compact community representation. Evidence does not establish equally reliable historical and modern habitat inference, or an all-season winner. Clean full-width v2 improves the well-calibrated summer proxy; clean v1 remains better on early winter recorded composition. House Finch inference is not settled because comparable refinements are invalid or incomplete.

## Capacity and calibration

Three splits must not be conflated:

1. Seasonal blocks: v2 contains64 summer and64 winter coordinates. Winter coordinates are not intrinsically temporal coordinates.
2. Spatial/temporal readout: cell means and within-cell deviations have different coefficient structure. The current House Finch fits retain80h: temporal amplitude fixed at0.14 times the spatial amplitude, direction correlated with the spatial direction through a Beta(8,2) prior. This is an inherited experimental setting, NOT a calibration of any current clean encoder or its128-feature kernel. Its presence cannot validate the current temporal prior.
3. Leading/tail rank blocks: E086i tested24+40 with fitted block amplitudes. It tracked rank64 and failed to protect the sparse-row regime. Merely adding a fitted tail amplitude is not an established solution.

Full width can still be too flexible for a particular target's temporal record: it permits more directions even at fixed amplitude. However, its128 coordinates also add seasonal information, and its sum kernel changes covariance geometry. Neither128 versus64 nor32+32 versus64 isolates temporal capacity. The32+32 cut discards directions from the spatial representation as well. Do not diagnose temporal overfitting from width or optimizer failure alone.

E059e supports separating spatial and temporal readout scales. E033 shows that apparently improved change predictions can instead learn species-specific regional trends, so retain an adequate trend placebo. E086t's target-specific modern temporal calibration did not reliably help common residents and harmed some backcasts. E080 in the downstream agent's experiment tree found static habitat forecast the temporal holdout about as well in its tested cases, but several fits hit caps or stalled; that is motivation for a matched constrained ladder, not a settled static-habitat adoption.

## Finalization strategy

Preserve clean v1 and full-width v2, and add v2 winter64 and the matched summer64 control. Winter-only is a substantive candidate for a year-round resident and a1902 backcast, not merely a truncation test. Its biological rationale does not guarantee predictive superiority. Preserve the128-coordinate export and explicit block contract. Avoid a broad encoder architecture search on repeatedly opened holdouts. Do not promote the contaminated four-seed ensemble because it has more seeds. Complete clean seeds3/4 for finalists only after the downstream/readout decision is fixed enough to justify that production comparison.

First recover diagnostic comparability: clean MAP heldout density, ecological maps, release counterfactual, and the exact stalled-point gradient audit. Distinguish MAP, invalid retained modes and valid refinements in every table. Change optimizer coordinates if evidence supports it; preserve the density and convergence gates. Do not use raw posterior potential across widths to select an encoder.

First test whether the richer representation permits simplification: use one shared space-for-time coefficient field, with full128, winter64 and summer64 geometry, rather than automatically retaining a split. Keep demography, observation model and initialization controlled. Cross the main representation comparison with a mechanistically justified absolute-density Allee implementation; do not attribute numerical failure to the readout or adopt relative Allee merely because it optimizes more easily. The existing aggregated absolute-density mate-encounter curve is smooth, but numerical thresholds and extreme multi-year establishment sensitivity remain separate potential causes. Local derivative/step-size audits should distinguish them before changing biology. Existing gradient checks at one point do not rule out near-extinction failures elsewhere.

If the shared readout needs calibration, prefer an explicit positive-semidefinite metric in Z: z'=A z, k(z,z')=z^T M z', M=A^T A. This retains one coefficient field and makes changed similarity assumptions reviewable. Candidate metrics include seasonal-block weights and direction-dependent shrinkage guided by multi-decadal signal reliability or temporal/between-place generalized covariance. Normalize spatial prior energy and fit hyperparameters on nonfocal development species/training locations with blocked decades; measure magnitude and direction, not just correlation. Do not optimize them on historical heldout or focal launch outcomes. Covariance ratios alone measure motion, not ecological truth. A global metric cannot separate temporal and spatial effects if they use the same directions; any resulting spatial tradeoff must be reported.

Anchoring zbar+gamma(z-zbar) is a possible explicit nonstationary kernel, but it is algebraically a tied temporal rescaling, not a conceptual escape from a split. Treat it as a fallback sensitivity, together with static and the old80h split, rather than claiming a newly principled default. Reduced temporal bases are also fallback interventions and require prior definition/validation. The0.14 setting is only a historical reference. Include an adequate covariate/trend baseline. Full128 may permit simpler biology/readouts previously rejected under smaller representations; test those simplifications rather than assuming old conclusions transfer.

Select on observation-aware predictive scores, paired uncertainty and temporal calibration, with spatial performance and ecological diagnostics alongside them. Require credible early and late behavior, and no unsupported historical bridge or extreme vital-rate compensation. Numerical validity is necessary but not sufficient. Thresholds for predictive equivalence and acceptable ecological changes must be fixed for this ladder before running; older experiments' thresholds are not automatically portable. Because current holdouts are reused, label the decision exploratory unless a genuinely untouched evaluation is available.

Use explicit two-hour gpu-a100-dev jobs, stage markers, immutable sources and resumable optimizer state. Inspect every terminal status before retrying: caps with progress can resume; stalls need diagnosis. Local RTX3090 handles verified64-feature refinement; full128 exceeded its comfortable headroom in measured TACC runs and stays on A100 until a validated memory reduction exists. Submit useful bounded stages rather than rely on idle long-lived workers.
