question: the corrected suite's oracle (true community features at the scored window, read through the species
readout) is at or below no change on species change (E006: -0.002 / -0.014 / -0.029). Squared-error skill against no
change factors exactly: skill = (var p / var t)(2k - 1), k = cov(p, t)/var(p) the truth-on-prediction slope. A
prediction beats no change only if k > 1/2, and the best any rescaling of it can do is corr^2. So "no better than no
change" can come from (1) little information (corr^2 small), (2) bad scaling (k < 1/2: predicted changes too large
for their accuracy), (3) survey noise in the oracle's own features amplified by the betas (errors-in-variables).
design: research/exp/readout_why.py on the same cells, species and level betas as E013, noise-free cross-half
estimates, pooled over resolvable species, centred (place-specific) and uncentred (as the suite scores, species' mean
change included), by prevalence tier; arms: the oracle (true community), DESK, and a smooth spatial field fitted to
the training cells' change (how regional species change is). Caches: tempho1995 s1 withheld; its epoch-490 state;
base trained.
predictions (rivals): information-limited -> oracle corr^2 < 0.1 and its best rescaled skill small; scaling-limited
-> k well below 1/2 while corr^2 is moderate; noise-limited -> the oracle's as-emitted k far below its noise-free k.

READING (UNVERIFIED): the oracle loses to no change for three multiplying reasons, in order of size:
1. INFORMATION: the true community's change carries ~5% (centred) to 7% (uncentred) of a species' noise-free change.
   Species change is strongly REGIONAL (a smooth 300-km field of the species' own change explains 23%), but the
   community's change at the same place carries a fifth of that. Most of a species' decadal change is species-specific
   regional dynamics the rest of the community does not share. (E011: even a change-fitted readout gets only
   +0.02-0.05 from community change beyond location -- so it is not only the spatial betas.)
2. SCALING: the level betas (fitted on spatial differences, which are large and strongly tied to species abundance)
   turn community change into species change about twice as large as its accuracy supports: k ~0.5, exactly the
   break-even, so even a noise-free oracle scores ~0 (-0.016 / +0.014).
3. NOISE: the oracle's own half-window features carry survey noise that the large betas amplify (prediction variance
   x1.26), dropping k to 0.37-0.43 and the score to -0.04 / -0.08.
Rare species (prevalence < 0.1) carry almost no information (corr < 0.1), so their readout is mostly amplified noise
and they pull the median down; common species (>= 0.3) are slightly positive noise-free. DESK in withheld decades has
almost no information (corr 0.07) and k 0.19; in trained years it reaches corr 0.22, k 0.70 and beats no change.
